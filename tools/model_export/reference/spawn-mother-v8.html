Against the spec

  • Size: about 7 blocks to the crest and 7 long. Legs span about 6 blocks wide, and the open claws about 8.
  • Chassis: a low sternum with six legs in three pairs, each with a basal segment, a thick upper leg, a raised knee and a long lower leg ending in a spike. The front pair is thick, the middle pair widest, the rear pair angled back. There's open space under the body.
  • Thorax: raised high on a narrower waist, with armored shoulders and overlapping side plates. The front of the chest is an open cavity holding the great glowing sac cluster. A dorsal ridge runs from the shoulders down to the abdomen.
  • Claws: shoulder, ridged upper arm, angular elbow, long forearm, oversized claw housing, then a fixed and a moving pincer with ivory gripping edges that curve inward. The right claw is bulkier.
  • Head: a long, narrow skull pointing down, with a pale bone carapace and a beaked upper face. It's asymmetric, with amber sensory pits in dark recesses. The vertical mouth opens sideways and a thick tongue hangs out. Eight facial tentacles: two very long lateral whips, three short ones under the mouth, three reaching forward. The frill has a central crest and two lateral fans of red membrane between bone-yellow ridges.
  • Abdomen: plated on top and softer and banded underneath. It's widest in the middle and narrows to the rear. Sac clusters sit in recessed cavities on the flanks. There are petal vents on both lower flanks and under the tip, and trailing tendrils at the end.
  • Fungal growth: trumpet vents on the shoulders and back, and shelf fungi only along seams and cavity edges.

The pool and stalks are separate arena dressing, as the spec asks. Angles and the gait are rough.
