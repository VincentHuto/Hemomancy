# Configuration

Hemomancy exposes active **server**, **client**, and reserved **common** config specs through `HemoConfig`. In practice, the active tuning surface is split between `HemoServerConfig` for gameplay balance and `HemoClientConfig` for presentation and rendering choices.

On the server side, pack authors can tune the major blood-magic systems directly. Config groups cover **Blood Volume** regeneration, drain, and kill gain; **Blood Tendency** shifts from combat and manipulation use; **Vascular System** strain, healing, and debuff behavior; **Bloodline** pool sharing, healing, and fane stake budget; and **Morphling** passive blood drain timing and cost. These options make it possible to speed up or slow down progression, change attrition pressure, and reshape multiplayer covenant balance without editing code.

On the client side, the config currently includes HUD and render-layer options, such as blood volume HUD placement and toggles for equipped visual layers like blood gourds, horns, and morphling mutation rendering. World-rendering toggles include Founding Fane boundaries, Harbinger cardinal rite perimeter fog (`renderCardinalRiteFog`), blood orbs, and Oculiflora network sight. Cardinal rite fog also exposes `cardinalRiteFogVerticalOffset`, a block offset that raises or lowers the diffuse smoke ring. That gives modpack creators and players a lightweight way to preserve the mod's systems while adjusting readability and visual intensity.
