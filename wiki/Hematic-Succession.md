# Hematic Succession

At Degree 5, a member of a bloodline with an established fane can ask an original Alchemist, Mnemonist, Artificer, Cicatrix Anchorite, or Vicar for a **Mnemonic Bequest**. This consent is separate from ordinary recruitment: several professions at one outpost can consent, and each bloodline earns its own consent. Originals remain at their outposts.

## Prepare the body and blood

Hold a Living Syringe with an empty vial in its rack when choosing the donor's donation dialogue. Donations have no cooldown or extra fee. Sneak-use another empty vial to sample your own blood for 100 mL. Older samples without individual identity cannot start either rite.

Manufacture a **Vacant Effigy** using Sanguine Formation, 250 mL blood, and this horizontal D5 blood structure. Target its Venous Stone center:

```
Conscious Mass | Hematic Iron | Conscious Mass
Hematic Iron   | Venous Stone | Hematic Iron
Conscious Mass | Hematic Iron | Conscious Mass
```

Place one free workplace inside the leader's registered fane:

| Profession | Workplace |
| --- | --- |
| Alchemist | Ghastly Alembic or Vial Centrifuge |
| Mnemonist | Somatic Loom or Mnemonic Reliquary |
| Artificer | Hematic Armature |
| Cicatrix Anchorite | Mason's Effigy or Fungal Implantation Pylon |
| Vicar | Cardinal Focus |

One physical workplace supports one successor. Players may still use claimed machines.

## Perform succession

Use the **Greater Dominion** floor. Place a Venous Stone plinth two blocks horizontally from the Cardinal Focus and one block above the floor; place the effigy on top. The plinth determines the rite's orientation. Seat Sanguine Quintessence in the Focus.

Light the first three marked Iron Brazier sockets: **1 donor blood**, **2 your own blood**, **3 Mnemonic Ambergris**. For an east-side plinth the socket offsets from the Focus are `(5,1,0)`, `(4,1,-2)`, `(3,1,-4)`; rotate the assembly together. Nearby braziers show their roles while the body is placed. JEI lists both rites and the effigy manufacturing recipe.

Plant a Living Staff using the normal Cardinal Rite interaction. After planting, remain in the circle for the 60-second sequence: Form, Identity, Life, Memory, Recognition, Resolution. Normal cancellation controls apply. There are no combat waves. Offerings are committed as the phases advance; cancellation retains already-spent costs and leaves unconsumed offerings.

Valid consent awakens a new resident with their own name, appearance, temperament, ancestry, and encounter history. Identity-bearing counterfeit or wrong-owner blood fails late and awakens a hostile Misbegotten. The hostile uses a malformed attack from its intended profession and tracks the responsible officiant.

## Residents and recovery

Open **Residents** in the Bloodline Pool interface. Living successors provide their profession's teaching, assignments, turn-ins, and services under each player's existing progression gates. They add 1,000 mL pool capacity and use the existing 1,000 mL helper reserve. Repeated residents cannot repeat player-owned one-time rewards.

Activating a rite with a helper requirement automatically teleports eligible successors from their registered fane workplaces into distinct rite stations. It summons only the missing number of helpers. Bloodspent, displaced, unaligned, and already-assigned residents are excluded. During preparation, bloodline players can take stations and release the replaced NPCs. Players must remain near their station to count. If too few eligible helpers exist, the rite still requires the remaining player or NPC helpers before sealing.

When the rite finishes, collapses, is cancelled, or loses its caster beyond the disconnect grace period, surviving NPC helpers teleport back beside their own workplaces, including across dimensions. Their return trip persists through entity reload. A blocked return location is retried until safe footing is available beside the station. Rites with no helper requirement do not automatically summon residents.

Destroyed or excluded workplaces displace the resident and suspend services and rite assistance. Residents search loaded fane areas for replacements. Chunk unloading does not count as death or create another NPC.

Death releases the workplace and drops a **Bound Mnemonic Remnant**. To restore the same person, rebuild the effigy and use Quintessence in the Focus, **1 remnant**, and **2 your own blood**. Leave socket 3 empty. Restoration needs a free workplace but no donor blood or Ambergris. UUID, appearance, history, and spent helper reserve persist.

Failed restoration preserves the identity and returns a consumed remnant. If a remnant is lost, stand within eight blocks of the leader's registered fane bloodwell and use **Recover remnant**. Recovery is free and invalidates all earlier copies. Living or currently reserved identities cannot be recovered or restored.

Only the leader can permanently dismiss a successor. Dismissed NPCs remain alive and unaligned but lose claims, benefits, and restoration through the former bloodline. Dissolving a bloodline leaves its successors dormant. Adoption and personal quests are deferred.
