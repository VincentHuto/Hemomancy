## What is Hemomancy?

Hemomancy is a comprehensive blood magic and fungal horror mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge). Unlike typical magic mods that focus on accumulating more power, Hemomancy emphasizes the **quality** of blood manipulation over quantity. The mod presents a morally gray world where blood magic is neither inherently good nor evil — it is a tool, a heritage, and a burden.

### Two Paths, One Choice

Players face a fundamental choice:

- **The Harbinger Path** — Embrace blood magic as a sacred inheritance. Join the Hematic Order, ascend through seven degrees of initiation, and unlock powerful blood manipulations. But know that each step brings you closer to something ancient and incomprehensible.

- **The Unstained Path** — Reject blood magic as infection. Purify yourself through brutal rituals using hemolytic solution and copper, guided by Our Lady of Still Waters. Become biologically immortal, but at a cost few understand.

**These paths are mutually exclusive.** Choose wisely.

## Key Features

### Blood Manipulation System
Manipulations are organized into **eight tendencies** (blood qualities), each with unique powers:
- **Animus** (Life) — Healing, regeneration, vitality
- **Flammeus** (Fire) — Combustion and heat
- **Ductilis** (Metal) — Iron control and weaponry
- **Lux** (Light) — Radiance and purification
- **Mortem** (Death) — Decay and withering
- **Congeatio** (Ice) — Freezing and preservation
- **Ferric** (Raw Iron) — Magnetic forces
- **Tenebris** (Shadow) — Darkness and concealment

### Progressive Degrees
Harbingers advance through **seven degrees** of the Hematic Order, each unlocking new powers and deeper secrets:
1. **Pledged** — The first step, marked by the Sanguine Initiation
2. **Illuminatus** — Understanding the blood's inner light
3. **Adeptus** — Mastery of manipulation
4. **Sentinel** — Guardian of the Order
5. **Apostle** — Spreading the cause
6. **Archon** — Leadership and command
7. **Apotheos** — Transcendence... or dissolution

### Living World
- **Fungal Biomes** — Explore the eerie Sporecrown Thicket, Hyphal Spires, and Fungal Gardens
- **Dynamic Structures** — Discover Harbinger Outposts, Mausoleums, and hidden trial chambers
- **Blood Moons** — Witness the Pale Lady's costly immune response in the sky
- **Unique Mobs** — Face Sanguine Thralls, Morphlings, Blood Constructs, and worse

### Deep Lore
Hemomancy features a rich narrative that challenges typical fantasy tropes:
- The Fungal Entity is an incomprehensible 4D organism, not a simple demon
- Harbingers are a found family, not villains
- The Unstained's methods are brutal, not heroic
- Common folk view blood magic like vultures — unsettling but not evil
- Every choice has consequences

## Important Notes

### Moral Ambiguity
This mod intentionally avoids "good vs evil" framing. Neither path is objectively correct. Both have costs. The Harbingers are taboo but not wicked; the Unstained are pure but not righteous. Approach the mod with this nuance in mind.

### Opt-In Gameplay
**Hemomancy is completely opt-in.** The mod's mechanics only activate when you choose to interact with the Mortal Display. While fungal biomes and structures exist in the world, natural infection is not a player mechanic unless you deliberately embrace it.

### Performance & Compatibility
- Requires **NeoForge 21.1.x** (not legacy Forge)
- Minecraft version **1.21.1**
- Java **21** required
- Optional integrations with Mana and Artifice, Curios, and JEI
- Depends on **HutosLib** (required library mod)
