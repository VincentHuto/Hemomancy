The whole point is that blood quality matters more than simply stockpiling volume. Hemomancy is a blood magic mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) about what people do with power drawn from their own bodies.

> *"Channel the carnal fluid flowing through your veins, don't let it go to waste. Use it to crush your enemies, or boost yourself! After all, it's just sitting there!"*
>
> Huto

## What is Hemomancy?

Blood manipulation depends on **quality**, not simply quantity. The story treats blood magic as a tool, an inheritance, and a burden rather than something inherently good or evil.

### Two Paths, One Choice

Players choose between two mutually exclusive paths:

- **The Harbinger Path**: Treat blood magic as a sacred inheritance. Join the Hematic Order, ascend through seven degrees of initiation, and unlock blood manipulations. Each degree also puts you closer to something ancient and incomprehensible.

- **The Unstained Path**: Treat blood magic as infection. Purify yourself with hemolytic solution and copper under the guidance of Our Lady of Still Waters. The result is biological immortality, with a cost that becomes clearer as you progress.

**These paths are mutually exclusive.** Starting one closes the other.

## Key Features

### Blood Manipulation System
Manipulations are organized into **eight tendencies** (blood qualities). Each tendency has its own set of powers:
- **Animus** (Life): Healing, regeneration, vitality
- **Flammeus** (Fire): Combustion and heat
- **Ductilis** (Lightning/Nerves): speed, nervous energy, and electrical impulse
- **Lux** (Light): Radiance and purification
- **Mortem** (Death): Decay and withering
- **Congeatio** (Ice): Freezing and preservation
- **Ferric** (Iron): barbs, solidity, permanence, and magnetic force
- **Tenebris** (Shadow): Darkness and concealment

### Progressive Degrees
Harbingers advance through **eight degrees** of the Hematic Order:
1. **Neophyte**
2. **Votary**
3. **Initiate**
4. **Adept**
5. **Illuminatus**
6. **Sanctified**
7. **Archon**
8. **Apotheos**: reached only through the post-projection Rite of Apotheosis

### Living World
- **Fungal Biomes**: Find the Sporecrown Thicket, Hyphal Spires, and Fungal Gardens.
- **Dynamic Structures**: Search Harbinger Outposts, Mausoleums, and hidden trial chambers.
- **Blood Moons**: Survive a fungal surge while the Pale Lady mounts a costly containment response.
- **Unique Mobs**: Fight Sanguine Thralls, Morphlings, Blood Constructs, and worse.

### Story and lore
The story avoids a simple good-versus-evil split:
- The Fungal Entity is an incomprehensible 4D organism, not a simple demon
- Harbingers are a found family, not villains
- The Unstained's methods are brutal, not heroic
- Common folk view blood magic like vultures: unsettling but not evil
- Every choice has consequences

## Important Notes

### Moral Ambiguity
Do not read this as a good-versus-evil system. Neither path is objectively correct. Both have costs. Harbingers are taboo but not automatically wicked, and the Unstained are pure but not automatically righteous.

### Opt-In Gameplay
**Hemomancy is completely opt-in.** The mechanics start when you choose to interact with the Mortal Display. Fungal biomes and structures can exist without forcing infection onto the player.

### Performance & Compatibility
- Requires **NeoForge 21.1.x** (not legacy Forge)
- Minecraft version **1.21.1**
- Java **21** required
- JEI support is alpha-ready through the current local NeoForge JEI jar
- Mana and Artifice and Curios integrations are dormant on the NeoForge 1.21.1 branch until compatible dependencies are available
- Depends on **HutosLib** (required library mod)
