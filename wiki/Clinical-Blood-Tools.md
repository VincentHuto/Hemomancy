# Clinical Blood Tools

The Living Syringe is required for thick-skinned creatures, selected powerful mobs, villagers, and all Hemomancy NPCs. A loose vial displays a requirement message and collects nothing from these sources. Hold and use the syringe with an empty vial in its rack; simply carrying it does not count. Ordinary unrestricted creatures still support loose-vial collection. See [entity blood profiles](Blood-Profiles.md) for the datapack settings.

The Alchemist teaches blood as specimen: collection, observation, injection, and stationary storage. The Artificer teaches field construction. The Mnemonist teaches retained expression beyond physiology.

The Ghastly Alembic, broader tendency work, and Body Answers remain Degree 2. First Separation is available from Degree 1 and still satisfies its existing chapter certification; the clinical side assignments add no new rank gate.

## Observation and injection

Each successfully examined entity ID counts once per player. Repeated vials from the same creature type do not advance the three-source requirement. A traded identified vial preserves its identification, but you must personally examine it for two seconds to record that source. Interrupted examination, unknown entity IDs, and ordinary item pickup grant no examination proof. Examination consumes no blood and does not alter potency.

After learning Borrowed Physiology, hold Use with a filled vial for 0.8 seconds. The injection leaves its empty vessel and causes 20 seconds of Transfusion Saturation. Both identified and unidentified usable blood can be injected once the player knows the technique. Read the identified tooltip for temporary benefits and drawbacks.

## Storage and travel

The Cabinet has nine cells of 64 full vials each. All components must match within a cell, including source, identification, and custom name. Click a held vial into a cell and use an empty cursor to withdraw one; shift-click moves between the container and inventory. Fit a clear glass pane for world inspection, or sneak-use a Knapper to remove the pane. Empty the Cabinet before dismantling it.

Crafting proof records your actual Cabinet craft. Storage proof records successful manual Cabinet-menu transfers; empty withdrawals, rejected deposits, hoppers, and Field Case transfers do not count. The evidence persists even after the stored vial is removed.

The Field Case recipe consumes an empty Cabinet, six leather, and two Hematic Iron Scraps. It holds nine identities of sixteen matching vials each. Place it before opening. Close every viewer's menu, wait for its lid to shut, then sneak-use an empty hand to recover it with its contents.

## Mnemonic recording

The Alchemist refers the unexplained echo to the Mnemonist. After formal D3 teaching, place a filled creature vial and a blank Ambergris Cylinder in the Clairaudiograph, choose a call, and Carve for four seconds. The blood is consumed and the empty vial remains. Recording itself still accepts unidentified creature blood. Carved cylinders play without further blood and cannot be used as blank stock.

## Knowledge and existing saves

Lessons award recipe-book knowledge, Liber pages, and advancement toasts. Clinical recipes are hidden in JEI and the Materials atlas until taught. Vanilla manual crafting and shared apparatus remain usable; the knowledge gates control discovery, while injection itself requires its lesson. The assignment ledger and NPC quest tabs show the next actions.

Progress is stored in a persistent player attachment and survives death and relogging. Existing item data and old First Separation reward flags remain valid. Older characters can take the lessons at any later degree. Show an existing filled vial, personally reexamine stored identified samples, and demonstrate Cabinet practice to establish the new evidence; owning tools alone does not fabricate prior study.
