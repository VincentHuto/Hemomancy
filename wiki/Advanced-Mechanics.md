# Advanced Mechanics

Hemomancy's deeper systems branch out well beyond basic blood casting. **Morphlings** are symbiotic fungal organisms introduced through wild Morphling Polyps, the Alchemist's Living Bestiary, and later full cultivation in the **Morphling Incubator**. Captured wild polyps can be surrendered for matching wild-bound morphlings, which are usable early but capped until incubated. Fully cultivated morphlings are equipped through the **Morphling Jar** and cycled through the **Living Staff**. Different morphling species provide different passive and reactive powers, and late-game **Primal** morphlings add capstone transformations and client-side mutation rendering.

**Puppeteering** gives Harbingers a distinct summon-control lane. A fresh **Marionette Crossbar** permanently attunes to the first player who right-clicks with it. The **Puppeteer's Spindle** converts each physical Puppeteering Thread into 8 charge, stores up to 512 charge, fuels the Crossbar, and immediately prepares any learned shape clicked in its list; there are no separate Attune or Prepare buttons, and the Spindle never calls a body. An attuned Crossbar holds 256 base charge plus 32 per Bound Command level. Tap air-use to call or recall; hold for 10 ticks to open the command radial. Its inner ring applies **Follow**, **Guard**, **Hunt**, or **Passive** to all bodies attached to that exact Crossbar. Follow stays near the puppeteer and attacks hostiles within 12 blocks; Hunt searches the full tether range, while attacking a hostile temporarily focuses every attached body. Before Degree 5, shapes can only be changed at the Spindle. The one-rank Degree 5 skill **Skein Transposition** adds the outer shape ring and atomically hot-swaps the formerly prepared cohort for one selected replacement at its normal adjusted call cost; other shapes and Commandeered Wills remain. Each body pays its own upkeep, loses hostile focus outside tether range, fades after the skill-scaled grace period if the matching Crossbar is unequipped, and unravels if starved, taken across dimensions, left by a logging-out owner, or carried through owner death. Owner-bound bodies persist through ordinary hostile despawn and Peaceful difficulty so those tether rules remain authoritative. A session-bound tether applies severance even to unloaded bodies when they return, and deterministic cap reconciliation prevents unloaded-body stockpiling. Silent Archon's bonus slots are reserved for claimed Wills and do not replace the ordinary shaped-body slots. Veinwing Vulture, Marrow Spitter, and Gorebound Hulk trials use Sanguine Quintessence; the Mnemonist Puppet trial uses Mnemonic Ambergris.

> **Post-alpha WIP:** Drudges are retained for development but are not part of the first-release progression promise. Their Electrode, Submission Device, and Semi-Sentient Construct are available through the WIP creative tab for testing.

The **Drudge** system adds a persistent semi-organic servant anchored to a **Semi-Sentient Construct**. A Drudge can hold one Blood Memory and autonomously execute supported manipulations, refill from its home construct, and even act as a direct-routing tender for nearby covenant machinery. It fills the niche between combat summon, worker, and long-term bloodline utility unit.

At the ritual layer, **Cardinal Rites** remain the core progression backbone. These multiblock rites handle Harbinger degree advancement, utility rituals, the **Bloom of the Qliphoth**, and other major unlocks. They are explicitly degree-gated, blood-costed, boundary-checked, and tied into the mod's major progression thresholds rather than existing as optional flavor.
