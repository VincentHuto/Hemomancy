# The Unstained Path

*"The blood remembers. But what if we could make it forget?"*

The Unstained Path is the way of purification — viewing blood magic not as sacred inheritance but as **infection** to be cleansed. Guided by Our Lady of Still Waters, the Unstained pursue biological immortality through brutal antiseptic rituals, becoming something preserved, pure, but perhaps no longer wholly human.

---

## Overview

**Philosophy:** Blood magic is corruption. The Fungal Entity is infection. Purity can be achieved through pain.

**Goal:** Purge all hemomantic taint from your body, achieve biological immortality, and serve as a Guardian against blood magic's spread.

**Key Features:**
- Progressive purification stages (Clarity system)
- Hemolytic Solution-based rituals
- Copper and white aesthetics
- Defensive combat doctrine: Guardians favor blunt weapons, while the long-reach Silthmere Glaive and universal Absolution Dagger remain sanctioned
- Defensive and anti-corruption abilities
- Biological immortality (configurable)
- Service to Our Lady of Still Waters

---

## The Path to Purity

Unlike Harbingers with their seven discrete degrees, the Unstained pursue a gradual purification measured in **Clarity** and **Purity** levels.

### Starting the Journey

**Prerequisites:**
- Activated blood volume (clicked Mortal Display)
- May have experimented with blood magic (not required)
- Desire to be free of corruption

**First Steps:**
1. Use **Hemolytic Solution** at an **Unstained Podium** to suppress the infection
2. Perform **Lethean Baptism** to begin the Unstained path and start Purity
3. At full Purity, offer **Consecrated Copper** at the Podium to prepare Clarity
4. Perform **Clarity Ascension** to unlock Clarity and the advanced Silver Ward

**What Happens:**
- Lethean Baptism begins Purity; the Podium alone does not
- Clarity remains locked until Consecrated Copper preparation and Clarity Ascension
- Founding a bloodline closes ordinary cure; curing Annetta can unlock an exceptional founder-severance rite
- You officially begin purging the infection

## Purification Stages

The Unstained path progresses through increasing **Purity** levels, measured by accumulated **Clarity** points.

### Stage 1: Initiate (0-25% Purity)
**"First Steps Toward the Still Waters"**

**Process:**
- Perform White Humor Purification repeatedly
- Use Hemolytic Solution in rituals
- Begin incorporating copper into your equipment
- Study the writings of Our Lady

**What You Gain:**
- Minor resistance to fungal corruption
- Reduced effectiveness of blood manipulations on you
- Ability to craft basic Unstained items
- First whispers from Our Lady of Still Waters

**What Changes:**
- Blood manipulations you know become weaker
- Fungal whispers (if you had them) start to fade
- A sense of cold clarity replaces warm vitality
- NPCs react differently — some with relief, others with concern

**Physical Changes:**
- Blood-magic signs begin to fade; no unrelated bodily or cosmetic change occurs during Purity

---

### Stage 2: Purified (25-50% Purity)
**"The Infection Loosens Its Grip"**

**Process:**
- Advanced purification rituals
- Copper armor integration
- Lethean water usage (sacred water from Still Waters)
- Meditation in copper-lined spaces

**What You Gain:**
- Significant corruption resistance
- Enhanced healing when using Lethean Dew
- Guardian basic combat stance
- Ability to sense nearby blood magic
- Blunt weapon damage bonuses

**What Changes:**
- Blood manipulations you knew are now severely weakened or lost
- Fungal whispers completely cease
- You can feel the Pale Lady's presence
- Your blood no longer "remembers" as easily
- Common folk sense your purity (reactions vary)

**Physical Changes:**
- Bleeding diminishes as the infection loosens; skin, eyes, hair, hunger, poison, and ordinary healing remain unchanged

---

### Stage 3: Guardian (50-75% Purity)
**"Sworn to the Still Waters"**

**Process:**
- Take oaths to Our Lady of Still Waters
- Complete Guardian trials
- Forge a sacred copper weapon
- Consecrate your body fully

**What You Gain:**
- **Guardian Status** — Official role in Unstained hierarchy
- All blood manipulations lost (cannot learn new ones)
- Enhanced anti-corruption aura (protects others nearby)
- Guardian combat abilities
  - Crushing Strike (bonus damage with blunt weapons)
  - Pallid Shield (temporary invulnerability)
  - Purging Touch (cleanse corruption from others)
- Ability to destroy Harbinger corrupted items
- Copper armor set bonuses

**What Changes:**
- You are now a warrior for purity
- Harbingers see you as enemy or traitor
- Unstained NPCs see you as hero
- You cannot be infected or corrupted (immune)
- Your presence disrupts blood magic
- Physical appearance clearly marks you as Unstained

**Physical Changes:**
- Blood-themed signs and bleeding continue to diminish; there are no additional progressive bodily changes during Purity

---

### Stage 4: Absolved (75-100% Purity)
**"Prepared for the Stillness"**

**Process:**
- Final purification rituals
- Complete the rejection of blood magic
- Prepare Consecrated Copper at the Podium after reaching full Purity
- Perform Clarity Ascension to begin preserved biology

**What You Gain:**
- Preparation for the Clarity path toward biological immortality
- Enhanced Guardian abilities
- Aura of Stillness (massive anti-corruption field)
- Ability to consecrate areas with copper
- Access to Paragon-tier equipment
- The Pale Lady's direct attention

**What Changes:**
- Blood magic has been fully rejected, but broader preserved physiology does not begin until Clarity is unlocked
- No new cosmetic transformation occurs during the Purity phase

### Clarity and Preserved Biology

After Clarity Ascension, bodily processes diminish gradually with Clarity rather than changing all at once:

- Instant Health potions and Regeneration restore progressively less health, reaching 25% effectiveness at 100 Clarity.
- Poison deals progressively less damage, reaching 25% damage at 100 Clarity.
- Hunger drains progressively more slowly, reaching 35% of normal exhaustion at 100 Clarity.
- Purity alone provides none of these effects, and unrelated healing, drowning, and ordinary damage remain unchanged.
- You exist in a state of perfect preservation
- Fungal Entity cannot touch you

**Physical Changes:**
- Skin appears almost translucent
- Eyes are fully glassy, silver-reflective
- Hair is white or very pale
- Wounds weep clear fluid instead of blood
- No body odor (chemical preservation)

---

### Stage 5: Ascended (95-100% Purity)
**"Beyond Human, Beyond Infection"**

**Process:**
- Final communion with Our Lady of Still Waters
- Ultimate purification (extremely painful)
- Transcendence of biological limitations

**What You Gain:**
- Absolute immunity to all corruption
- Reality-warping purification abilities
- Can cleanse entire areas of fungal taint
- Our Lady's Champion status
- Ultimate Guardian powers
- Access to Unstained endgame content

**What Changes:**
Everything. You are preserved perfection, a living shrine to purity.

**WARNING:** Late-stage whispers from Our Lady hint at her becoming **autoimmune** — attacking even clean tissue. Ascended Unstained may face a terrible realization: purity taken too far becomes its own corruption.

**Physical Changes:**
- Appear as living statue
- Eyes like mirrors
- No bleeding ever
- Radiate cold aura
- Perfectly preserved forever

## Core Mechanics

### Hemolytic Solution
The cornerstone of Unstained purification, Hemolytic Solution is a formaldehyde-equivalent antiseptic that destroys fungal tissue.

**Crafting:**
- Specific alchemical recipe (check JEI)
- Requires common but careful ingredient combination
- Can be brewed in batches
- Store in glass vials

**Uses:**
- Primary component in purification rituals
- Applied directly to scarred skin (painful)
- Used to cleanse corrupted items
- Environmental purification
- Combat applications (damages infected creatures)

**Danger:**
- Toxic if consumed incorrectly
- Painful application process
- Overuse can damage healthy tissue
- Must be handled carefully

### White Humor Purification
The basic purification ritual, performed at a Copper Altar.

**Process:**
1. Construct Copper Altar (copper blocks + ritual components)
2. Prepare Hemolytic Solution
3. Place Solution and other components on altar
4. Activate ritual (right-click with empty hand)
5. Endure the pain (screen effects, damage taken)
6. Gain Clarity points

**Cost:**
- Hemolytic Solution (primary)
- Copper dust or nuggets
- Lethean Dew (higher stages)
- Health (ritual damages you)

**Rewards:**
- Clarity points toward next purity stage
- Temporary purification buffs
- Corruption resistance boost
- Step closer to immortality

**Tips:**
- Perform in safe location
- Have healing items ready
- Can be done repeatedly (diminishing returns)
- More painful at higher purity levels

### Copper Equipment

Copper is sacred to the Unstained, representing conductivity of purity and resistance to corruption.

**Copper Armor**
- Craftable full set
- Bonuses against infected creatures
- Corruption resistance
- Enhanced when purified yourself
- Can be oxidized for different bonuses

**Copper Weapons**
- Maces, hammers, and clubs are preferred
- The Silthmere Glaive is sanctioned for defensive reach
- Every initiated Unstained carries an inherently hemolytic Absolution Dagger
- Extra damage to corrupted entities
- No bleed effects (by design)
- Can be blessed at Copper Altar for enhancement

**Copper Tools**
- Picks, shovels, hoes
- Can cleanse corrupted blocks
- Slower than iron but purifying

**Copper Structures**
- Copper Altars for rituals
- Copper-lined sanctuaries (corruption-free zones)
- Copper stakes for marking purified territory

### Guardian Combat Style

Unstained Guardians minimize bloodshed and specialize in blunt combat, but their doctrine explicitly permits the defensive Silthmere Glaive and universal Absolution Dagger.

**Allowed Weapons:**
- Maces
- Hammers
- Clubs
- Fists
- Copper implements
- Staves
- Silthmere Glaive
- Absolution Dagger

**Discouraged Weapons:**
- Ordinary swords and axes used to spill blood
- Close-range blades without an antiseptic or reach-based purpose

**Combat Abilities:**

**Crushing Strike**
- Massive damage with blunt weapons
- Bonus against infected/corrupted
- Chance to stun
- Costs Clarity temporarily

**Pallid Shield**
- Temporary invulnerability bubble
- Reflects blood magic
- Short duration
- Long cooldown

**Purging Touch**
- Touch enemy to inflict purification damage
- Removes buffs from infected creatures
- Can cleanse corruption from items/blocks
- Medium cooldown

**Aura of Stillness** (High Purity)
- Passive aura around you
- Reduces corruption in area
- Weakens blood magic within radius
- Calms aggressive infected creatures

## Lethean Water

**Lethean Dew** and **Tears of Silthmere** are sacred fluids associated with Our Lady of Still Waters.

### Lethean Dew
- Clear, cold water from sacred springs
- Found in specific biomes/structures
- Used in purification rituals
- Heals Unstained more effectively than normal healing
- Can be collected and stored

**Effects:**
- Strong healing for Unstained
- Removes minor corruption
- Grants temporary purity boost
- Calming effect (removes negative mental status)

### Tears of Silthmere
- Rare, more potent version
- Found only in special locations
- Used in high-level purification
- Grants temporary Guardian abilities
- Extremely valuable

**Effects:**
- Major healing and cleansing
- Temporary purity spike
- Enhanced Guardian powers
- Can revive from near-death

## Our Lady of Still Waters

The Pale Lady is the entity that guides the Unstained — a localized force of nature, analogous to a white blood cell defending against infection.

### Her Nature
- **Not a goddess** — She is a force, not a deity
- Defensive immune response to the Fungal Entity
- Represents stillness, preservation, clarity
- Associated with water, copper, white/silver

### Her Guidance
- Whispers to Purified and higher
- Messages emphasize:
  - Purity over power
  - Preservation over growth
  - Stillness over movement
  - Clarity over passion

### Her Gifts
- Biological immortality
- Corruption immunity
- Guardian powers
- Lethean water

### Her Warning
Late-stage whispers hint at danger:
- *"Is this purity, or merely another form of death?"*
- *"The still waters can drown as surely as the flood."*
- *"What if the infection was never the true enemy?"*

At highest purity levels, some Unstained realize: Our Lady may be becoming **autoimmune**, attacking healthy tissue in her zeal to destroy infection. The path to perfect purity may not end where you expect.

## Progression Tips

1. **Start Early** — Begin purification before high Harbinger degrees. Reverting from Archon is traumatic.

2. **Stock Hemolytic Solution** — You'll need lots. Brew in batches.

3. **Copper Everywhere** — Build with copper blocks. They provide passive benefits.

4. **Accept the Pain** — Purification hurts. That's the point. Have healing ready.

5. **Carry the Right Tool** — The polearm is the battlefield standard; every initiated Unstained also carries an Absolution Dagger for close infection work.

6. **Find Lethean Water** — Locate a source and protect it. It's your best healing.

7. **Study Our Lady** — Her whispers contain warnings and wisdom. Listen.

8. **Don't Rush Purity** — Each stage is significant. Understand what you're becoming.

9. **Guardian Trials** — Practice combat before taking Guardian oaths. You'll need skill.

10. **Question Everything** — Late-game whispers hint at problems. Stay aware.

## Philosophical Considerations

### Is This Really Better?

The mod intentionally questions whether the Unstained path is truly superior:

**Arguments For:**
- No corruption or infection
- Biological immortality
- Clear purpose and moral framework
- Immune to Fungal Entity
- Protect others from infection

**Arguments Against:**
- Methods are brutal (self-inflicted chemical embalming)
- Loss of all hemomantic abilities
- Physically changed, perhaps disfigured
- Late-stage hints at autoimmune disaster
- "Purity" may be its own form of death
- Living, but preserved — is that truly living?

Neither path is objectively correct. Choose based on your values and playstyle.

---

## Mutual Exclusion

**The Unstained Path and Harbinger Path are mutually exclusive.**

### Starting Unstained While Harbinger
- First White Humor Purification zeros your degree
- Lose all known manipulations
- Bloodline dissolved
- Extremely painful lore-wise
- Cannot reverse easily

### Starting Harbinger While Unstained
- First degree rite zeros your purity
- Lose Guardian status
- Corruption floods back
- Our Lady's whispers cease
- Clarity lost

### Can You Switch Back?
Technically yes, but it's devastating:
- Lose all progress in abandoned path
- Severe penalties to switching
- Lore-wise described as traumatic
- Not recommended for roleplay reasons

Choose your path and commit.

## Common Questions

**Q: Can Unstained use any blood magic?**
A: At low purity, weakened versions. At Guardian and above, none at all.

**Q: Do I have to give up blood magic completely?**
A: Yes, eventually. That's the point of purification.

**Q: Is biological immortality optional?**
A: Check config settings. Can be disabled if you prefer.

**Q: Can Unstained and Harbingers be friends?**
A: Lore-wise, complicated. Gameplay-wise, opposing factions but can coexist.

**Q: What happens if I drink Hemolytic Solution?**
A: Damage and poison effects. It's for external/ritual use.

**Q: Is Our Lady evil?**
A: No. She's an immune response. But immune responses can go wrong (autoimmune).

**Q: Can I serve both paths?**
A: No. They are mutually exclusive by design.

**Q: Are Unstained the "good guys"?**
A: Not necessarily. Their methods are brutal. Neither path is objectively good or evil.

## Observances and Quiet Work

Church Acolytes, Zealots, and Guardians offer nine persistent assignments called **Observances**. Speak to the relevant office to accept one, then return with its offering to claim the reward. The first accepted assignment grants the **Book of Observances**, which opens its own ledger of active and completed work grouped by the NPC who directed it. The **Self-Reflection Mirror** remains the separate Unstained progression screen.

- Gather four Ghost Pipe blooms.
- Weave a Lethean Poppy Wreath.
- Prepare two Hemolytic Solutions.
- Consecrate four Copper Ingots.
- Offer a Lethean Chalice after Clarity is awakened.

Observances pay substantial one-time Purity or Clarity and useful pale reagents. Acolytes teach gathering and preparation, Zealots ask for distilled water and devotional craft, and Guardians provision the church's hemolytic watch. They are deliberately non-combat objectives, making patient gathering, cultivation, restraint, and service the fastest thematic route through purification.

## Specialized Stations

The **Stillwater Condenser** has a dedicated vessel interface that turns stored glass bottles into Lethean Dew when placed directly above source water with living Ghost Pipe within four blocks. It unlocks in practice at 50 Purity. A nearby Verdigris Lattice within five blocks halves processing time and doubles each batch.

The **Verdigris Lattice** is a visible copper-and-silver ward fixture rather than a solid construction block. Its core pulses every four seconds, granting Resistance to nearby Unstained players while weakening and slowing Hemomancy creatures. Inspecting it reports its eight-block shelter and condenser effect.

The Pale Silver Pickaxe and Verdigris Censer are intentionally not part of the active path.
