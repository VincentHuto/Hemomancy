# The Qliphoth

The Qliphoth is Hemomancy's Tree of Death. It is not a simple symbol of evil. It is the inverse side of life, decay, and revelation, and in the fiction it places blood magic inside a fungal reproductive and memory cycle rather than a purely human occult tradition. That revelation drives the Harbinger endgame.

The mechanic is **Qliphoth Communion**. An Archon shatters the **Sanguine Monolith**, performs the **Bloom of the Qliphoth** cardinal rite, raises a protected bloom, and waits for its nine **Qliphoth Pomes**. The rite registers the Bloom's owner and pome lifecycle; placing a Bloom block alone does not establish that bond. Consuming all nine from one bloom completes Communion.

During Pome empowerment, the Vicar notices the change but still offers ordinary services and valid unfinished lessons. His reaction does not complete an ending or change your degree.

Make room for the Fungal Spine when the ninth pome completes Communion. A full inventory leaves its first delivery pending until space opens. That pending first claim survives death; retry waits until you are alive and can actually carry it. Periodic whispers are not a prerequisite for receiving the Spine. If you later lose it, sneak-use your original living Bloom empty-handed to reclaim it. You must have consumed that Bloom's nine husks and have inventory space. Carrying a Spine prevents another claim; rebuilding a Bloom at the same coordinates does not restore the old claim.

The ninth pome is the break point: it makes the **Fungal Spine** tear free. Its first use is a two-minute consciousness projection with no accessible items or armor; an accelerating red vignette ends in forced return. Only then does the player answer the revelation. Choosing Silence records a pending refusal: striking the owned Bloom with any base Living Weapon reshapes it into a temporary Living Sickle; a second cut opens the wound as a retryable portal into a separate 50-by-50 Chamber arena. Defeating Vesper first as the Crowned Refusal and then as the Evening Star earns the Degree-7 **Silent Archon** state and the one-time Memory of Vesper. Absorbing that memory into the Living Staff permanently unlocks the sickle in the radial's interior ring. Death, logout, or leaving resets only the fight; the open wound remains available to its owner. Victory seals the wound into an inert trophy. The **Rite of Apotheosis** remains the alternate advance to Degree 8 Apotheos.

The Memory of Vesper waits if your inventory is full. Its earned pending claim survives death and delivers on login or after you free a slot. Only an explicit older pending claim is migrated; being a Silent Archon does not grant replacement Memories.

The Materials atlas shows the Communion chain at D7 and teaches Memory of Vesper only after your first projection returns. The Seed comes from pressing further in the Sanguine Monolith conversation; seat it in the Bloom rite, then consume the nine bound Pomes from that one Bloom. Saint trials are not prerequisites for this chain.

The revelation explains before selection that neither response completes its ending. The Silence confirmation gives the two-cut sequence and wound-entry instruction, and states that refusal remains pending at Degree 7 until victory over both faces of Vesper. An untransformed Living Staff is not the base Living Weapon required for that first cut.

After the second cut, use the wound normally; the restored Living Weapon can remain in hand. Logging out between cuts restores the original Staff, so reshape it and repeat the first cut on the same Bloom when returning.

Planting and rooting an owned Qliphoth Bloom also changes **The Chamber of Will** for players who have unlocked the Degree 6 refuge. The chamber's sky shifts to the `qliphoth_communion` theme: dark red-purple-blue mist, root-script/star-glyph patterns, and one small shader-rendered red-black sky vortex for each of the first eight consumed pomes. At nine pomes, those small vortices become residual rings and a massive black hole opens at the zenith, making the caster's supposedly private blood-memory visibly less private.
