# The Qliphoth

The Qliphoth is Hemomancy's Tree of Death: not a simple symbol of evil, but the inverse side of life, decay, and revelation. In the mod's fiction, it reframes blood magic as part of a fungal reproductive and memory cycle rather than a purely human occult tradition. That revelation is central to the Harbinger endgame.

In gameplay, this unfolds through **Qliphoth Communion**. An Archon first shatters the **Sanguine Monolith**, then performs the **Bloom of the Qliphoth** cardinal rite to raise a protected bloom. Over time the bloom produces nine **Qliphoth Pomes**, one for each husk, and consuming all nine from a single bloom marks the player as having completed Communion.

That sequence matters mechanically and narratively. The ninth pome makes the **Fungal Spine** tear free. Its first use is a two-minute consciousness projection with no accessible items or armor; an accelerating red vignette ends in forced return. Only then does the player answer the revelation: **Pruning of the Qliphoth** leaves them Degree 7 as a Silent Archon, while the **Rite of Apotheosis** advances them to Degree 8 Apotheos.

Planting and rooting an owned Qliphoth Bloom also changes **The Chamber of Will** for players who have unlocked the Degree 6 refuge. The chamber's sky shifts to the `qliphoth_communion` theme: dark red-purple-blue mist, root-script/star-glyph patterns, and one small shader-rendered red-black sky vortex for each of the first eight consumed pomes. At nine pomes, those small vortices become residual rings and a massive black hole opens at the zenith, making the caster's supposedly private blood-memory visibly less private.
