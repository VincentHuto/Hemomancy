# Saints and Encounters

The Saints' mausoleums open their outer halls in the middle Harbinger degrees; the Saints themselves wait for Degree 8. The four Saints are ancient hemomancers whose blood carried their bodies past ordinary limits. Their own people sealed them away, and the Hematic Order inherited the tombs and keeps them. Sainthood is not goodness: a Saint is proof of what the blood can become, not an example to follow. The full biographies are in [the lore reference](../docs/LORE_REFERENCE.md#10-the-saints).

## Finding a Saint

Each Saint rests in its own mausoleum complex. The entrance, antechamber, ossuary stair and crypt gallery are open to Harbingers from about Degree 3–4: opened burial niches hold early loot, and a reliquary behind an iron door off the gallery holds the better chest. There is no trial in the outer halls for now. From the gallery a stair spirals two turns down round an open well to the threshold. A ward across the threshold turns back anyone below Degree 8 (a faint sheet over the line, a flare and a toll where they touch it); past it a galleried nave runs back under the halls above, three Chapel wings leave it (one at its floor, one from its gallery, one down into a crypt), and the ring door at its end opens on the Saint's chamber, the lowest room, under the entrance itself: a stair down into a great pillared hall with a wide clear floor for the fight and an aisle behind each rank of pillars, and at the far end the tomb, long enough for a lying body, on a three-step dais under its own vault. Each Chapel's trial plays across its wing: witnesses to walk back to, a stone to read and stands to choose from, a gauntlet hall, a ring to keep still in. The Saint fights themselves are Degree-8 content. A Silentium completes the Chapels and opens the ring door with their blood for a proper awakening. An Apotheos can shatter the door at any time, but the Saint wakes enraged with the power of every skipped Chapel, gives only a fragment of its Hallowed Residuum, and the hallowed ground burns the Apotheos while they stay. Hallowed ground is consecrated, not holy: it is the Saint's own residue rejecting the hive. Saints are not random: a sarcophagus holds the Saint it was built for, and an unassigned sarcophagus does nothing until a **Saint relic** names one. Pressing a relic to the sarcophagus names that Saint and wakes it.

Consecrate a sarcophagus by pouring blood into the bowl at its foot, slide the lid off, and draw from the corpus with an empty Blood Vial. If your dominant blood tendency is one of the Saint's two tendencies, the corpus gives a **Consecrated Syringe** once, peacefully; the Vial Centrifuge turns it into Hallowed Residuum. Otherwise the Saint wakes and the encounter becomes a boss fight. Foul Paste smeared on the sarcophagus wakes the Saint whatever your alignment.

Hallowed Residuum is the catalyst for the Saint's Canon Memory at the Somatic Loom, woven with the Saint's two tendency enzymes and projected blood.

## The encounters

No Saint can be killed. Every fight ends with the Saint returned to its tomb and the sarcophagus restored, closed and dormant. Each ending rolls the Saint's reward once: its Hallowed Residuum and one or two Sanguine Quintessence.

- **Hemorath** drains nearby blood and collects wounds and casting as debt. His stored blood leaks, so overload means feeding him faster than he empties. Watch the outer ring before his reaching siphon. He is exsanguinated, not killed: his marrow never stops making blood, and in time he reforms.
- **Seraphae** heals wounds through luminous transfusions, then keeps growing tissue after the wound is closed. Leave her nerve reach before repeated excess growth causes convulsions. Dispersed nerve impulses arrive in regular waves. Bind her fragments, light the containment anchors and strike while she condenses until she is chained again.
- **Putriciel** releases Withering rot novas without fire. During absolution, novas stop and cleansing flame pulses close to their body after a warning. Time five hits between those burning pulses. The fifth purifies them, and they rest for a time before the rot reclaims them.
- **Velorum** builds stillness around players who stand near him; movement resists it. A landed strike can briefly interrupt his powers and release the pressure, with a cooldown before the next interruption. Outside that opening, frost, darkness, hit resistance and low-health blood drain preserve his attack rhythm. Defeat returns him to dormant stillness.

The models now follow the reconciled lore: iron erupts through Hemorath's bones;
Seraphae's wings branch into exposed luminous nerves; Putriciel carries growing
fungal colonies and dripping olive rot, with cleansing flame during absolution;
Velorum wears a plain dark hood and knotted monastic cord. The next attack pass adds Hemorath's leaking blood absorption and close siphon, Seraphae's healing and dangerous excess growth, Putriciel's close-range cleansing pulses, and Velorum's stillness pressure. Keep moving near Velorum; a landed strike can interrupt his powers briefly before the opening goes on cooldown. Trial chambers and a rescuable frozen congregation remain planned.

## Secondary attacks

Warnings commit to their first position or direction. Moving afterward does not drag the attack after you.

- **Hemorath:** step between Iron Rake's three lanes; jump Marrow Rupture or leave its outer circle.
- **Seraphae:** sidestep Nerve Lance and leave the circles where Regrowth Knots take root.
- **Putriciel:** leave Spore Garden's three colonies and move behind Censer Sweep's forward fan. These rot attacks stop during cleansing.
- **Velorum:** use Frozen Advance's lane gaps or jump its approaching fronts; leave Shadow Cross diagonally.

## Annetta Knowles

**Annetta Knowles**, the Stained Priestess, is a separate authored encounter in the **Broken Church**. Harbingers can push her into a tragic boss escalation, while Unstained players can externalize and fight her latent infection. The route depends on the player's ideology as well as the combat mechanics. Watch her closely when you find her: Unstained lose their colour, but Annetta still has long red hair and gold eyes. A Harbinger who unseals a jar of Tooth Pecks near her starts a long, frantic fight that ends only when she breaks; what rises from her body afterwards flies, hurls her lancea, drops on you spear first, and wakes thorns of blood out of the floor wherever it strikes. An Unstained with Clarity can offer her the Draught of Still Mercy instead, and then fight what it draws out of her with Annetta at their side, healing them.
