# Saints and Encounters

The Saints system is planned for the middle Harbinger degrees. The four Saints are ancient hemomancers whose blood carried their bodies past ordinary limits. Their own people sealed them away, and the Hematic Order inherited the tombs and keeps them. Sainthood is not goodness: a Saint is proof of what the blood can become, not an example to follow. The full biographies are in [the lore reference](../docs/LORE_REFERENCE.md#10-the-saints).

| Saint | Title | Tendencies | Canon Memory |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hemorath** | The First Saint | Animus + Ferric | Crimson Tithe |
| **Seraphae** | The Chain Saint | Lux + Ductilis | Unclosing Eye |
| **Putriciel** | The Befouled Saint | Mortem + Flammeus | Bloom of Rot |
| **Velorum** | Saint of the Endless Hour | Congeatio + Tenebris | Endless Hour |

All four, including Hemorath's implemented prototype, are reserved for the first major post-release content update. The chambers, trials, rewards, Canon memories, balance, and presentation stay behind the same boundary instead of exposing one Saint early. The underlying systems remain available to developers through the Hemomancy WIP tab.

## Finding a Saint

Each Saint is planned to get a dedicated trial chamber. Only one generic mausoleum exists today, and it does not generate in the world. Saints are not random: a sarcophagus holds the Saint it was built for, and an unassigned sarcophagus does nothing until a **Saint relic** names one. Pressing a relic to the sarcophagus names that Saint and wakes it.

Consecrate a sarcophagus by pouring blood into the bowl at its foot, slide the lid off, and draw from the corpus with an empty Blood Vial. If your dominant blood tendency is one of the Saint's two tendencies, the corpus gives a **Consecrated Syringe** once, peacefully; the Vial Centrifuge turns it into Hallowed Residuum. Otherwise the Saint wakes and the encounter becomes a boss fight. Foul Paste smeared on the sarcophagus wakes the Saint whatever your alignment.

Hallowed Residuum is the catalyst for the Saint's Canon Memory at the Somatic Loom, woven with the Saint's two tendency enzymes and projected blood.

## The encounters

No Saint can be killed. Every fight ends with the Saint returned to its tomb and the sarcophagus restored, closed and dormant. Each ending rolls the Saint's reward once: its Hallowed Residuum and one or two Sanguine Quintessence.

- **Hemorath** feeds on the blood you lose near him, by wounds or by casting, and collects it as debt. The same blood fills him, so spend enough and he overloads and bursts. He is exsanguinated, not killed: his marrow never stops making blood, and in time he reforms.
- **Seraphae** floods her chamber with burning light. Bind her fragments, light the containment anchors and strike while she condenses until she is chained again. She is immortal and can only be contained.
- **Putriciel** releases rot novas, then briefly stills. Land five hits during those absolution windows. The fifth purifies them, and they rest for a time before the rot reclaims them.
- **Velorum** roots you with frost, blinds you with darkness, hardens briefly after every hit, and drains blood when weakened. Defeat returns him to his dormant stillness.

Each Saint will later get its own full combat and visual overhaul; the current fights are the generic versions.

## Annetta Knowles

**Annetta Knowles**, the Stained Priestess, is a separate authored encounter in the **Broken Church**. Harbingers can push her into a tragic boss escalation, while Unstained players can externalize and fight her latent infection. The route depends on the player's ideology as well as the combat mechanics. Watch her closely when you find her: Unstained lose their colour, but Annetta still has long red hair and gold eyes. A Harbinger who unseals a jar of Tooth Pecks near her starts a long, frantic fight that ends only when she breaks; what rises from her body afterwards flies, hurls her lancea, drops on you spear first, and wakes thorns of blood out of the floor wherever it strikes. An Unstained with Clarity can offer her the Draught of Still Mercy instead, and then fight what it draws out of her with Annetta at their side, healing them.
