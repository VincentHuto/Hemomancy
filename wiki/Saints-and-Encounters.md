# Saints and Encounters

The Saints system opens around the middle Harbinger degrees and frames ancient Hemomancers as entombed powers rather than ordinary dead. Each Saint is tied to a **Trial Chamber** that seals the player inside, demands a trial, and culminates in a sarcophagus encounter. If the player's blood tendency aligns, the Saint can be sampled peacefully with a **Consecrated Syringe**; if not, the Saint awakens and the encounter becomes a boss fight.

The current saint roster includes **Hemorath**, **Seraphae the Chain Saint**, **Putriciel**, and **Velorum**. Initial lore, the shared sarcophagus foundation, and Hemorath's complete trial form the first-release scope. Seraphae, Putriciel, and Velorum are deliberately reserved for the first major post-release Saints update, where their bespoke rooms, environmental storytelling, victory conditions, and presentation can express their individual tragedies. Their rewards feed directly into **Hallowed Residuum** and **Canon Memory** progression through the **Somatic Loom**.

Outside the saint line, one of the mod's most important authored encounters is **Annetta Knowles**, the Stained Priestess. Found inside the **Broken Church**, she supports a two-route encounter: Harbingers can push her into a tragic boss escalation, while Unstained players can externalize and fight her latent infection instead. That structure makes Hemomancy's major encounters feel ideological and narrative, not just mechanical set pieces.
