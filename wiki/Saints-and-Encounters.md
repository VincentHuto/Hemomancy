# Saints and Encounters

The Saints system is planned to open around the middle Harbinger degrees and frames ancient Hemomancers as entombed powers rather than ordinary dead. Each Saint is tied to a **Trial Chamber** that seals the player inside, demands a trial, and culminates in a sarcophagus encounter. If the player's blood tendency aligns, the Saint can be sampled peacefully with a **Consecrated Syringe**; if not, the Saint awakens and the encounter becomes a boss fight.

The planned saint roster includes **Hemorath**, **Seraphae the Chain Saint**, **Putriciel**, and **Velorum**. The entire suite—including Hemorath's implemented prototype—is deliberately reserved for the first major post-release content update. Its chambers, environmental storytelling, victory conditions, rewards, Canon memories, balance, and presentation will launch together rather than exposing one Saint early. The underlying systems remain available to developers through the Hemomancy WIP tab.

Outside the saint line, one of the mod's most important authored encounters is **Annetta Knowles**, the Stained Priestess. Found inside the **Broken Church**, she supports a two-route encounter: Harbingers can push her into a tragic boss escalation, while Unstained players can externalize and fight her latent infection instead. That structure makes Hemomancy's major encounters feel ideological and narrative, not just mechanical set pieces.
