# Saints and Encounters

The Saints system is planned for the middle Harbinger degrees. These ancient Hemomancers are entombed powers, not ordinary dead. Each Saint has a **Trial Chamber** that seals the player in, runs a trial, and ends at a sarcophagus. A matching blood tendency allows peaceful sampling with a **Consecrated Syringe**. Otherwise, the Saint wakes and the encounter becomes a boss fight.

The planned roster is **Hemorath**, **Seraphae the Chain Saint**, **Putriciel**, and **Velorum**. All four, including Hemorath's implemented prototype, are reserved for the first major post-release content update. The chambers, trials, rewards, Canon memories, balance, and presentation stay behind the same boundary instead of exposing one Saint early. The underlying systems remain available to developers through the Hemomancy WIP tab.

**Annetta Knowles**, the Stained Priestess, is a separate authored encounter in the **Broken Church**. Harbingers can push her into a tragic boss escalation, while Unstained players can externalize and fight her latent infection. The route depends on the player's ideology as well as the combat mechanics.
