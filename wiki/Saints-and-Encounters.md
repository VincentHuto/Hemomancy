# Saints and Encounters

The Saints' mausoleums open their outer halls in the middle Harbinger degrees; the Saints themselves wait for Degree 8. The four Saints are ancient hemomancers whose blood carried their bodies past ordinary limits. Their own people sealed them away, and the Hematic Order inherited the tombs and keeps them. Sainthood is not goodness: a Saint is proof of what the blood can become, not an example to follow. The full biographies are in [the lore reference](../docs/LORE_REFERENCE.md#10-the-saints).

| Saint | Title | Tempers | Canon Memory |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hemorath** | The Hollow Vessel | Animus + Ferric | Crimson Tithe |
| **Seraphae** | The Chain Saint | Lux + Ductilis | Unclosing Eye |
| **Putriciel** | The Befouled Saint | Mortem + Flammeus | Bloom of Rot |
| **Velorum** | Saint of the Endless Hour | Congeatio + Tenebris | Endless Hour |

All four, including Hemorath's implemented prototype, are reserved for the first major post-release content update. The chambers, trials, rewards, Canon memories, balance, and presentation stay behind the same boundary instead of exposing one Saint early. The underlying systems remain available to developers through the Hemomancy WIP tab.

## Finding a Saint

Each Saint rests in its own mausoleum complex. The entrance, antechamber, ossuary stair and crypt gallery are open to Harbingers from about Degree 3–4: opened burial niches hold early loot, and a reliquary behind an iron door off the gallery holds the better chest. There is no trial in the outer halls for now. From the gallery a stair spirals two turns down round an open well to the threshold. A ward across the threshold turns back anyone below Degree 8 (a faint sheet over the line, a flare and a toll where they touch it); past it a galleried nave runs back under the halls above, three Chapel wings leave it (one at its floor, one from its gallery, one down into a crypt), and the ring door at its end opens on the Saint's chamber, the lowest room, under the entrance itself: a stair down into a great pillared hall with a wide clear floor for the fight and an aisle behind each rank of pillars, and at the far end the tomb, long enough for a lying body, on a three-step dais under its own vault. Each Chapel's trial plays across its wing: witnesses to walk back to, a stone to read and stands to choose from, a gauntlet hall, a ring to keep still in. The Saint fights themselves are Degree-8 content. The ring door opens to Blood Projection held on it for ten seconds (1000 blood on either path); let go and its progress slowly bleeds away, and right-clicking it does nothing. A Silentium completes the Chapels first and their blood spreads over the door and turns to black-and-red monolith stone before it sinks open, for a proper awakening. An Apotheos can force the door at any time, its hyphae spreading over it and cracking it until it bursts, but the Saint wakes enraged with the power of every skipped Chapel, gives only a fragment of its Hallowed Residuum, and the hallowed ground burns the Apotheos while they stay. Hallowed ground is consecrated, not holy: it is the Saint's own residue rejecting the hive. Saints are not random: a sarcophagus holds the Saint it was built for, and an unassigned sarcophagus does nothing until a **Saint relic** names one. Pressing a relic to the sarcophagus names that Saint and wakes it.

Consecrate a sarcophagus by pouring blood into the bowl at its foot, slide the lid off, and draw from the corpus with an empty Blood Vial. If your dominant blood temper is one of the Saint's two tempers, the corpus gives a **Consecrated Syringe** once, peacefully; the Vial Centrifuge turns it into Hallowed Residuum. Otherwise the Saint wakes and the encounter becomes a boss fight. Foul Paste smeared on the sarcophagus wakes the Saint whatever your alignment.

Hallowed Residuum is the catalyst for the Saint's Canon Memory at the Somatic Loom, woven with the Saint's two temper enzymes and projected blood.

## The encounters

No Saint can be killed. Every fight ends with the Saint returned to its tomb and the sarcophagus restored, closed and dormant, and the chamber put back as it was. Each ending rolls the Saint's reward once: its Hallowed Residuum and one or two Sanguine Quintessence. If you leave the chamber empty for half a minute, the Saint goes back to its tomb without a reward.

Every Saint fights the same way underneath. Each attack winds up before it lands, and its shape shows on the floor while it does: watch the floor, not only the Saint. Once the attack commits it stops following you. After many attacks the Saint stays open for a moment, and those openings are where the fight is won: each Saint takes little or nothing from blows outside them. A meter under the Saint's health bar shows the thing that fight is about. Each fight also asks for something beyond hitting: holding Blood Absorption or Blood Projection, spending or keeping your blood, casting a school, or simply moving.

- **Hemorath** drinks the blood of everyone near him into a vessel you can see swelling in his ribs, and the vessel leaks all the time down the floor gutters into four wells. Hollow, he is fast and hard as iron; full, he is slow and soft. Feed him faster than he leaks (cast near him, hold Blood Projection into his chest when he sags gorged) to overload him, or cut him down while he is swollen and keep him from drinking his wells. His first full vessel empties across the floor instead of bursting him ("The Vessel Empties"): get off the boss floor before the tide. He is exsanguinated, not killed: his marrow never stops making blood, and in time he reforms.
- **Seraphae** wants to heal you. Near her a wound closes, and then keeps growing until it slows you, roots you and bursts. White light heals and grows; a gold flash along her nerves is the strike. Her reach is her chain: stand just past where it ends and the chain stops her short, and strike while she is drawn back. Blows outside her openings close in white and do nothing. Re-bind the anchors she tears loose and wake the reserve anchors with held Blood Projection, and draw her loose fragments in with held Blood Absorption, until her containment is full and the anchors draw her back into the tomb.
- **Putriciel** cannot be beaten down, only absolved. In the rot, fungus grows on anyone who stands near them; every blow you land, and every fire or Flammeus cast, stokes the censer under their bar. When it fills, the purifier wakes in fire: the pyres light, the fire is the most dangerous thing in the room, and its heat burns your own rot off. Twice in each fire Putriciel kneels and holds out the open palm: one solid blow, or blood poured into the palm with Blood Projection, lights one of five nodules. The fire scorches the floor to ash, and only the ash is safe when the rot blooms. Some of their rot attacks leave thick, glossy pools of sludge on the floor: wading through one slows you and grows the fungus on you faster, so they cut the floor into lanes. They stay until the fire comes. When the purifier wakes, Putriciel's whole body burns, the staff like a torch and the back tallest, and the fire runs out across the floor to the pools, from the burning body, from the pyres and from one pool to the next. Each pool catches, boils, crusts black and crumbles to ash, and that ash is safe ground when the rot blooms again. The fifth absolution purifies them, and they rest for a time before the rot reclaims them.
- **Velorum** fights from stillness. Under his bar is your own stillness: it climbs while you stand, sneak, use an item or are slowed, and only travelling brings it down; when it fills you are preserved in rime for a moment and badly hurt. Outside his openings he barely feels a blow and will not fall past his next floor. Watch for his lantern guttering, his raised hand shaking under your blows, and his bow beneath a wound. The frozen faithful are cover from his gaze and his darkness, and if you woke him properly, warm blood from Blood Projection wakes them to fight beside you. If you forced the door, they rise against you. When his body fails, the faithful walk back to their places and kneel. All his ice is frozen blood, dark red under pale frost, and you can always see where it comes from: welling up in the floor's seams, running in veins out of the frozen figures, or breaking out of your own blood where you stood still. His darkness is a liquid that pools across the floor wherever his lantern does not reach; standing in it makes your stillness climb faster and narrows your sight. When his lantern gutters the dark floods in and he steps down into it to rise somewhere among the congregation. Jump his low cuts of dark and stoop under the high one. At the end he puts out every light in the room: only the seams glow red, and the frozen faithful stand up as shapes of darkness that grab and reach. Pour warm blood into him with Blood Projection to light his lantern again.

A Saint woken by force fights harder, and every Chapel you skipped lends it something of its own: hidden warnings, unpaused attacks, new moves.

The models follow the reconciled lore: Hemorath frail and sunken at rest, swelling only as he fills, with iron erupting through his bone; Seraphae's wings branching nerves, white in the vessels and gold only in the flashes; Putriciel in ivory vestments, olive and mould-green robes, ochre and cream shelves and dark eschar, with no red until the cleansing sets the staff, censers, back and shoulders on fire; Velorum in a plain dark hood and knotted cord, rime and black, with frozen blood on his halo's links and in every blade, spine and slab of ice he raises.

## Annetta Knowles

**Annetta Knowles**, the Stained Priestess, is a separate authored encounter in the **Broken Church**. Harbingers can push her into a tragic boss escalation, while Unstained players can externalize and fight her latent infection. The route depends on the player's ideology as well as the combat mechanics. Watch her closely when you find her: Unstained lose their colour, but Annetta still has long red hair and gold eyes. A Harbinger who unseals a jar of Tooth Pecks near her starts a long, frantic fight that ends only when she breaks; what rises from her body afterwards flies, hurls her lancea, drops on you spear first, and wakes thorns of blood out of the floor wherever it strikes. An Unstained with Clarity can offer her the Draught of Still Mercy instead, and then fight what it draws out of her with Annetta at their side, healing them.
