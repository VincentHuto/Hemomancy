# Animus and Mortem visuals

Blood Shots fly as a living droplet: a glossy core in a translucent sheath with a clot at its back, dragging a twisting sinew tail along the path it actually flew. Guided shots sway two seeking feelers, mortars carry orbiting clots and shed drips, and halo shots stay tethered to the caster. On contact a shot bursts into a crown of droplets that streak, fall and settle: it sprays on through a body, splashes back and stains a block the moment it lodges, and a mortar bursts upward over a shock ring. Blood Needles from the Living Pistol, Bloodburst armour and Vesper keep the glossy blood spine: a tapered dark-cored needle with a hairline wake that quivers and wells a bead when lodged, and spurts a narrow jet on puncture. The Blood Needle manipulations are Ferric and forge their needles in iron instead; see [Ferric and Ductilis](Ferric-Ductilis.md).

Animus blood moves as fresh crimson liquid: gathered needles, guided projectile trails, thin binding coils and circulating surfaces on the existing Avatar and Coronation swords. Blood clouds drift and shed narrow threads; Sanguine Tempest strikes with crimson bolts.

Hematic Riposte grows a limb the body never had. An oversized arm erupts between the shoulder blades, arcs over one shoulder at a seeded side and height, and slaps across the wearer's view before fraying into wisps. Its translucent shell flickers and trails behind the sweep around twisting sinew strands that pulse with a heartbeat; four long claws curl through the slap with clotted tips. The root follows body facing and the slap follows head facing, so it crosses the centre of the first-person view. Every cast retires the previous cue before sending a fresh one, so rapid recasts replay the whole swing. A successful parry flashes at the contact point with radial claw sparks and crossing streaks for melee, or a deflection streak for a swatted projectile, alongside the shared claw slash and a shed of blood drops.

Blood Aneurysm swells a veined knot at the hand whose beat quickens with the charge. On release a bulging vessel reaches the victim and a sac swells inside them, then tears: a hot flash, flung shreds, ruptured vessels that whip out and go limp, a sphere of spray and a clotted haze that lingers.

Crimson Coronation rings the head with a crown of blood, one spike for every sword it holds. Each armed sword hangs from its spike by twisted sinew with a bead of blood running down to the pommel, leans outward, and condenses after the one before it.

Mortem appears as dried blood, fissures, sinking sludge and wisps. Hemorrhage tears, Hunger contracts, and Grave Debt erupts before a successful refund draws blood back into the caster. Carrion Communion maintains streams from its victims. Blackhearted's clot responds to saturation. Funeral Bell is hollow and spectral.

Funeral Bell condenses a hollow spectral bell above the caster, hung from a rope of tendons and swaying harder as the charge builds. It tolls once: the bell swings on its pivot against its clapper, a ring of sound bursts from the mouth, and three echoes roll out over a pool of rot to the bell's reach while souls rise from it.

Later Mortem effects introduce mycelium and sparse spores. Bloom of Rot gives this contamination its fullest expression, briefly staining the ground and affected bodies. This hints at the existing life/decay dichotomy without adding a new persistent infection mechanic.

Particle settings reduce secondary accents. The main spell shapes remain visible with Minimal particles. Costs, damage, progression and weapon/Avatar silhouettes retain their existing behavior.
