# The Brined Court

The Brined Court is the sea offshoot of the Harbinger tradition: eight houses down the ocean's depth layers, each keeping a hereditary Morphling strain, between Marimo at the sunlit top and Hydra at the vents. Its sites only appear while Pelagic worldgen is on. See `docs/HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` (Structures, "The Brined Court") for the rules and `docs/brined_court/` for the build plan.

## The house strains

Each house keeps one Morphling strain, shallow to deep: Sunburst (the Giving Light), Holdfast (the Holding Stone), Mermaid's Purse (the Shared Wake), Dead Man's Rope (the Woven Current), Ink Cap (the Veiled Fold), Hushbell (the Still Bell), Marrowplume (Last Marrow) and Kinbud (the Kindling). You will likely meet them first as the Order catalogues them, from wild polyps and the Alchemist's Living Bestiary, with no house names attached. Once you know of the Court, a strain's tooltip and Bestiary entry also say which house raises it and what its partner organism is. See [[Advanced Mechanics]] for what each strain does.

## Learning of the Court

Three routes lead to the Court, in any order, and whichever comes first is the one the others acknowledge.

- **The Vicar and the Voyagers.** At the fifth Degree, the Vicar's "Who else keeps to the sea?" sends you to a Voyager (with a vessel bearing first if you haven't done The Reef Watch). A Voyager explains the Court and draws you a **Relay Chart** that points to the nearest Lighthouse Garden. Any Voyager reissues a lost chart.
- **A Brined Votary's letter.** The first Brined Votary you kill while you don't know the Court drops a **Brine-Sealed Letter**, once. Show it to a Vicar, Alchemist or Voyager. If you lose it unread, you can still tell any of them about it.
- **The Alchemist.** At the fifth Degree, with a strain at Mature or higher, the Alchemist admits the best cultivators of these lines aren't in the Order and points you to the Voyagers or the coast.

You can also simply sail into Court water: entering any Seat or outpost counts. You can visit before the fifth Degree; residents won't teach you yet.

## Your place in the Court

The rules below are in place. At the two Seats the residents now teach, take you in and accept offerings; the inner houses' residents arrive later.

- **Standing.** Each house counts you a stranger, a guest or a friend. Visiting a house's Seat or outpost once you know the Court makes you its guest; keeping its custom (food to the Garden, a promise to the Holding Stone, an offering at a vent) makes you a friend. Standing is never bought.
- **Joining a house.** At the fifth Degree, as a guest of a house, its lead or senior member can take you in. No strain is needed. You get one free entry and one free switch; after the switch your house is fixed. The house you leave still counts you a guest.
- **The first lesson.** At the fifth Degree the Lightkeeper at the Lighthouse Garden teaches that a strain grows faster when fed in its own house's water: once taught, a strain's upkeep bonds it 1.5 times as fast inside its own house's Seat or outpost.
- **Wrongs and amends** (the Bloom Warden isn't built yet). A wrong against the Giving Light is remembered, even through death, and the Garden turns you away until you hold out food as an offering to make amends.
- **The Kindled Champion** (the fight itself isn't built yet). With a strain at Apex and the Kindling's friendship, you may challenge for the Champion's title. Lose, and you wait a day before trying again.
- **Unstained visitors** are shunned by every house: no standing, no membership, no lessons. Nobody attacks them.

## The two Seats

The Court keeps two full settlements, one at each end of its gradient. Both can be visited at any degree; before Degree 5 the residents are polite but don't teach.

**The Lighthouse Garden** (the Giving Light) stands on its own tide rock on an Erythrocoral Reef shelf, the only Court site you can see from the surface. Its lighthouse burns no flame: the lamp is a living Sunburst bloom in a bone-and-glass room, gold at night. Terraced gardens step down from the tower into the reef. There are frond arbors where anchored members rest among the growth, communal tables and drying racks, a guest house where newcomers are fed and housed, and a landing on the north shore. A hatch under a frond-roofed kiosk east of the tower opens into the undercroft: a tube coliseum descending through moss-covered galleries, damp but dry, to a pool round a moss island at the bottom. The kneeling figures on its lowest walls are petrified followers of Marimo.

**The Vent Sanctum** (the Kindling) is built round a hydrothermal vent field in the Hydrothermal Depths, and is never visible from the surface. Its chimneys are left exactly as the sea floor made them; the Kindling crown each one with a pale polyp ring and flame-coloured tentacles, and keep carved offering basins at their feet. A walkway climbs from the processional ring to the mouth of the tallest head, the threshold. Two dry halls of preparation are cut into the mound's flanks: swim in through the flooded antechamber and come up through the moon pool. Beside the mound lies Hydra's body.

## The Seats' people

Residents stand where the Seats and outposts were built for them and stay close to home. They can't be hurt and never fight you. One lost to the void is replaced a day later, while nobody is near.

- **Lighthouse Garden.** Gardeners tend the terraces and the guest house: they feed you when you're hungry, let you rest, give a small welcome gift once, and take food for the table (the Garden's custom). Arbors kneel among the fronds and in the undercroft; one of them withdraws now and then. The **Lightkeeper** in the lighthouse gives the first lesson and takes members into the Giving Light at the fifth Degree.
- **Vent Sanctum.** Offering-bearers in the halls of preparation take food down to the heads (the Kindling's custom). Kindled elders, members grown into the mature Hydra form, rest in the halls' alcoves. The **Head-tender** keeps the threshold and takes members into the Kindling.
- **Tide Refuge** (the Holding Stone, the first house with residents beyond the Seats). A Tidewarden walks the passage with a sounding pole and drives off monsters that come in from the sea; it lets you rest in the shelter and takes a block of stone for the passages (the Holding Stone's custom). A Braced elder grips the arch from a notch over the west jamb; it gives a welcome gift of kelp once and takes members into the Holding Stone at the fifth Degree.
- **The Shared Wake.** Its people are a moving school: four to six Edge-swimmers, sleek and barely human, swimming a wide loop through the open ocean behind a leader, guarding the edge with bone lances and driving off monsters. You may meet one out in Pelagic Ocean (never near a Seat, and at most one school to a region), or one sets out from a Wake nursery the first time you come near it. At the rare nursery, Nursery tenders raise the young in their nest cups: they give a welcome gift of cooked salmon once, take a raw fish for the young (the Shared Wake's custom) and take members into the Shared Wake at the fifth Degree.
- **Line Camp** (the Woven Current). A Linesman tends the lines with a bone gaff and takes string for them (the custom); glowing strands tie it to the camp's other members. A Lamp-colony hangs in the lines, lets go to talk, gives two leads once and takes members into the Woven Current at the fifth Degree.
- **Screened Cleft** (the Veiled Fold). The Host at the door gives you a room to rest in, never asks your name and takes an ink sac for the screen (the custom). A Veiled resident inside folds away almost out of sight when it stands still and unfolds when you speak to it; it gives four tinted glass once and takes members into the Veiled Fold.
- **The Bell** (the Still Bell). The Bellwarden keeps the air pocket: rest on the ledge, two blocks of dried kelp once, wool for the bell's warmth (the custom), and membership of the Still Bell. Sealed Sleepers hang from the dome and don't wake.
- **Whale Fall** (Last Marrow). Marrow-tenders feed you from the fall, let you rest under the ribs and take bones or rotten flesh (the custom). A Rooted elder lives in the jaw bone with red plumes in the water; it gives bone meal once and takes members into Last Marrow.
- Every resident can tell you about its house and where the other houses live, and remarks on the strain you wear. Before the fifth Degree they feed and talk but don't teach or take you in. An Unstained visitor gets one refusal and nothing else; residents step away or close up, and never attack.

## Marimo and Hydra

You never fight Marimo or Hydra. They take no damage, have no boss bar and can't be targeted.

**Marimo** is the moss at the bottom of the undercroft: a mass over the pool's island with Marimo's Heart on its crown, threads climbing every tier's wall and petrified followers sunk into it. It can't be broken outside Creative. Coming down to it the first time plays a short scene; every arrival sends a pulse through the moss, and touching the heart with an empty hand lets the anchored speak for him.

**Hydra** is the Vent Sanctum's crowned chimneys and its body. The first time you come near the Head-tender, it calls the largest head: it rises from the tallest chimney, lowers its crown over you, and withdraws. Inside a Court site, the petrified followers, Hydra Crowns and Kindled Tissue can't be broken outside Creative.

Marimo Moss and Marimo's Heart are in the Brined Court creative section for building.

## The Bloom Warden and the Kindled Champion

The fights at the Seats are against followers in the poles' image, never the poles.

**The Bloom Warden** wakes when you wrong the Giving Light at the Lighthouse Garden: breaking its plants, beds or moss, reaching for a petrified follower or Marimo's moss in the undercroft, or hitting one of its residents. You lose your standing with the Garden until you make amends. The Warden roots among gold blooms that heal and shield it while light reaches them from above, so cut off the light or clear the blooms (they break at a touch), and hit it hard when it uproots to move. Hold any food in your main hand and it won't attack you; use the food on it to make amends, which takes one item, clears the wrong and makes you a guest again. Beaten, it kneels back into its tier and the anchored tell you what you did, but you still owe amends. If it kills you, the wrong is still recorded.

**The Kindled Champion** is a title you can choose to take. Once you know the Court, carry a strain at Apex and the Kindling count you a friend, the Head-tender at the Vent Sanctum lets you challenge for it. The Champion comes out of the Kindled elders and fights on the mound: it sweeps with its long neck, stings with its crown (Stung), grabs you with its tentacles and pulls you in, and grows health back quickly, so you have to burst it down. It grows more of its crown at two thirds and one third health, and the chimneys erupt round the mound throughout (watch for the amber rings). Win and the old Champion yields and lives, Hydra's largest head lowers its crown over you, and you hold the title (recognition only). Lose and you die, and have to wait a day before you can ask again.

## Outposts

Each of the six inner houses keeps one small outpost, and every outpost has a carved house marker at its edge naming whose it is. Every outpost has its residents now.

- **Tide Refuge** (the Holding Stone), on the rocky shore: a rock stack round a calm pocket pool, its seaward arch braced by holdfast root masses. A shelter room above the tide line has a bed and a fungal hearth, and a passage is cut right through the stack. The only outpost you can see from the surface.
- **Wake nursery** (the Shared Wake), rare, on a shallow bank of the open ocean: no buildings, just a hollow ringed by boulders and kelp, full of grown purse cups holding red egg cases.
- **Line Camp** (the Woven Current), in a Twilight gully: living lines strung wall to wall, lit by violet rope pods, with two grown pods hung from them. Sometimes the pods are joined by a strand tube, sometimes they have drifted apart. You can climb the lines.
- **Screened Cleft** (the Veiled Fold), in the Midnight depths under warmer water: a narrow cleft in a rock face behind a hanging veil, almost invisible. Inside is one receiving room; the doors to the rest of the house stay shut.
- **The Bell** (the Still Bell), in the Midnight depths under cold water: a grown diving bell on stone legs. Swim in under the rim and up through the moon pool into its air pocket, where there is a bed, food stores, and sealed sleepers hanging in the upper ring.
- **Whale Fall** (Last Marrow), in the Carrion Depths: a whole skeleton on the sediment, homes under its ribs, a hollowed skull, and rooted elders living in the jaw bones with red feeding plumes in the water.

## Signs

Smaller signs say whose water you are in without a settlement: gold Sunburst blooms floating over reef and coast (thickest round the Lighthouse Garden), a lone house marker on the seabed, relay posts at the depth boundaries trailing red current threads, messenger buoys on the open sea, and offering basins at the feet of chimneys outside the Vent Sanctum. They are scenery only, and each kind is capped per area so the sea never fills up with them.

## Light and materials

Court light is biological: Sunburst crust and blooms at the Garden, polyp glow and mineral heat at the Sanctum, rope-pod violet in the Line Camp, shroomlight and glow lichen at the deeper outposts. You won't find torches, lanterns or anything of copper, brass or bronze at a Court site.
