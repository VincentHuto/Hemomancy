# World Content

Hemomancy spreads its progression across discovery-heavy world content rather than a single straight tech tree. Custom biomes include the fungal-dimension regions **Fungal Gardens** and **Fungal Isles**, overworld infection zones like **Sporecrown Thicket**, **Hyphal Spires**, and **Drifting Mycelium**, and the ocean biome **Erythrocoral Reef**, which introduces its own deep-water palette, fauna, and structure hooks.

Exploration is also driven by structures. Early progression starts at landmarks like the **Blood Temple** and **Unstained Church**, while midgame and late-game travel can uncover **Harbinger Outposts**, **Voyager Wrecks**, **Active Voyager Vessels**, **Crimson Lodge Annexes**, **Bog-Body Ossuary Niches**, and the **Broken Church** encounter space. Endgame content expands into places like the **Qliphoth Fane** and saint-linked trial chambers.

Those spaces are populated by a wide range of creatures and rewards. The mod mixes roaming mobs, aquatic threats, bosses, and progression NPCs with discoverable reagents and relics such as **Erythrocoral Fragments**, **Chalybeate Sclerites**, **Mnemonic Ambergris**, **Puppeteering Thread**, **Sanguine Quintessence**, and unique route rewards like **Annetta's Sanguis Lancea**. The result is a world meant to be read, looted, and interpreted as much as fought through.
