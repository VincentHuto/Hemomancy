# Crimson Troupe

The Crimson Troupe is the main Puppeteering school, adjacent to the Brined Court. Find its teaching camps or travelling pavilion, prepare a charged, owner-attuned Marionette Crossbar, and speak to the professors. Instruction reveals an ordeal recipe. Victory in that existing Cardinal Ordeal unlocks the shape; a practical demonstrates mastery.

The Ringmaster and every professor also have a Trade box in their dialogue. Each professor takes one kind of goods that suits their act. Only the Ringmaster takes curiosities (shells, horns, heads, odd specimens, graded by rarity), and he also takes a spectacle you've earned by finishing a pavilion act. You can bank at most four spectacles ever: four if you choose Succession, one if you choose Liberation. Trading needs no Degree and no active blood.

A Threadkeeper inspects the Crossbar for **An Empty Ring** and records your reports. Use that same instrument to call, command and recall a known body. Each professor offers a practical using your own puppets and existing controls. Practicals spawn two real rehearsal threats and expire after five minutes or when you leave the professor. Return to retry.

The Side ledger lists the next action for your apprenticeship, **The Wrong Audience**, and **The Missing Understudy**. Passed practicals can become guest acts paying four Enthralling Filaments once per Minecraft day for the whole school, not once per professor. A full inventory reserves the exact remaining payment; collect it from a Threadkeeper later.

The Fire Breather's Cinder Bellows combines a serpentine coil with a gaunt upper body and exposed lung-sac. Its Degree 4 frame ordeal costs 900 mL. It inhales, breathes a short five-block cone and recovers, respecting walls and allies without igniting terrain. The Juggler has a floating crimson robe, six arms and three masks. It retains the older `marrow_spitter` identity in saves and recipes.

The Bellows' coil curls behind a rising, scaled trunk, with a free tail tip that lifts and glows. Ribs wrap two pleated bellows lobes, and a crimson ruff, a wick torch and a charred skull with a hinged jaw complete the Fire Breather's puppet. It rears back to draw breath, then lunges forward and pumps its arms as it breathes; its eyes, throat, lung folds and coil cracks glow brighter through the breath.

The Juggler is a floating harlequin puppet in a diamond-pattern doublet, with a pleated ruff and a two-pointed belled hood. Its laughing, wailing and blank masks ring the hood. Six bone arms in puffed sleeves and hematic iron cuffs catch three knives in staggered arcs, and tattered robe panels and a striped sash sway beneath it.

The Strongman's Hulk is a hunched, gorilla-like stitched brute: a barrel chest and trapezius hump behind a small carved skull with temple bolts and an iron moustache. Oversized forearms end in iron wrist bands and huge bone-knuckled fists that rise before its strike. Striped circus trunks, a wide lifting belt and iron-capped boots complete the body; its knees and ankles articulate when it walks.

The other Troupe bodies follow the same style. The Mummer is a narrow porcelain Pierrot with a one-eyed painted face, whose eyespotted frill fans open when it performs. The Hound is a slender, Set-like flayed sighthound with tall ears, a curved bridled snout, stitched rupture seams and a forked tail; its Curs share the body in brighter blood. The Vulture is a hunched bald vulture on bone-and-hematic-iron wing spars, trailing veined feathers and a needle tail. The Mnemonist is a tall, starved bisque doll with a tilted, cracked face, with three to five living red threads writhing out of a spindle turning on its back. They trail as it walks, lash over its head when it strikes and go slack as it dies. The Ringmaster Pattern is an empty crimson tailcoat under a top hat, with only two gold eyes inside.

The faculty dress for their trades. The Threadkeeper is a lean clerk with an eyeshade, spectacles, a ledger and a thread spindle that spins while your Crossbar is inspected. The Retired Understudy is stooped, faded harlequin and shawled, leaning on a cane. Their old half-mask rests on the brow until an act brings it down. The Strongman presses a barbell overhead in a striped one-strap leotard. The Beast Tamer wears a frogged crimson coat and fur mantle, and uncoils a whip from the belt to perform.

Every performer works its puppets on visible blood-red threads from the right hand, just as you do with a Crossbar. This applies to the professor's demonstration body and to the dolls a performer raises when attacked.

Teaching camps have peaked red-and-ivory canvas tents opening onto a practice yard. Small wooden decks, worn paths, lantern posts and benches surround the courts, with props for each professor's specialty. The Threadkeeper's Spindle stays inside the left tent; the right tent holds sleeping quarters, with books and records at the Understudy's camp.

Normal Scarlet Waybill use points to the next eligible professor whose instruction you lack. Sneak-use searches for an unfinished pavilion. The Waybill says it is searching and marks the target a moment later, without stalling the server.

In the pavilion, acclimation grows while you stay inside, and in one-time steps: talking to each performer for the first time, An Empty Ring, each practical, each investigation, the attention act, choosing a route and each later act. As it grows the pavilion looks calmer and more coherent. That is all it changes: the galleries and canopy the Troupe's lore describes are not solid, and the Ringmaster's perch has to be reached by building up.

Apprenticeship adds no Harbinger Main or finale requirement, and neither ending checks your Degree. Succession runs the remaining acts, needs full acclimation, downs the troupe without killing it, leaves the pavilion standing and grants the Ringmaster Pattern. Liberation needs no further acts. Right-click each of the three captives on the carousel to cut its thread; the cut kills the captive and exposes an anchor. Break the three anchors to ruin the carousel, then kill the Ringmaster. The missing performers are not rescued alive by cutting their strings. Liberation's reward is the Thread Ripper memory, a Summa manipulation you may not be able to learn yet, so it can arrive as the memory item. Both endings give the Ringmaster's Topper, held for you if your pack is full. Other camps keep teaching after either outcome.
