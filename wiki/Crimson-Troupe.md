# Crimson Troupe

The Crimson Troupe is the main Puppeteering school, adjacent to the Brined Court. Find its teaching camps or travelling pavilion, prepare a charged, owner-attuned Marionette Crossbar, and speak to the professors. Instruction reveals an ordeal recipe. Victory in that existing Cardinal Ordeal unlocks the shape; a practical demonstrates mastery.

The Ringmaster and every professor also have a Trade box in their dialogue. Each professor takes one kind of goods that suits their act. Only the Ringmaster takes curiosities (shells, horns, heads, odd specimens, graded by rarity), and he also takes a spectacle you've earned by finishing a pavilion act. You can bank at most four spectacles ever: four if you choose Succession, one if you choose Liberation. Trading needs no Degree and no active blood.

A Threadkeeper inspects the Crossbar for **An Empty Ring** and records your reports. Use that same instrument to call, command and recall a known body. Each professor offers a practical using your own puppets and existing controls. Practicals spawn two real rehearsal threats and expire after five minutes or when you leave the professor. A practical ends early if a threat it needs dies (the Mummer and Bellows need both, the rest one), and none can be taken in Peaceful. Return to retry. Ask the Threadkeeper what the first entry on your account is to read the An Empty Ring briefing.

The Side ledger lists the next action for your apprenticeship, **The Wrong Audience**, and **The Missing Understudy**. Passed practicals can become guest acts paying four Enthralling Filaments once per Minecraft day for the whole school, not once per professor. A full inventory reserves the exact remaining payment; collect it from a Threadkeeper later.

The Fire Breather's Cinder Bellows combines a serpentine coil with a gaunt upper body and exposed lung-sac. Its Degree 4 frame ordeal costs 900 mL. It inhales, breathes a short five-block cone and recovers, respecting walls and allies without igniting terrain. The Juggler has a floating crimson robe, six arms and three masks. It retains the older `marrow_spitter` identity in saves and recipes.

The Bellows' coil curls behind a rising, scaled trunk, with a free tail tip that lifts and glows. Ribs wrap two pleated bellows lobes, and a crimson ruff, a wick torch and a charred skull with a hinged jaw complete the Fire Breather's puppet. It rears back to draw breath, then lunges forward and pumps its arms as it breathes; its eyes, throat, lung folds and coil cracks glow brighter through the breath.

The Juggler is a floating harlequin puppet in a diamond-pattern doublet, with a pleated ruff and a two-pointed belled hood. Its laughing, wailing and blank masks ring the hood. Six bone arms in puffed sleeves and hematic iron cuffs catch three knives in staggered arcs, and tattered robe panels and a striped sash sway beneath it.

The Strongman's Hulk is a hunched, gorilla-like stitched brute: a barrel chest and trapezius hump behind a small carved skull with temple bolts and an iron moustache. Oversized forearms end in iron wrist bands and huge bone-knuckled fists that rise before its strike. Striped circus trunks, a wide lifting belt and iron-capped boots complete the body; its knees and ankles articulate when it walks.

The other Troupe bodies follow the same style. The Mummer is a narrow porcelain Pierrot with a one-eyed painted face, whose eyespotted frill fans open when it performs. The Hound is a slender, Set-like flayed sighthound with tall ears, a curved bridled snout, stitched rupture seams and a forked tail; its Curs share the body in brighter blood. The Vulture is a hunched bald vulture on bone-and-hematic-iron wing spars, trailing veined feathers and a needle tail. The Mnemonist is a tall, starved bisque doll with a tilted, cracked face, with three to five living red threads writhing out of a spindle turning on its back. They trail as it walks, lash over its head when it strikes and go slack as it dies. The Ringmaster Pattern is an empty crimson tailcoat under a top hat, with only two gold eyes inside.

The faculty dress for their trades. The Threadkeeper is a lean clerk with an eyeshade, spectacles, a ledger and a thread spindle that spins while your Crossbar is inspected. The Retired Understudy is stooped, faded harlequin and shawled, leaning on a cane. Their old half-mask rests on the brow until an act brings it down. The Strongman presses a barbell overhead in a striped one-strap leotard. The Beast Tamer wears a frogged crimson coat and fur mantle, and uncoils a whip from the belt to perform.

Every performer works its puppets on visible blood-red threads from the right hand, just as you do with a Crossbar. This applies to the professor's demonstration body and to the dolls a performer raises when attacked.

Teaching camps are sawdust yards ringed by a low earth bank, open to the south under a pennant line. The professors stand in a practice ring in the middle. The Threadkeeper keeps the Puppeteer's Spindle and a crafting table under a canvas lean-to, a painted wagon stands beside the yard, and lamp posts light the corners. Each camp is built round its discipline's apparatus: a rigging tower and net for the Acrobat, a knife wheel and target boards for the Dagger Thrower, a stilt rack and weight frames for the Stilt Walker and the Strongman, an ember pit, bellows and hound hoops for the Fire Eater and the Beast Tamer, and a prompt-book table with the quarters ledger for the Understudy. The abandoned stage is a burnt stage wagon by the road, its boards run out as a broken stage with the stage bill still on them.

The Circus keeps no chests. The first time you pass a professor's practical, they give you two Enthralling Filaments and one material for the frame of the body they taught. If your pack is full or you die first, the gift waits for you. After a Liberation the pavilion is a ruin: the canvas is torn open, the ring splintered and the trapezes down, and the performers leave, though the faculty stay and still teach. After a Succession your banner hangs behind the Ringmaster's podium, and he names you to other visitors.

The Troupe has its own set pieces: king and quarter poles, ring curbs round a sawdust ring, seating tiers, gallery rails, trapezes over a safety net that softens a fall, crimson footlights, painted wagons on two-block wheels, trunks that never open, a ticket window, and one apparatus for each camp (a knife wheel, a stilt rack, an ember pit, a hound hoop, a prompt-book table). The clue records are now things you can tell apart: a stage bill, a loft rigging log, a quarters ledger and a carousel maker's plate. The pavilion is built from them: a striped big top with a sawdust ring, two king poles, seating, rigging towers, a lower gallery (the loft) and an upper gallery with the Ringmaster's box, a midway out front and wagons, a cookhouse and a dressing tent behind. The rebuilt camps are still to come. The pieces can also be placed from the Creative tab, and none of them drops anything when broken.

At the third Degree the Mnemonist tells you the Circus exists and that its camps keep to open country, but writes no Waybill yet. Normal Scarlet Waybill use points to an abandoned stage while the Retired Understudy's lead is open and its stage record unread, and otherwise to the next eligible professor whose instruction you lack. Sneak-use searches for an unfinished pavilion, passing by any whose Ringmaster is gone. The Waybill says it is searching and marks the target a moment later, without stalling the server.

In the pavilion, acclimation grows while you stay inside, and in one-time steps: talking to each performer for the first time, An Empty Ring, each practical, each investigation, the attention act, choosing a route and each later act. As it grows the pavilion looks calmer and more coherent: shapes in the galleries, cloth on the rails and lights in the rigging, few and restless at first, settled once you are attuned. That is all it changes. The galleries are real for everyone, and stairs at both ends of the upper gallery lead to the Ringmaster's box, where you can speak to him.

Apprenticeship adds no Harbinger Main requirement. The Ringmaster's bargain does: you need the fourth Degree and one school report (An Empty Ring, The Wrong Audience or The Missing Understudy), and he checks again when you ask for the finale. If your blood has been washed out by the Unstained, he offers only Liberation, and asks The Missing Understudy report instead of a Degree. Succession runs the remaining acts, needs full acclimation, downs the troupe without killing it, leaves the pavilion standing and grants the Ringmaster Pattern. Liberation needs no further acts. Right-click each of the three captives on the carousel to cut its thread; the cut kills the captive and exposes an anchor. Break the three anchors to ruin the carousel, then kill the Ringmaster. The missing performers are not rescued alive by cutting their strings. Liberation's reward is the Thread Ripper memory, a Summa manipulation you may not be able to learn yet, so it can arrive as the memory item. Both endings give the Ringmaster's Topper, held for you if your pack is full. Other camps keep teaching after either outcome. Your puppets' blows count as yours in the acts and the finale, and the troupe's attacks spare anyone else nearby; the finale is still yours alone. Thread Ripper cannot cut Vesper's puppets.

The finale opens with the Ringmaster in his box. For the first few seconds nothing moves; then the knives start. He fights with the Circus's own tricks. The Spotlight lights a pool that follows you: look away from him while you stand in it and it holds you in place. On Sleight he and two doubles stand round you and trade places; only the real one strikes, and only he bleeds, so finding him breaks the trick. On the Cue every performer still standing strikes on the same beat. The Knife Board rings you with knives; dash out before they close. Once he comes down, each performer you left lying in the ring lends him its act once: a vault, a fan of knives, a breath of fire or a stilt slam. If you climb where he cannot follow, he throws. Stay in the ring: wander too far and the finale ends without you. When you strike his last blow on Succession he kneels, takes the topper off his staff and hands you the ring; the Pattern is yours when he has finished. Then he climbs back to his box and still trades and teaches. On Liberation the threads let go of him one by one before the pavilion falls to ruin.

Each taught body also has one act you fire from the Act entry on the Crossbar's radial: the Mummer's Spotlight pulls threats onto itself, the Vulture's Trapeze swaps your place with its own, the Juggler's Feint draws swings and pins one, the Bellows' Smoke hides you, the Mnemonist's Encore replays its wound now, the Hound's Scent marks threats through walls and the Hulk's Lift throws you up and forward. Each costs a little thread and has a cooldown. The Ringmaster Pattern's Ring makes every body within 32 blocks perform its act at once. The Juggler's third dagger in each volley pins its target for a moment.
