# Deep Dark recording script

The three cylinders already have voiced audio. The in-game captions below have been rewritten, but the OGG tracks still speak the old lines. Record new audio against these captions before shipping them together.

## Severed Record

Unknown Recorder: Lower district. Record nineteen.

Unknown Recorder: The growth to the west has stopped spreading.

Distant Voice: Is it dead?

Unknown Recorder: No. It's still alive.

Unknown Recorder: Catalysts still take the dead. Listeners still react when we move.

Unknown Recorder: But they barely answer each other now.

Unknown Recorder: Hold still.

Unknown Recorder: It's still holding.

Distant Voice: Then why is it still alive?

Unknown Recorder: I don't know.

Unknown Recorder: We broke the connection between them.

Unknown Recorder: The growth survived anyway.

## Lower District Survey

Unknown Recorder: Lower district survey. The growth feeds on the dead. The listeners react when we move, but won't cross the black floor.

Distant Voice: Don't bring any of it upstairs. Keep the dead away. Mark the last clean stair.

## The Quieting

Unknown Recorder: No bells below the third stair. No iron wheels. Cover the road with wool. Walk softly.

Distant Voice: If the floor answers twice, leave. If it answers three times, don't go back for those below.
