# Mycophant (Spawn Mother) Rebuild Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the old Uzouthrhix-based Mycophant with the locked Spawn Mother: a generated model, procedural animation, and a controller-driven Nursery fight (the waking opening, a claim phase, the 50% split, a joining phase, and the yielding ending).

**Architecture:** Pure rules classes own every number and timeline, and they are JVM-tested. One `MycophantController` on the entity owns the drag locomotion, facing and the move timeline, following the `VesperMountController` pattern. The state is synced through `SynchedEntityData`. The client model is generated by `tools/model_export/mycophant.py`, with the hand-written `mycophant_anim.java` sampling a `Drive` from that synced state (the Latent-infection pattern). The opening's screen effects run in a packet-started client state.

**Tech Stack:** NeoForge 1.21.1 (Java 21), the HutosLib effect suite, Python 3 and Pillow for the generator (`annetta_kit`), JUnit 5.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-mycophant-spawn-mother-design.md`. The locked geometry reference is `.superpowers/brainstorm/33952-1792299911/content/spawn-mother-v8.html`.

## Global Constraints

- Mod id `hemomancy`, package `com.vincenthuto.hemomancy`. Use `net.neoforged.*` only, plus `DeferredHolder` and `Hemomancy.rloc(...)`.
- Networking: `CustomPacketPayload` with a static `TYPE`, a `STREAM_CODEC`, and `playToClient` registration in `PacketHandler`.
- Owned-boss stats stay at 720 HP, 20 armor and 16 attack. The phase-2 threshold stays at ≤ 50% health (`MycophantCombatRules.phase`).
- Model: generated only. Never hand-edit `MycophantModel.java`. The renderer scale is 2×, `TEXEL_SCALE = 2`, and blockout dimensions are halved in model units. Big forms only: surface detail is painted.
- Visuals: no vanilla `ParticleTypes` in any Mycophant path. Use `HemoParticleData`, HutosLib tendrils, `PacketHandler.sendClawSlash` and client geometry.
- No `setNoAi` on the Mycophant, ever.
- A filled claim or fusion meter never kills: the player keeps at least 4.0 health from that damage.
- Damage: ordinary damage lands at full rate in both phases. A broken fusion adds a bonus of 8% of max health.
- A cast breaks a hold or the Wing Guard only if it hits her within the cast window, or (for holds) if it is cast while the player is held. One instant break per hold.
- Contracts that must stay green: `EndgameBossRewardResourceTest`, `ChamberThemeCommandSourceTest`, the Orb of Perspective tests, `EntityLootTableCoverageResourceTest`, and GameTest `mycophantTendrilWaitsForInventorySpaceAcrossDeath`.
- Leave the work uncommitted unless the user asks (memory: no auto-commit). The "Commit" steps below mean "checkpoint": run the tests and leave the tree green.
- Pronouns: "she/her" in player-facing text and docs. Lore text never implies a sex.

## Review Focus

1. **A player who logs out or dies while claimed, siphoned or fused.** The hold must release, no player stays pinned after relog, and failure stays the nonlethal `failAttempt`. Test: claim state clears in `onDeath`/logout and on reload (Task 4).
2. **An old save loading a mid-fight Mycophant from before this change** (no synced fields in NBT). Expect IDLE, phase from health, split complete if ≤ 50%, and no claim held. Test: `readAdditionalSaveData` with the old keys only (Task 4).
3. **Her health crossing 50% during an active hold or mid-move.** The split queues to the next recovery boundary, the hold releases first, and the split plays exactly once. Test: `MycophantControllerRulesTest.splitQueuesUntilRecovery` (Task 1) plus the entity flag test (Task 4).
4. **Brood funglings outliving the fight** after victory, failure or a rematch. All brood funglings are discarded on `clear()`. Test: the source contract that `MycophantEncounterManager.clear` discards brood (Task 4).
5. **Lethal stacking:** a filled meter, an engulfing drown tick and an ordinary hit landing in the same second. The meter damage clamp leaves ≥ 4 health, while ordinary hits can still kill (into `failAttempt`). Test: `MycophantClaimRulesTest.filledMeterDamageNeverLethal` (Task 2).

   - Ordinary owner hits deal full damage and count toward `hitsToBreak`.

   - Siphon: on latch, set SIPHON_TARGET_ID.

     - It reels the player at `siphonReelPerTick`.
     - It breaks when the distance exceeds `siphonRange` + 2, when there is no line of sight, or when she takes ≥ 6 damage from the owner while latched.
   - Brood call: spawn 2 brood funglings (3 in phase 2, cap 4 alive) at the petal-vent world positions.
