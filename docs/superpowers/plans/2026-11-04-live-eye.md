# Live Eye Implementation Plan

> Executed inline (owner rule: native execution, one final reviewer). No commits unless asked.

**Goal:** the husk's open eye shows a live, parallax-correct stand-in of the owner's Chamber, with the blocks they built.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-04-live-eye-design.md`

## Tasks

1. **Snapshot (server, pure + GameTest).** `common/encounter/ChamberProxySnapshot` (record, packing, NBT, `take(manager, server, owner)` scanning the room); the husk stores and syncs it; taken on wake, brief open and link. Tests: packing round trip; a room with a placed block snapshots that block and the floor, excludes air and the gate, is capped; no Chamber level gives an empty snapshot.
2. **Camera rules (pure).** `client/render/world/chamberofwill/ChamberProxyRules`: proxy position and yaw from facing, husk anchor, gate position and the viewer's camera. Tests: front of a south-facing husk maps to south of the gate looking north; east-facing turns by 270.
3. **The pass.** `ChamberProxyView`: requests from the husk renderer, one render at AFTER_SKY into a half-size target with depth, baked block buffer per snapshot, ribs, gate eye, arbor stand-in; full state save and restore; texture id lookup. Wired in `WorldEffectDispatcher`.
4. **The eye samples it.** `lidless_door_panel` gains `Sampler0`, `ScreenSize`, `Live`; the portal render type binds the texture; `LidlessPortalPanel.draw` takes the texture id; the husk renderer passes it. `LidlessShaderContractTest` extended; wiring test for the dispatcher and the request.
5. **Review and docs.** Harness: place a block in the Chamber during the visit, reopen the eye after the return, capture front and angle. Reference docs, ledger, memory. `alphaCheck`, final reviewer, one fix pass.
