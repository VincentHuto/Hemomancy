# Book models: design

Approved in chat on 2026-11-01 from a concept sheet of four front covers.

## Intent

Every openable Hemomancy book is drawn with vanilla's enchanting-table `BookModel` and a 64x32 recolour, through
HutosLib's `RenderItemGuideBook`. The four books should each have their own model that fits its owner and lore. They
should still open, close and flip pages the way they do now.

## The books (user-approved look)

| Book | Owner | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Liber Sanguinium | Harbinger field guide | Vellum pages in a deep crimson leather binding. Iron corner caps, iron filigree frame with scrolled corners, raised iron spine bands, a central iron rosette holding a blood-glass drop, and an iron clasp. Chapter tabs on the fore-edge (matching the reader's tabs) and a crimson ribbon. The user specified this one. |
| Liber Immaculatus | Unstained guide | White vellum binding with patinated copper bands, corners and clasp. Pale silver rules on the cover and silvered page edges. A copper crescent for the Lady over blind-tooled still-water ripples. Slate-blue ribbon. |
| Book of Observances | Unstained observance register | A girdle book. Slate-teal leather runs past the foot into a tail, knotted round a patinated copper ring. Copper-bound edges, three copper studs and three ribbons, one each for the Acolyte, Zealot and Guardian. |
| Harbinger Assignment Ledger | A Vicar's ledger | Tall, narrow account book in near-black oxblood leather. Iron hinge plates on the spine, a buckled strap, an iron-framed vellum index card on the front, and loose waybills sticking out of the head, one sealed in red wax. |

## Goals

- One authored model per book, generated by a version-controlled script in `tools/model_export/` (Java layer
  definitions plus painted box-UV atlases), like the other model generators.
- The same animation inputs as now: HutosLib's per-player `BookAnimState` (open amount, flip, page angle) drives the
  open/close and the two flipping leaves.
- 32 texture pixels per block: the user asked (2026-11-01) for more detail than the 16 px rule allows on these
  focal items. The generator builds every mesh at twice block size, one texel per model unit, and the renderers draw it
  at `HemoBookLayers.MODEL_SCALE` (0.5), so in-game size is unchanged.
- Palette rules: no brass, bronze or copper on the Harbinger books (iron, bone, lacquer); copper and pale silver belong to
  the Unstained books; no blood red on the Unstained books.

## Non-goals

- The flat-sprite items (Unsigned/Signed Ancestral Ledger, Scout Field Notes) stay as they are; they do not open.
- No change to the reader screens, book data, or HutosLib's animation tick.

## Decisions left to the builder (Claude's calls)

- **Where the books are drawn.** The item renderer, the Enzymatic Scriptorium's desk book, and the books placed on the
  Dictation Table and Harbinger Escritoire all switch to the new models. Before this, all four drew the vanilla model.
  A Dictation Table keeps its pending-dictation glow. The Dictation Table keeps HutosLib's 45-degree lectern pose; the
  Harbinger Escritoire lays the book open almost flat on its writing surface, spine running front to back with the
  head toward the pigeonholes, because the lectern pose cut into the desk.
- **One shared chassis.** Every book uses the same named parts (`left_lid`, `right_lid`, `spine`, `left_pages`,
  `right_pages`, `flip_page1`, `flip_page2`) so one hand-written `HemoBookModel` animates them all. The boards and their
  text blocks turn on the spine line. The spine's back slides back and narrows as they open, so it always spans the
  two boards' hinge corners. The lids' children `left_closed` / `right_closed` show only while the book is shut, and
  `left_open` / `right_open` only while it is open (clasp straps and the ledger's strap).
- **Textures.** The new atlases live under `textures/entity/book/`. The old vanilla-layout textures are deleted (the mod
  is not live) and each item's HutosLib texture points at its new atlas.
- **Sizes.** Liber Sanguinium and Liber Immaculatus have 7x10 covers, the Book of Observances 5x8 plus its tail, the
  ledger 5x11, in block pixels. They draw at their block size (32 px per block) on the Dictation Table and Escritoire. A shut book
  in a slot, frame or on the ground is fitted like an icon: its longest side fills 15, 16 or 8 px. In the hand it draws
  at 0.6 of that, close to vanilla's held-book size; the Scriptorium desk keeps its 0.8.
- **Held pose.** Same pose as HutosLib's guide-book renderer, turned so the head is up, with the first-person display
  transform raised slightly so more of the open book shows.
