# Resonant Forge design

The Resonant Forge is the Artificer's Degree 3 machine. Its three-wide workshop combines a treadle hammer, a blood basin, and a grindstone-driven wax-cylinder carver. It records enchantment vibration patterns onto blank ambergris cylinders and hammers those patterns into other compatible equipment.

The base machine records and removes a complete enchantment pattern. The Degree 5 Precision Governor upgrade permits one enchantment or Scriptorium curse to be selected independently. The Degree 7 Master Cam upgrade can stabilize recorded cylinders for repeated playback. Ordinary cylinders are consumed when applied; master cylinders remain.

Extraction and playback preserve exact recorded levels, merge by the higher existing or recorded level, reject incompatible or unchanged results, and treat a Scriptorium curse and severity as an independent pattern. The machine holds 5,000 blood. Extraction costs 100 plus 25 per recorded load, application costs 200 plus 50 per load, and master stabilization adds 1,000 blood.

The hammer needs rebuilding after 64 applications. The player deposits one Hematic Iron Block and one Smouldering Ash at the hammer, then projects 500 blood into that side; partial blood persists. The wheel needs redressing after 32 extraction or stabilization cycles with Befouling Ash and eight durability from a Vivianite Scalpel. A worn hammer locks the interface. A worn wheel blocks extraction and stabilization while leaving playback available.

The Precision Governor and Master Cam are delivered by the Artificer at Degrees 5 and 7. The Rite of the True Groove uses two 250-blood projection stages. The Rite of the Enduring Pattern uses three. Both use the Cardinal Rite escrow and recovery flow and have no combat or random failure.
