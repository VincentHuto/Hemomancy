# Player journey and lore contract

[Implementation index](../HARBINGER_PROGRESSION_REDESIGN.md)

## Before Degree 1

Preserve the Hermit's permission, Mortal Display, and Charm of Vascularium sequence. The outpost Waybill leads to the Vicar. Formal reception grants D1, the core Absorption/Projection manipulations, and the existing initiation supplies and Conduit at their established completion point.

A qualified D5+ founder with an actual founded bloodline may receive a player through the existing ceremony. Mere degree is insufficient. Interruption grants neither rank nor membership. Do not attach the recruit to a conflicting bloodline or recreate obsolete early Cardinal rank rites.

## Degree 1: Neophyte of the Crimson Veil

The Vicar explains how to take in blood and deliberately project it. The player demonstrates absorption, forms Sanguine Formation and Venous Stone, and builds a first useful blood structure. The Vicar teaches the Liber; the Artificer demonstrates Hematic Iron. Either eligible structure still satisfies the existing fourth proof.

The Alchemist introduces First Draws as a Side assignment: five successfully collected animal samples, representing at least three common species. They issue five empty vials and demonstrate direct sampling. Keep the filled samples available for later centrifuging. Explain the continuing Red Taxonomy/Living Bestiary work as Catalogue activity, with supported specimens and flora handled appropriately.

Do not require microscopy, injections, a syringe, rare species, a biome hunt, or complete collections for promotion. Bloodwood can be mentioned when encountered; its formal lesson belongs to D2.

Main completion: return First Bloodcraft to the Vicar for D2.

## Degree 2: Votary of the Hematic Covenant

The Alchemist gives those samples a purpose. The player learns to identify them, build/use the centrifuge, perform the assigned separation, recover its enzyme, and claim the separation result. They then complete and personally collect a basic Alembic distillation. The normal syringe/rack reward remains available; advanced sampling practice comes later.

Explain bloodwood by projecting into a Dead Bush. Do not advertise arbitrary sapling conversion.

The Artificer teaches the Hematic Armature and first Hematic Iron piece, then the Living Staff. Completing a full armor set is a Vocation project, not the price of D3. The Mnemonist offers the first directed choice of a crude combat/utility memory and explains using it. Early memories found or learned elsewhere remain valid.

The Vicar can suggest an optional survey of the Overworld Fungal Gardens. The Alchemist asks for observable specimens, not proof of the Order's cosmic origin. The player may encounter the ecosystem without understanding what it implies.

Main completion: claimed First Separation and recovered distillation allow Concentrated Blood; the player injects it directly and completes sleep to reach D3. The first gourd is still ahead at D3.

## Degree 3: Initiate of the Incarnadine Fane

The Mnemonist teaches the blank Hematic Memory, one authored weave, and basic Reliquary management. The blank's Neurotic Enzyme comes from already accessible centrifuging. The existing Woven Vessel supplies support the first Blood Shot recipe without requiring cultivated spores or a whale-derived material.

The Alchemist explains Thelemic preparations and how they differ from Noetic memories, then teaches the syringe's handling of restricted specimens. Introduce the existing Condenser upgrade as a Vocation branch after its real requirements are met.

The Artificer offers one biological armor direction: Barbed, Chitinite, or Prismatic. Teach why a choice suits the player's observations and playstyle; do not demand all three.

The Vicar develops the rite vocabulary already introduced with the staff. Teach Scarlet Vanity and Pallid Vessel, yielding the first gourd. Refer the player to the Voyagers in the Erythrocoral Reef as an optional expedition.

The Mnemonist introduces the Scriptorium as equipment inscription, distinct from the Loom's learned memories. Recording through the Clairaudiograph is a related optional branch, using ordinary wax. A deliberately started guided Chamber glimpse ends in a safe forced return. If the player has already dreamed, frame this as understanding a familiar place.

Main completion: personally complete the first deliberate weave; the corresponding rank rite admits the player to D4.

## Degree 4: Adept of the Sanguine Brotherhood

The Vicar sends the player to Masons Respite. The Cicatrix Anchorite teaches an existing low-tier tendency-appropriate scar, burning it into memory, preparing an Effigy loadout, and committing that loadout. This is the degree's defining work.

The Mnemonist deepens Loom and Reliquary practice and gives the full Somnolent Seat lesson. Intentional visits succeed when their normal conditions are met; the player is learning to remain and return, not gambling for access.

The Alchemist teaches larger existing preparations, Mycelial Lantern cultivation, and supported Morphling handling/jarring. The player learns care before D5 incubation. The Artificer teaches the full basic Resonant Forge cycle, relating its recorded equipment patterns to the Mnemonist's work.

The Vicar teaches Crimson Vessel. The Circus is introduced as an optional specialist Harbinger sect. The Mnemonist supplies the practical puppetry referral; ordinary early puppet-learning routes remain available outside the Circus.

Main completion: the Vein-Mason scar/effigy cycle opens the Illuminatus rite.

## Degree 5: Illuminatus of the Crimson Lodge

The player is now fit to keep a Fane and receive others. Their formal degree is Illuminatus; they can exercise responsibilities associated with a Vicar. Most ordinary practitioners reach no higher.

The Vicar teaches Bloodline Founding, the separate Founding Fane ceremony, recruitment, and founding duties. The site becomes a practical community, not a mandatory city-building quota.

The Alchemist teaches incubation and the existing higher Alembic work, and formally commissions the optional Deep Dark and Phlegethontic research. Previous discoveries count. Nether access and an earlier Antecedent inquiry are not reset or prohibited.

The Artificer grants the Armature Consecration Kit, teaches Blood Lust armor/masks and the relationship to earlier biological armor, and offers the existing Precision Forge upgrade. The Mnemonist teaches the Dendritic Distributor and Eightfold Script upgrade. The Anchorite offers further scar practice rather than a first introduction. The Vicar teaches Ashen Vessel.

Chamber practice is longer and better understood, but independent rite attunement still belongs to D6. A recruited Mnemonist remains helpful without becoming a new mandatory teleport service.

Main completion: a founded bloodline and its first usable Fane certify A Covenant Written in Place and open the Sanctified rite.

## Degree 6: Sanctified of the Bloodline Covenant

The teachers become collaborators whose expertise has limits. The Mnemonist teaches independent Chamber access through its existing rite. The Vicar teaches the Covenant Throne and a Vigil whose assigned helper must survive; recruitment must provide a solo player's legitimate helper route.

The Mnemonist and Alchemist commission one optional Vagrant Mind expedition for different reasons. One studies organized memory outside a familiar body; the other studies the Choir Keeper, organisms, and chorus ecology. Shared discoveries can support both reports without forcing duplicate expeditions.

Explain existing memory skills and remembered patterns through the findings. Do not promise new capacity increases, currency, or a new Forge tier without implemented reward rules. The Artificer can refine existing Precision practice and explain coming Masterwork requirements. The Vicar teaches Horn of Culmination.

Main completion: Chamber return, bound Throne, and successful Vigil certify The Living Covenant and open the Archon rite.

## Degree 7: Archon of the Hematic Order

The Vicar gives the final doctrinal disclosure. The Mnemonist interprets the Chamber and introduces Monolithic Script. The Artificer teaches Masterwork and the final equipment framework. The Alchemist describes observable changes without presenting speculation as complete knowledge.

The player shatters the Monolith, receives its seed, grows the Bloom, consumes nine Pomes from one Bloom, receives the Spine, and experiences the first temporary projection. The projection returns the player before the final response becomes available.

Silence requires the owned Bloom's severance and the two-phase Vesper ordeal. Only its verified completion produces Silent Archon, still D7. Apotheosis is the alternative route to D8. Preserve retryability, ownership, and branch-specific rewards.

## Degree 8: Apotheos

Ordinary instruction runs out; relationships do not disappear. NPCs can acknowledge, assist, fear, or fail to understand the player while ordinary services and unfinished valid projects remain usable.

Continue the existing Apotheos/Mycophant consequences. Neither the Entity nor the Pale Lady is a God/Satan analogue, and neither final response is a clean good/evil ending.

## Lore boundaries

| Stage | Appropriate knowledge |
|---|---|
| D1-D2 | Practical bloodcraft, inheritance, individual tendencies, observable unusual organisms. No settled explanation of the Entity's reproductive purpose. |
| D3-D4 | Shaped memory, bodily routes, communal practice, clues and qualified interpretations. Chamber belongs to the individual's blood-memory. |
| D5 | Crimson Lodge's concealed research and the link between blood and mycelium. Founding brings responsibility, not omniscience. |
| D6 | Covenant infrastructure and evidence beyond common teachers' experience. Deep Dark claims remain limited to what the inquiry supports. |
| D7-D8 | Final revelation and the consequences of refusal or fruiting; preserve the actual timing of recorded discoveries and choices. |

The Chamber, Overworld Fungal Gardens biome, and fungal projection dimension are three distinct contexts. Use eight tendencies consistently. Vicar is a role. The Circus is a specialist sect, not a separate degree ladder or mandatory hostile faction. Saints/Canon acquisition and Drudges remain deferred unless separately authorized.
