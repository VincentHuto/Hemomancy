# Saint Mausoleums

Status (2026-11-06): pass 1 of the legacy-dungeon rework is committed (`2ff58cc50` and the fix commits through `62f5b38da`); the four complexes generate, every Chapel kind runs and the ring door opens. The per-Saint combat and visual overhauls (D-2) were built on 2026-11-05 and 2026-11-06 on one shared Saint combat kit and are uncommitted: all four fights pass their GameTests and their fight review clients, but none has been played. D-1 was answered on 2026-11-06: the Canon Memories need complete reworks. A design draft for the four is in [the canon memory rework spec](superpowers/specs/2026-11-06-canon-memory-rework-design.md), awaiting review; nothing of it is built.

## What it is

Four Saints rest in four buried complexes, one each: Hemorath (The Hollow Vessel), Seraphae (The Chain Saint), Putriciel (The Befouled Saint) and Velorum (Saint of the Endless Hour). Each is a jigsaw structure `hemomancy:mausoleum_<saint>` in the set `saint_mausoleums` (random spread 40/12), biomes per Saint (Hemorath badlands and savanna; Seraphae meadow, cherry grove, flower forest, sunflower plains; Putriciel swamp, mangrove, dark forest; Velorum snowy biomes). The old generic `hemomancy:mausoleum` stays in the data, disabled.

**Placement.** The entrance pavilion sits flush on the highest ground within 14 blocks of its centre and refuses a site whose ground there varies by more than 4 blocks (`MausoleumEntranceRules`), so it is never cut into a hillside. `max_distance_from_center` stays 116 so the complex folds back under itself. `MausoleumSealing` keeps ground water out of every chunk as it generates.

**Chain and layout.** Entrance, stairs, antechamber (barrels: `chests/mausoleum`), ossuary descent, crypt gallery (six bays of loculi, three barrels, a reliquary behind a hematic iron door with the `chests/mausoleum_treasure` barrel), a two-piece spiral down an open well, the threshold hall (ward line), the galleried nave (31 by 21, 16 tall, the ring door at its far end), three Chapel wings (slot 0 at the nave floor, 1 from its gallery, 2 down into a crypt) and the Saint's chamber as the lowest room, under the entrance. Walked backwards: the Saint's life, rest above and beginning below. The nave is 34 below the entrance floor, the chamber 40; 19 pieces, lowest box -39.

**Degrees.** The outer halls (entrance through the gallery and its loot) are open from about D3 to D4; there is no trial out there any more. The ward, the Chapels, the ring door and the Saint are D8.

**Consecrated ward.** Inside a complex survival players cannot break or place, and explosions and pistons move nothing; every refusal strikes the ward (`WardStrikePacket`: ripple, flash, low bell, glow motes). Past the threshold slab everything is warded: a hidden `ward_anchor` block entity in the threshold wall (`WardAnchors`), a D8 gate in `MausoleumWardRules` that sets anyone lower down a block before the line ("The ward does not know you"), a breathing glyph layer in the Saint's ward colour within 12 blocks (`MausoleumWardRenderer`, `mausoleum_ward` shader). Hallowed ground: an Apotheos loses 4 spore load a second, then takes 1 `hallowed_burn` a second (`HallowedBurnLayer` crawls the glyphs over them); Silentium and lower Harbingers are untouched. The ground is consecrated, not holy: the Saint's residue rejecting the hive.

**Chapels.** Each wing is approach, trial hall and focus room; the generator writes the trial's marks into the `chapel_focus` (`ChapelRules`, one D8 challenger at a time; leaving, dying or a wrong answer resets with a 100-tick cooldown). Four kinds:

| Kind | Trial | Set pieces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beholding | Four witnesses flash in order; walk back and stand at each in that order within 400 ticks | `witness_stone`, Velorum's `witness_devotee` |
| Litany | A stone by the door shows three words (Abocipher unless literate); offer those three of six candidates at the focus, in order, within a minute | `litany_stone`, six `offering_stand`s |
| Downfall | Step on the gate; three waves of three Saint mobs (constructs, vexes, bog revenants, strays) rise from their seals; reach the focus | `downfall_seal` seals |
| Repose | Stay still in the inlaid ring for 600 ticks while corner pylons pulse (2 magic damage every 60 ticks, never lethal; Silentium stillness counts double) | `pulse_pylon` |

Slots: Hemorath Beholding/Litany/Downfall; Seraphae Beholding/Downfall/Repose; Putriciel Litany/Downfall/Repose; Velorum Beholding/Litany/Repose. A completed Chapel stays lit.

**Ring door.** A 5 by 5 controller multiblock (`ring_door`, 24 fillers, no item): venous slab in a hematic iron frame, three rings with lacquer nodes. One node lights per completed Chapel (found within 64 blocks, plus or minus 16 high) and its ring turns to the seam. It opens to held Blood Projection on either path: `RingDoorRules.OPEN_TICKS` 200 fed ticks paying 5 each (1000 in all, `ring_door` source), a 4-tick grace then a bleed-off at half speed, no refund. A Silentium with all three nodes opens it properly (`PROPER`): blood spreads to monolith stone and the slab sinks. An Apotheos forces it at any time (`FORCED`): hyphae crack it and it bursts, and the tomb records the skipped Chapel kinds. Right-click with an empty hand only says the rings answer blood.

**Sarcophagus and chamber.** The tomb is 2 by 4 on 23 fillers, centred on its main block, cut at z 32 into foot and head sections (`SectionedBakedModel`, shared with the door), retextured per Saint by `SarcophagusPalettes` and `RetexturedBakedModel`; the item is the tomb at half scale. The chamber is 37 by 27 by 17: processional, pillar ranks, a clear 15 by 15 boss floor, a three-step dais and an apse; `CHAMBER_SOCKETS` are filled by each theme's `chamber_sockets` hook. Consecration is three 100-blood offerings in the bowl; a vial draw with a dominant temper matching the Saint's pair gives one peaceful Consecrated Syringe (Centrifuge makes Hallowed Residuum), otherwise the Saint wakes. Foul Paste or a Saint relic wakes it regardless.

**Set pieces.** Eleven generator-authored blocks: `frozen_devotee`, `witness_stone`, `litany_stone`, `offering_stand`, `pulse_pylon`, `downfall_seal`, `containment_anchor_block`, `rot_pyre`, `sealed_niche`, `opened_niche`, `reliquary_sconce`, plus Hemorath's `blood_gutter` and `venous_basin`. No template carries an entity. Per-Saint looks are `style` or `flame` properties on the shared block, not new blocks.

**Themes.** Every theme grades by depth, walked backwards from the chamber, and keeps the threshold corridor and the boss floor pinned open.

| Saint | Family and motif | Own halls | Ambience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hemorath | Venous stone, hematic iron plating, bone; basins and channels, gutters wetter by depth; the Order's own house | Downfall (the Draining Pit), descending to the chamber | Heartbeat quickening with depth, drips |
| Seraphae | Quartz brick, calcite, diorite grilles; chains and healed overgrowth (GROWTH 0 to 4), swollen pillars; a containment, not a tomb | Repose (the Still Ward) | Choir hum, anchor ticks |
| Putriciel | Mud brick, mossy stone brick, packed ash, charred wood, bone keystones only; alternating burned and overgrown bands (BAND 8 to 3), pyres and spore shelves the only light | Litany (the Burned Library), Purge Corridors, the Ash Garden | Crackle, creak, far flares |
| Velorum | Rimed deepslate, packed and blue ice, dark lacquer; the frozen congregation everywhere (FROST 1 to 4), rime lanterns, icicles | Litany (the Rule), Repose (the Cell Row) | Ice creaks, the player's own steps muffled |

New block families: §24.12 (Putriciel) and §24.13 (Velorum) of the reference.

**Awakening and the fights.** The rising Saint reads its tomb's door record (`SaintEnrageRules`). Forced: +30% attack, +15% speed, and each skipped Chapel adds its stat floor (Downfall +50% health, Repose +20% speed, Beholding Resistance, Litany Regeneration) and a deed in the Saint's own language (below); residuum drops as a fragment (three craft one). Proper fights as designed. All four extend `SaintTrialEntity`, which carries the shared rise, the tomb return and one reward roll (`entities/<saint>`: residuum and 1 to 2 Sanguine Quintessence). No Saint can be killed: every ending runs `endTrial` once, and only `/kill` removes a Saint (unrewarded, back to its tomb).

**The Saint combat kit (2026-11-05).** The four fights share one kit (`common/entity/boss/` for the generic parts, `common/entity/boss/saint/` for the Saint parts) and differ only in their tables. A Saint returns a `SaintKit` from `createKit()`: its `SaintMove` enum (each move a `BossMoveTimeline`, a `BossFootprint` the server tests and the client draws, an aim rule, band and sector weights, cooldown, phases, follow-ups two deep at most, and `onTick` / `onHit` hooks), its locomotion, its `SaintMeter`, its `SaintPresence`, its `SaintPhaseTable`, its damage type (`hemomancy:saint_<name>`, Putriciel also `saint_putriciel_cleansing`) and its ending spec. `SaintCombatController` owns locomotion, facing and the move state machine (pick, a wind-up that tracks, a commit that locks the aim, the active span, the recovery); one range-band set serves all four (HUG under 3, NEAR under 6.5, MID under 12) with FRONT, FLANK and REAR sectors; wind-ups and footprint reveals are never under 12 ticks for melee or 20 for area moves; a stagger or ordinary phase change waits for the recovery boundary, and only an ending or a `Cut.IMMEDIATE` phase change cancels a move. `SaintOpenings` holds one punish window at a time (entry, length, what counts, damage-taken factor, hit caps by challenger count). The kit owns the damage pipeline in `SaintTrialEntity.actuallyHurt`: classify the hit (melee, projectile, school cast, held channel), admit it to the open window, apply the Saint's damage-taken factor to the damage container, and hold health at its floor (never under 1). `SaintArena` resolves the chamber from the tomb's position and facing; every block a fight changes goes through it, and it puts the original block and block-entity data back in `endTrial`, on removal, on the 600-tick empty-chamber leash reset, on load and when the server stops (`TransientServerStateEvents.onServerStopping`); entities a fight spawns (hazards, devotees, fragments) are owned by it and go with the trial. `SaintChannelRouter` hands held Blood Absorption and Projection aimed at a Saint, its anchors, figures or wells to that Saint before the item's own paths run, so every blood cost goes through `BloodAccounting` with a named source. `BossHold` (Seraphae's hold), `BossGroundHazardEntity` (lingering footprints), `SaintEnding` (a saved ending window that resumes on load) and `SaintDeeds` complete it. Client: one HUD layer, `SaintMeterLayer`, draws the fighter's meter under that Saint's bar from `SaintMeterPacket` (the kit's one payload) with a `SaintMeterStyle` per Saint, and one world pass, `SaintTelegraphRenderer`, draws every move's footprint from the synced move block in that Saint's material families (`SaintClientStyles`). `SaintCombatKitGuardrailTest` holds the rules.

Each fight uses its chamber through the arena: Hemorath's gutters fill with his circulation and he draws on his four wells (a pit sump was added to his chamber); Seraphae's anchors are woken, slip and are re-bound with held Blood Projection, and her radiance spreads through the healed stone (`radiant_healed_quartz`); Putriciel's pyres go out at the rise and light in each interval, and the fire scorches the boss floor to packed ash, the only ground the Bloom of Rot cannot take; Velorum's `frozen_devotee` figures are cover, are woken with held Projection to fight beside a Silentium (`VelorumDevoteeEntity`, the jointed generator rig) or pinned by an Apotheos, rise against an Apotheos, and his Long Night and Endless Hour darken rime lanterns (two aisle-end votive stands were added so the chamber is never black). Deeds of a forced awakening: Hemorath *What was not watched* (late tells, a feint), *The unsung measure* (wells refill, the four-well draw, Tithe x1.5), *The gorging* (he rises at 25% meter and gains the Gorge pull); Seraphae *Unwatched Hands*, *The Extraction*, *No Rest Given*; Putriciel *The Unread Rite* (a glyph names the act the palm accepts), *The Faithful Rise* (bog bodies), *It Never Pauses* (no Embering); Velorum *They watch you* (the figures' gaze cones), *The Rule kept in silence* (the Draw of Silence, no opening extensions), *He never pauses* (a 40-tick Bow). Seraphae speaks short nurse's lines and Velorum four lines (voice sheets in `NPC_VOICES.md`); Hemorath and Putriciel have narration only. Full designs, build notes and verification: `superpowers/specs/2026-11-05-saint-overhauls-design.md` and the four `2026-11-05-saint-<name>-overhaul-design.md`.

**Residuum and Recollections.** A Recollection cyst (one Saint each, rarity 1/24 chunks in the Archive biomes) gives its Recollection to an Apotheos or a possessing Silentium by touch. Each Canon Memory (Crimson Tithe, Unclosing Eye, Bloom of Rot, Endless Hour) is woven at the Loom from the Saint's Hallowed Residuum and its Recollection with the temper pair and 100 blood (§25.7.2).

**Models and animation.** Each Saint's rig, atlases and `.bbmodel` come from `tools/model_export/<saint>.py`; `<saint>_anim.java` holds one Catmull-Rom keyframe track per move, opening and ending on the move's own clock (`BossReplayClock` over the kit's synced move block), each blending from the last pose over 5 ticks. The 2026-11-05 pass: Hemorath's three vessel stages that swell with his meter, nine spike-patch sites and a hematic-iron palette with no orange; Seraphae rebuilt at about 3.2 blocks with ivory nerve-tree wings, a bone halo and hematic iron locks, gold only as nerve light; Putriciel with jointed legs under the robe, pale mould over olive (no brass, no red outside the fire) and char and ember layers; Velorum with `rime_iron` in place of silver, frozen cruor on the halo links as his only red, a breast seam, and the jointed devotee mob (`velorum_devotee.py`). All at 16 px per block. Visual pass 2 (2026-11-06) rebuilt all four animations on Hemorath's machinery after the user found his first build "stiff and disjointed": a frame is the stance, then the locomotion, then the playing track as deltas whose keys start and end at zero, then an inertial blend, then springs on everything that hangs, then `pose()`; each strike has a coil, a fast arc through the commit, follow-through and a weighted settle, and the feet stay on the floor. Seraphae's Healed Ground now kneels about a block into the slam (the plant sink capped at `PLANT_REACH`) instead of lying flat in the floor; Velorum's tracks are long still holds broken by one decisive motion. Effects carry the limbs' own trails (`<Saint>AnimState`, fed by each renderer every frame).

**Visuals (visual pass 2, 2026-11-06).** After playing Hemorath's fight the user found the effects plain next to the rest of the mod: flat ground tints, hairline edges, thin spikes, line streams. The cause was the kit's `TelegraphCanvas` budget (fills at alpha 0.22, edges 0.012 wide), which had carried the "subtle, no spaghetti" ruling from body and screen overlays onto attacks. Each Saint now has its own telegraph pass, `<Saint>TelegraphPass` in `client/render/world/`, run from the manipulation visuals' solid and translucent passes. It reads the same synced move, `FootprintFrame`, timeline and reveal as `SaintTelegraphRenderer`, so a drawn edge is still the hit test's edge. Its `<Saint>TelegraphStyle` claims every move and hazard and paints nothing, so the kit's flat tints never show for a Saint; `SaintTelegraphPainter` stays as the fallback for a move a style declines. Each pass draws on its Saint's own materials, built once in `<Saint>RenderTypes` over shaders in `shaders/core/world/` (`hemorath_fissure` and `hemorath_iron`; `seraphae_tissue` and `seraphae_bone`; `putriciel_ground`, `putriciel_spore` and `putriciel_growth`; `velorum_frost` and `velorum_ice`), with the school flows as accents. Every move runs the same life: emerge, build to the commit, a flash, the strike, a linger and a dissolve. The floor answers each Saint in its own language. For Hemorath, bone and venous stone crack, blood wells and iron punches up with mass. For Seraphae, the floor is cut open and swells shut too fast, white light wells and runs out along vessels, bone grows and curls, and light pours out in streams and sheets with body, caught link by link in her chains. In Putriciel's rot, mould colonies of many sizes spread, ooze pools and spore sacs push up; in the cleansing interval the char eats the rot in curled plates and Flammeus tongues with full flame bodies erupt. For Velorum, seams freeze, a partly clear rime glaze spreads plate by plate, black ice grows, darkness folds out and real ice stands up, holds and sublimes. Struck bodies get `<Saint>ImpactGeometry` (Velorum's pass draws its own impacts).

**The trench (pass 2b).** The user asked for Hemorath's Iron Rake lanes to become trenches and for the idea to be carried over to the other three. A lane is a strip on the floor plane whose shader traces the view ray into a channel below it through the strip's mouth (the frame rebuilt from screen derivatives, walls leaning in, detail on 16 texels a block), so it reads sunk from any angle with no geometry under the floor. Hemorath: the Iron Rake's lanes and the Rib Burst's spokes are trenches 1.0 deep (`TRENCH_DEPTH`, equal in `HemorathFissureRules` and `hemorath_fissure`), with stone walls darker than the floor, blood flooding to 0.45 of the depth (0.62 at the surge) with a buried glowing seam and a bright meniscus, and slabs of the chamber's own dark brick heaved along the broken lips (`HemorathFissureRules.slabs`; `hemorath_iron` shapes SLAB and BROKEN). Seraphae: each lane is one filled furrow (`seraphae_tissue` shape 0, 0.75 deep) with torn flesh walls, milky light standing in it, swollen lips and a gold nerve along each lip. Putriciel: rot trenches with layered walls, hanging hyphae and glossy ooze at most 78% full; fire trenches with soot and char walls over a bed of coals; both pinch shut at their ends (`PutricielGroundGeometry.strip`). Velorum: the five Black Ice lanes sink and black ice wells up to fill them, glossy blue-black between rimed lips.

Each Saint had five native polish rounds. **Putriciel's revision: the burning purifier, rot sludge and colour (2026-11-06).** The user found the purifier stage not fiery enough ("the red lights in his chest" were not enough), asked for the staff and the back to burn, for rotting sludge that persists after attacks as area denial until the purifying stage burns it away, and for colour variation on the model. In the cleansing interval Putriciel is now physically on fire: about seventy fire anchors from the generator (`PutricielFireAnchors`; staff 12, censers 2, back 19, shoulders 15, crown 9, chest 6, hem 7) carry layered Flammeus tongues with soot and a bed of coals (`PutricielBodyFireGeometry`, every tongue drawn 1.75 times taller and 1.6 times wider than the rules' heights after the polish); the staff burns from the grip to a three-pronged torch head, the back colony carries the tallest fire and a back column with a soot plume, the arms and shoulders smoulder, the censers roar through their lids, and the body sheds ash, tear-offs, burning shelf fragments and scorched footprints as it moves. `PutricielBodyFireRules` (pure, common) times it all: the Ignition builds the fire up the body from tick 10 to the commit's flare, the steady burn follows the Kindling (0.6 + 0.4 of its fraction), the moves raise peaks, an Offering draws it down to the palm, the Embering gutters it by tick 10 and puts the last embers out by 30, and every value is zero in the rot. A burning layer on the model (`PutricielBurnLayer`, `putriciel_burning.png`, fire colours only) lights the rims, cracks and wraps region by region, and the block-light floor rises to 13 while it burns. **Rot sludge** (`PutricielSludgeRules`; hazards `ROT_SLUDGE` 5, `SLUDGE_FIRE` 6, `FIRE_RUNNER` 7 on the kit's ground hazard): Shelf Volley's landings 0, 3 and 6, the Censer Drop's crater, every other Offering Grasp's runnel, a Root Snare that hits no one and the Bloom of Rot's last pulse leave pools on the boss floor, never on this fight's ash or in a pyre circle. Wading slows to 0.65 (`hemomancy:putriciel_sludge`) and adds colonisation at twice the presence's rate; there is no damage. At most 6 pools and 42 square blocks with one challenger (+1 and +6 per extra, at most 9 and 60); laying past the cap settles the oldest into the moss. At the Ignition's commit the server schedules when the fire reaches each pool and colony patch: the Ignition ring, a fire runner crawling from each relit pyre and on from each burning pool, the fire moves, the burning body or a burning player, and a fallback 140 ticks after the commit. A caught pool burns from the catch point outward over 50 ticks (catch, boil, char, ash) and leaves packed ash at age 44 through `SaintArena` under the budget `putriciel_sludge_ash`, so the rot a player let pool becomes the next Bloom's safe ground; the ending burns every pool in 10 ticks and lays no ash. The pool is a real heightfield mesh on `putriciel_growth`'s `SLUDGE` shape with a stain (`putriciel_ground` `STAIN`), and telegraphs ride its surface (`PutricielSludgeField`). **Colour** (`tools/model_export/putriciel.py`): ivory and pale cream vestments with a new stole, rot-dyed olive-brown and mould-green robe panels with ochre tide marks and a charcoal hem, dark eschar, graded cream-to-ochre sacs, shelves in three zoned schemes, mould-green moss, rag-wrapped staff rings; measured dark 30.6%, mid 43.2%, light 26.2%, ochre 10.6%, mould green 6.3%, no red outside the fire (`PutricielPaletteTest`).
