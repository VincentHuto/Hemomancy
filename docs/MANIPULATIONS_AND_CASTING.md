Status (2026-11-14): 115 registered manipulations across eight schools (108 after balance pass 2; the core loop added the Tendon Line, Blood Lash, Lode Draw, Hematic Hewing, Conjure Pickaxe, Living Land and Venous Cling). 33 memories carry 39 Sculptings (counted in `ManipulationInit` on 2026-11-14, rechecked 2026-11-16): three are lashes, the only strands since 2026-11-12, and eight hold a mechanic of their own (see [CORE_LOOP.md](CORE_LOOP.md)). Two balance passes landed. Casting animations v2 and the Cast Outpouring were committed in `18dc1ec92` (2026-10-19); cast impacts, the body-anchored basics and the infusion vein overlay in `3fae723d0` (2026-10-21). The owner answered the question sheet on 2026-11-13, and an overnight build on 2026-11-14 built the rulings that waited on nothing (for this doc: packages P02, P03, P05, P08 and P16 to P18 of [`owner_answers/BUILD_LOG_2026-11-14.md`](owner_answers/BUILD_LOG_2026-11-14.md)). The verbs, the lashes, the held manipulations and the new numbers have not been played by the owner. Open questions are in [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

## What it is

Blood manipulations are the abilities a Harbinger fuels with blood. Each registered entry has a temper (school), a secondary temper, a rank, a cast type (Quick, Charged, Continuous, Passive), a cost and a vascular section it draws from (`HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` §8.1). The eight tempers are Animus, Flammeus, Ductilis, Lux, Mortem, Congeatio, Ferric and Tenebris (§9). Ranks run Humilis (D0), Mediocritas (D1), Summa (D3), Magister (D5), Perfectus (D6).

**School combat layer.** Every DIRECT school hit applies the school's state through `SchoolStates` (Pressure, Necrosis, Lodestone, Searing, Rime, Disrupted, Illuminated, Obscured) and `SchoolCombatEvents.prepare`/`commit` consume them for the payoffs (Aneurysm, Funeral Bell, Vitric Combustion, Lodestone Collapse, Umbral Rend and the rest; `wiki/School-Combat.md`, plan §4 and §10).

**Drudge and entity casts.** A manipulation attaches its Drudge behaviour with `setDrudgeAction(...)` (work in `DrudgeActions`) and what a Will or Vesper does when it casts with `setEntityCast(EntityCasts::x)`; `EntityManipulationEffects.cast` applies only the shared rules (`AGENTS.md`).

**The two verbs and Sculptings** (core-loop Track 1, 2026-11-10). Projection and Absorption are verbs, not equipped memories: the player's active verb is its own state, switched by the verb key (default Y) or picked on the radial's inner ring (CL-2); the key also conjures the active verb's cell hand into an empty hand and, sneaking, dispels it. The Living Staff channels the active verb. A selected manipulation may carry a Sculpting of either verb (`setProjectionSculpting` / `setAbsorptionSculpting`): it claims what the player aims at after rites, Saints and Wills and before the plain verb's endpoints, pays per pulse through `tryPerformVerbPulse` (the cast gates, `BloodAccounting.payUpkeep` as `sculpting`, no cooldown) and counts one ordinary use per 80 ticks of work. Track 1 attached none; 33 memories carry one today. R stays the combat cast (CL-1). Full rules: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` §8.0; design: [`archive/tracks/core_loop/01-verb-framework.md`](archive/tracks/core_loop/01-verb-framework.md).

**The first Sculptings** (core-loop Track 2, 2026-11-10). **Blood Binding leads a creature home** (core-loop Track 5, 2026-11-10). Held for three seconds on a creature that is passive or worn to a third of its health, Blood Binding's Sculpting leashes it (`MenagerieLeash.heldByBinding`, called from the Sculpting's pulse): it follows its keeper, placid, for blood upkeep each second, until it is put in a Specimen Vat or Jar, let go, or breaks free unpaid. One rule (`MenagerieRules`) says who may be kept, for the leash, the jar and the vat alike. Plain Projection at a passive animal doses it with Sanguine Fertility, at the cost of taint (`AnimalTaint.tryProject`, a link right after the Sculpting). Full rules: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` "The menagerie and research"; design: [`archive/tracks/core_loop/05-menagerie-and-research.md`](archive/tracks/core_loop/05-menagerie-and-research.md).

**Strands: held Projection as a weapon** (core-loop Track 12, 2026-11-10; rebuilt 2026-11-12). A strand is a held Projection Sculpting with a combat form. Since 2026-11-12 three memories hold one, and all three are lashes: Blood Lash (Animus Humilis, nothing to cast on R), Searing Lash and Gloam Laceration. Once begun a strand keeps the channel until release or an unpaid pulse. A left click with a lash selected and nothing channelled is a tap straight down the crosshair (`LashTapPacket`). Held, the lash gathers as an orb that charges the first crack; a flick of the mouse throws it along an arc that cracks at first contact; it then swings on a whip aim of its own while the view turns at a capped rate, and fades back to the orb when the hand rests. Each lash's `LashProfile` leans it toward the tap or the charge, and its `LashStyle` gives it a rope and a signature: Blood Lash is the tether (a charged crack holds its target, a landed sweep returns blood), Searing Lash the heavy whip (burst, fire, cracks in a row build), Gloam Laceration the three-tailed razor (three cuts a blow, ambush, pierce, a tap chain). While a channel is held the attack button is the accent of whichever Sculpting holds it (`VerbSculpting.takesAccent` and `accent`, sent by `StrandSnapPacket`); on a lash it snaps. Every lash memory keeps its R cast. Absorption names the one drawing and rides the school state on the creature (`AbsorptionRiders`), taking the drawer's branded creatures first. Full rules: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` "Strand combat" and section 8.0.2; design: [`archive/tracks/core_loop/12-strand-combat.md`](archive/tracks/core_loop/12-strand-combat.md) and [`superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-lash-rework-design.md`](superpowers/specs/2026-11-12-lash-rework-design.md).

**Eight held manipulations with a mechanic of their own** (2026-11-12). The first build gave these memories a jet, a spray or a plant. Each is now its own `VerbSculpting` class and keeps its R cast: Hematic Flare (a searchlight beam that wounds nothing and builds heat on one body; the attack button spends the heat as a flash), Blood Eclipse (a dome of dark carried on the caster, ended in a flash by the attack button), Glacial Grasp (a hand of ice that holds one creature and squeezes on the attack button; its narrow Sculpting still freezes water first), Cruor Javelin (drawn into the hand through three stages and thrown on release; held Absorption recalls one stuck in a block), Blood Needle Lance (up to six hovering needles, loosed together on release or one at a time on the attack button), Synaptic Jolt (keeps a conductor live, or jolts the creature in the aim), Crimson Flame Conjuration (paints standing flames that the attack button flares together; its kindling is still tried first) and Ferric Spikes (a run of blades steered along the ground that erupts on the attack button). Six memories lost their held form the same day and cast on R only: Hemorrhage, Blood Needle Fan, Prismatic Reproof, Conductive Mark, Insatiable Hunger and Grave Debt. Blood Binding still lays the Animus brand on what it holds. Numbers: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` 8.0.2.

**The Living Staff's mining** (core-loop Track 8, 2026-11-10). The Staff grows rings from the blood moved through it (reach, channel rate, the digging forms' tier, a cheaper swap), and its ninth form, the pickaxe (`conjure_pickaxe`), comes with the bond. Two new Ferric memories have nothing to cast and sculpt Projection: Lode Draw crawls an ore lode and breaks it on release; Hematic Hewing cuts a tunnel, a sneaking 3x3 face or a stair's column each second. Both harvest at the Staff's ring tier with plain drops, never take a block entity (Hewing) and cost more a block than the pickaxe. Full rules: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` "The Living Staff grows and mines" and section 8.0.1; design: [`archive/tracks/core_loop/08-living-staff-and-mining.md`](archive/tracks/core_loop/08-living-staff-and-mining.md).

**Living Land and Venous Cling** (core-loop Track 9, 2026-11-10). Two terrain Sculptings with nothing to cast. Living Land (Animus, Ferric secondary) is a held Projection on a floor, wall or ceiling: the patch wakes and strikes the nearest enemy in front of it with a column of its own real blocks, which settle back home; with nothing near the patch rises into a step. Every lifted block is in the level's `LivingLandLedger` until it is back, so nothing is gained or lost. Venous Cling (Animus Humilis) roots the player to a wall or ceiling they touch for upkeep each second; walking crawls them along it, Hematic Cleats halve its upkeep (CB-21) and quicken the crawl, and the Holdfast morphling now reads the same `ClingRules`. Full rules: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` "Living Land and Venous Cling" and 8.0.1; design: [`archive/tracks/core_loop/09-living-land-and-cling.md`](archive/tracks/core_loop/09-living-land-and-cling.md).

**The second wave of Sculptings** (core-loop round two P4, 2026-11-11). Seven memories took a Sculpting and kept their R casts: Sanguine Mending (Projection mends a dropped tool, heals an injured iron or snow golem not fighting the caster, beats a worn anvil back a stage after eight pulses; 40 mL/s), Crimson Flame Conjuration (Projection kindles unlit campfires and candles within 2 of the aim, two a pulse, and primes TNT as the player's; 12 mL/s), Furnace Veins (Projection burns blood as the fuel of every furnace within 2 that has something to cook, only while held; 12 mL/s), Glacial Grasp (a narrow Projection Sculpting ahead of its main one, the ice hand since 2026-11-12: open water freezes into Frozen Cruor that melts back to water, 10 mL/s; Absorption thaws cruor and ice, 4 mL/s), Glacial Rampart and Ferric Rampart (Projection draws an ice wall, or block-wide iron segments, along the sweep; 48 and 40 mL/s) and Carrion Communion (Absorption draws Mortem Temper and health, never blood, from rotten flesh, bones, skulls and bone blocks; 8 mL/s). A manipulation may now carry a narrow Sculpting tried ahead of its main one (`addProjectionSculpting`), which is how Glacial Grasp freezes water and still lays its hand of ice on a creature. Prices and work: `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` §8.0.1; build record: `archive/tracks/core_loop/BUILD_LOG_ROUND2.md` P4.

**Engram weaving.** The Somatic Loom teaches a memory by unfurling an eight-pointed engram on the ground; the weaver threads one fragment per recipe enzyme home with a Living Staff, colours never sharing a node, with scars, valves, prisms and false fragments by rank. Spans (1 enzyme = 3 spans, 192 per vial) are spent only at completion and no weave can kill. The player's guide is [`engram-weaving/README.md`](engram-weaving/README.md); the design is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-somatic-loom-engram-weaving-design.md`, which superseded the combat half of the 2026-10-11 witnessed weave (setup, tiers, echo casts and the Cortical dome stayed).

**Casting presentation** (all 2026-10-19):

- *Casting animations v2*: 40 school-by-rank keyframe tracks in both views, 31 bespoke styles, an aim layer owned by purpose, and the Unstained Still Art lane (five clarity stages, endings). Six layers: base track, cast type, rank scope, aim layer, bespoke override, secondary accent (`wiki/Casting-Animations.md`).
- *Cast Outpouring*: the caster's own blood leaves the manipulation's vascular section, shader veins light under the skin, the blood turns into the school's material, scales with rank and leaves fading client-only marks. Nothing detached floats behind the caster. Replaced hand veins, rank constructs and Lux wings.
- *Cast impacts*: one `CAST_IMPACT` per landed DIRECT school hit, hooked last in `SchoolCombatEvents.commit`; shape by delivery (MELEE, PROJECTILE, AREA, TETHER), flourish by school, 10-tick life, one per target per 5 ticks unless heavy. Committed in `3fae723d0`; kept as built (RV-23).
- *Body-anchored basics*: Blood Rush, Sanguine Ward, Sanguine Mending, the Living Arsenal conjure (one class for all `conjure_*`), the cell hands (Blood Absorption/Projection) and the avatar manifest, drawn from a `CasterFrame`. Committed in `3fae723d0`; kept as built (RV-23).
- *Infusion veins* (2026-10-20): blood infusion gets a vein overlay growing from the impact point as a sore and a separate melt shell. Committed in `3fae723d0`; kept as built (RV-23).

The visual bar is [`MANIPULATION_VISUALS.md`](MANIPULATION_VISUALS.md): a focal effect is its own geometry class with a named timeline, layered shell/sinew/knots/business end/motion anatomy, white base tint plus exactly two accents, rooted on the body and aimed with the head, with a separate impact burst; particles are accents and geometry carries the effect.

### Standing direction

- The Hematic Riposte arm is the gold-standard visual; reuse its layered recipe when upgrading any focal manipulation, rite, boss or impact effect (`MANIPULATION_VISUALS.md`).
- Custom Hemomancy/HutosLib particles and geometry first; vanilla particles only as a deliberate fallback. No vanilla `ParticleTypes` in `common/manipulation/` (`ManipulationVanillaParticleSourceTest`), none in the Outpouring, impacts or Still Arts.
- No rings, funnels or "vacuum cleaner" mechanical shapes on blood effects; prefer organic behaviour (gravity, capillaries, drips). The first cell-hand use geometry was rejected for this.
- Blood streams read as fluid with travelling bulges, a sheath, a dark underside and a highlight; constant-width thin ribbons are "stiff wires".
- Every hand visual lines up with the real first-person palm while moving (`FirstPersonPalm`); conjures coat the real weapon's faces in its real held pose (`HeldWeaponPose`).
- Infusion veins grow slowly from the impact point as a sore, not a star of spokes; they spill onto neighbours, go dark before the break, and the melt shell is a separate overlay. "Lava lamp" contour veins and a stiff radial tree with glowing cracks were both rejected.
- Casting v2 goal: readable, school-distinct casting in both views, less restrained than the 2026-09 revision but never disjointed (no torso off the waist, no arms off the shoulders).
- Outpouring Revision 1: reuse the mod's toolkit (the first build's own material and mark shaders were "blocky"); veins must look like the floor-shader veins at screen resolution; no copper, brass, bronze or silver on the Harbinger lane; nothing detached behind the caster.
- Engram weaving must be fun, challenging and "alien, magical, visceral" in the style of the Cardinal floor membrane; no weave can kill.
