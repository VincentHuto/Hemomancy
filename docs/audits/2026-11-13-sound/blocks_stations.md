# Sound audit: Blocks, stations, block sound types

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- No block or block entity in common/tile or common/block plays a single mod sound event; all 106 calls are vanilla, apart from three borrowed mod events (CardinalFocus rite.floor_form/anchor_break, which fit, and the Monolith BE:342 plus LidlessPassageService:321 both reusing item.synaptic_step.use for a door/monolith moment, which needs its own event).
- SomaticLoomBlockEntity:1032 plays BEACON_POWER_SELECT on every Living Staff charge call with no tick gate, so it likely fires every tick while the player holds Projection; the Effigy (:188) and Brazier (:298, :338) gate theirs to every 8 ticks. The replacement charge sound needs a gate or should become a held loop.
- UnstainedPodiumBlock:93 and SemiSentientConstructBlock:127 play ZOMBIE_AMBIENT with the acting player as the excluded listener, so other players hear a zombie moan whenever someone touches the Unstained Podium. Tonally wrong for Unstained content.
- Biggest silent stations: Vial Centrifuge (no spin sound at all), Enzymatic Scriptorium, Resonant Forge, Hematic Armature, Scar Station, Mycelial Crucible, Morphling Incubator/Cradle, and every station-upgrade completion. The station menus and screens play no sounds either.
- Ghastly Alembic and Pallid Retort share identical animateTick bubble code (15% chance per client tick at 0.6 volume); they should get different sounds, thick blood against clear water.
- SoundType: sclerite_block and stillwater_condenser use SoundType.COPPER. Sclerite is blood-side (chitin), so copper is wrong there; the Stillwater Condenser is Unstained, where copper is fine. Many lacquer/bone stations (Somatic Loom, Scar Station, Morphling Incubator/Cradle, Mason's Effigy, Sanguine Monolith) are registered as plain STONE.
- VigilArchiveBlockEntity calls were kept vanilla on purpose: they drive real vanilla sculk sensors, shriekers and deepslate set dressing, so the vanilla timbre matches what the player sees.
