# Sound audit: Manipulation schools

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- Every manipulation's generic cast sound comes from ManipulationCastSounds (root of common/manipulation, outside this slice): a vanilla cue table covering about 30 entries, including school ones here (absolute_stillness, rimebound_sentence, activation_potential, synaptic_storm, living_circuit, sanguine_ward, deadly_gaze, white_verdict, radiant_wake, funeral_bell, carrion_communion, penumbral_drift, eclipse_well, umbral_rend, cruor_javelin, fruiting_contagion, crimson_coronation). Replacing that table with one magic.cast.<temper> event per school, plus bespoke ids for focal casts, would give each school a consistent material in one place. Funeral Bell uses BELL_BLOCK there, which goes against the no-bell rule for Harbinger content.
- Double layering: Umbral Rend (sweep + Enderman scream), Cruor Javelin R cast (impact at the target and throw at the caster in the same tick, so the throw plays after the hit), Umbral Rend's cast-table sweep plus its own sweep, Eclipse Well's cast-table sonic boom plus two chorus-fruit teleports. Collapse each to the bespoke event.
- Many manipulations with no cast-table entry and no sound of their own are fully silent: Blood Cloud, Hematic Command (Rebuke and Impressment), Sanguine Marionette, Tendon Line haul, Venous Cling, Cruor Javelin recall, Sovereign Instinct (empty class, passive). Blood Lash and other strand forms play their sounds from verb/ strand code, which this slice did not cover.
- Several school sounds are recognisable vanilla mob voices: Warden heartbeat (Hemolymphal Pulse), Warden sonic boom (Black Veil Covenant), Wither hurt/death/shoot (Aneurysm, Blackhearted, Hemorrhage, Exsanguinate, Blood Eclipse), Enderman ambient/scream/teleport (all Tenebris movement), Husk ambient (Insatiable Hunger), sculk shrieker (Grave Debt). These should go first.
- Congeatio leans entirely on GLASS_PLACE/GLASS_BREAK/POWDER_SNOW; a shared cruor ice set (ice.cruor_form, ice.cruor_thaw) plus a few bespoke focal cracks would cover the school.
- Cruor Javelin stage tick is load-bearing feedback (the code comment says the hand counts stages by ear); the replacement must be tonal enough for the per-stage pitch shift.
- Axonal Transduction uses the existing item.synaptic_step.use event (not owned here). Still Arts (Unstained) have no sound under common/manipulation/stillarts; their actions live in capability code and were not audited in this slice.
- Living Land strikes and Blood Shot/Blood Cloud projectiles play (or miss) their impact sounds in entity classes outside this slice.
