## Notes

- Taken 2026-11-15 (B31, chunk 15 T14): the priority 1 Circus rows `entity.circus_ringmaster.phase_change`, `.finale_collapse`, `.hurt`, `entity.circus_performer.windup` and the two carousel rows, registered as `entity.impaled_procession.thread_sever` and `.anchor_break` (the carousel's entity id; `circus_carousel` is a retired name). They play the candidates as variants until the owner picks. The priority 2 Circus rows are not taken.

- BloodConstructEntity.playConstructLifecycleSound uses level().playLocalSound, which does nothing on a server level; if expiry/dissolution run server-side these cues are silent today. Volume is also 0.1.
- VerdigrisMothEntity brushing plays AXE_SCRAPE twice (inside shedViridSalis and again at line 138), and line 138 also plays while the brush is on cooldown.
- CloudEntityBlood plays LIGHTNING_BOLT_THUNDER at volume 10000 on SoundSource.WEATHER, so every player in a huge radius hears it; SanguisLancea return plays at volume 10.
- ChewWoodGoal plays a zombie wooden-door bang every 8 ticks while a termite chews: the loudest repeated vanilla borrow in this slice.
- LuminalCicada ambient returns BEE_LOOP, a loop asset, as a one-shot ambient.
- Six AbstractArrow projectiles (blood needle/bolt/bullet, recall barb, gore harpoon, circus knife) are not in the calls file but inherit vanilla ARROW_HIT; recorded as one silent_gap row.
- Many entities here make no action sound at all (Harbinger and Unstained NPCs, hagfish, siphonophore, vampire squid); only attacks and abilities were raised as gaps. Facsimiles inherit vanilla sounds on purpose.
