# Sound audit: Rites, world, progression, screens

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- Biome sounds are split: BiomeInit.fungalGardens (code bootstrap) sets nether_wastes loop, soul_sand_valley mood and crimson additions, but the authored fungal_gardens.json has no loop, cave mood and music.end. The JSON wins at runtime; decide which one is the source and delete the other.
- No Hemomancy biome registers its own ambient loop, mood, additions or music: every one borrows a vanilla nether, underwater, cave or End sound (17 biome files).
- rite.segment_seal, rite.wave_clear, rite.ambient.heartbeat and mausoleum.hemorath.heartbeat all alias the Warden heartbeat; HeartNodeMomentRenderer, SuccessionEffects and AntecedentClientEffects also call WARDEN_HEARTBEAT directly. One authored blood.heartbeat.deep set would fix seven places.
- rite.culmination is the only rite event with its own file, a 3.6 s stereo cinematic boom used positionally; needs a mono world version. Every other rite.* event (25) aliases a vanilla sound.
- rite.ambient.drone also drives the Lidless Passage loop (LidlessPassageSound); give the Lidless crossing its own loop when replacing.
- HarbingerCardinalRiteEvents:207-209 stacks three cues on the staff impact (rite.staff_impact, a vanilla ROOTED_DIRT_HIT and rite.consecrate); fold the dirt layer into the impact file.
- ENDERMAN_SCREAM is used for blood infusion completion and for two "not enough blood" refusals (BloodStructureFeedManager hint, BloodStructureCrafting); rite.complete also aliases enderman.scream. These are the most jarring borrows in the slice.
- BloodStructureFeedManager.java:186 and BloodStructureCrafting.java:143 call ServerLevel.playLocalSound with a null source; on a server level that is a no-op, so these refusals are probably silent today.
- HarbingerEquipmentEntityEventHandler:75 plays TOTEM_USE to everyone but the player while LayerEvents:445 plays it locally for the player; both are the Curved Horn save and should share one event.
- Unstained rite cues use bell and amethyst sounds; per sound direction they should move to glass and still water (copper stays fine for Unstained only).
