# Sound audit: Endgame bosses and encounters

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- The antecedent.* events show used_at empty only because the Clairaudiograph plays cylinder audio by resource id string (ClairaudiographSoundPacket -> client/sound/ClairaudiographSounds), not through the SoundInit constants. They are live. The client also decodes these files for the spectrograph, and AncientRecordings cue windows are fixed (73 s / 30 s / 30 s; Severed Record anomaly at ticks 1100-1360), so a replacement must keep the length and timing.
- Vesper puppet strings (recall, muster, inherit flare) currently use vanilla CHAIN_* sounds. Lore-wise these are blood or sinew strings off the Crossbar, so the briefs ask for gut and sinew, not metal chain.
- VesperPhaseTwoCombat's melee hit helpers (hitNear/hitArc/hitLine) make no sound, so most Evening Star school strikes are heard only when they cast a manipulation. This is the largest silent gap in the slice.
- Phlegethosoma uses only vanilla audio (ambient, hurt, death and every move), and its coagulum entities and suction, spray and lunge moves are silent. It needs a full set of its own.
- EndgameBossActions:91 and VesperTheCrownedRefusalEntity:866-867 already layer a mod sound (entity.vesper.hit or hurt) with the vanilla one. After replacement, drop the vanilla layer instead of keeping both.
- Several Mycophant moves stack two vanilla sounds on one moment (ROOTED_DIRT_BREAK plus another). Each pair is listed as one row with one event, so each pair should become a single designed sound.
- Not added as gaps: VesperOrdealManager.enter and VagrantMindEncounterManager.tryAwaken play no sound in their own code, but each starts a boss scene (PacketSyncVesperFightScene, BossScenes.playTo), which may carry audio in another slice. Worth checking in the boss presentation slice.
- Crossbow bolt at VesperPhaseTwoCombat:1104 is left vanilla; the living crossbow is still a crossbow, and swapping it for a mod sound can wait.
