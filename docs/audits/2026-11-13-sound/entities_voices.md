# Sound audit: Creature voices

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- This slice has no calls file, so calls_in_file = calls_covered = 0.
- Six creatures play another creature's files: Blood Lantern Jelly and Mnemonic Whale (Hemojelly), Chalybeate Snail (Barbed Urchin), Tooth Pecks (Chthonian), Brined Votary (Fargone) and Will (Abhorrent Thought). The whale is the worst fit: a 38 HP whale plays a 0.45 s jelly hurt.
- Naeglerophaeon has no files of its own. Its ambient, zap and drain play the deleted Synapse Hound's files; its hurt, death, charge and blink play Myelin Borer files. entity/myelin_borer/hurt alone serves four events (Myelin Borer hurt, Naeglerophaeon hurt and blink, item.synaptic_step.use).
- The abhorrent_thought files are stereo (ch 2) and serve nine events (three Abhorrent Thought, six Will). Positional mob sounds must be mono or distance falloff breaks. The Abhorrent hurt is also 1.4 s, long for a hurt.
- Borrowing from code in other slices: Hemorath's siphon plays entity.naeglerophaeon.drain (HemorathMoves.java:152); Putriciel plays entity.fungling.hurt and entity.erythromycelium_eruptus.ambient; PossessionSceneRenderer plays hemojelly.death and eruptus.death; CircusCarouselEntity:89 uses entity.enthralled_doll.ambient as the carousel's ambient. Each should get its own event when those slices are done.
- GroundPuppetEntity extends Zombie, so all five puppet summons (Cinder Bellows, Gorebound Hulk, Mnemonist Puppet, Ringmaster Pattern, Scarlet Mummer) groan like zombies. Sanguine Hound sounds like a vanilla wolf, Veinwing Vulture like a vex, Marrow Spitter like a skeleton and Peacock Spider like a full-size spider. Players hear these summons constantly, and Vesper's puppet roster uses the same bodies.
- Protected NPCs (all Harbinger outpost staff, the Unstained, Court members) are invulnerable, so their default GENERIC_HURT and GENERIC_DEATH never play; for them only rare idle breaths are suggested. Circus performers and the Ringmaster are not protected and currently use the player's hurt sound when attacked.
- Mobs whose voices are explicit vanilla constants (Bog Revenant, Excoriated, Drudge, Blood Thrall, Morphling Polyp, Verdigris Moth, Osteophage, Luminal Cicada, Choir Keeper hurt and death, Bombardier and Vampire Bat ambient) are vanilla_call rows in the entities slice and are not repeated here. Saint and boss voices (Hemorath, Seraphae, Putriciel, Velorum and devotees, Annetta, Latent Infection, Phlegethosoma) belong to the boss slices.
- To keep the file compact, some gap rows cover several moments in one brief: each Facsimile and puppet row covers ambient, hurt and death, and the Bombardier, Vampire Bat, small sea creature, Ringmaster and Hydra rows name their second event inside the brief. Give each moment its own event when implementing.
- Frayed Thread (invulnerable, unravels) gets no row. The scripted Hydra head is not a mob, but its rise got a row because this Court set piece is completely silent.
- Harbinger Hermit's ritual farewell death animation plays no sound at all, and it is a signature moment.
