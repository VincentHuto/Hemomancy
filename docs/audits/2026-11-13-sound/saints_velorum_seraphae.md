# Sound audit: Saints: Velorum, Seraphae

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- Two identities: Seraphae is bright, sustained and tonal (bowed glass and quartz, hematic-iron chain, dry gold nerve crackle, wet growth); Velorum is dark, sparse and low (frozen blood setting and cracking, iron staff and lantern, viscous dark, hush). No bell timbres on either; Velorum's tolls are struck iron, not bronze.
- Hits sound twice: Velorum onHitTaken (VelorumEntity:494/496) plays glass and getHurtSound (:1383) plays glass again for the same blow; Seraphae onHitTaken (:1440/1443) and getHurtSound (:1942) do the same if her zero-factor damage still triggers the hurt sound. Keep one source per hit.
- Seraphae fragment getHurtSound (SeraphaeFragmentEntity:200) plays right before the STRUCK shatter (SeraphaeEntity:1367); drop or shrink it.
- Several moments stack two sounds that should be one authored file: Velorum toll (rite.staff_impact + BELL_RESONATE), anchor rebind (rite.anchor_repair + CHAIN_PLACE), Fracture (GLASS_BREAK + AMETHYST_CLUSTER_BREAK), preserve (HURT_FREEZE + GLASS_PLACE), Convulsion (BEACON_DEACTIVATE + CLUSTER_BREAK).
- Both Saints lean on events owned by other slices: rite.offering_drawn opens five Velorum invocations, rite.collapse covers three Velorum endings, entity.will.dissolve/materialize is his Shadow Step, entity.vesper.grab_release ends Seraphae's hold, entity.stained_priestess.flare is her reach, rite.backlash is her Radiance Flood. When those slices cut their own files, these Saint moments want entity.<saint>.* events of their own.
- Both death sounds look unreachable (Seraphae's health never changes; Velorum's failing body is the scripted ending), so they stay vanilla as fallbacks.
- entity.seraphae.growth_gain (SeraphaeEntity:1192) plays to every whole player inside 9 blocks on every presence pulse: it needs several quiet variants.
