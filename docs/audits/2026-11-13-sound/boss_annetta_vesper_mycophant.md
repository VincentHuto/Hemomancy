# Sound audit: Annetta, Vesper, Mycophant

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- Vanilla calls for Vesper and the Mycophant live in common/entity/boss/endgame and are in the boss_endgame slice; this slice's calls are all Annetta, the Latent Infection and the Stained Priestess. Vesper and Mycophant rows here are their sounds.json events and their silent gaps.
- Every Vesper and Mycophant entity file except the Mycophant summon is stereo. Minecraft plays stereo files without positional falloff, so they need re-exporting as mono.
- Annetta's ambient/hurt/death are SKELETON_*: she is a living woman, and the skeleton rattle is the most wrong-sounding vanilla use in this slice.
- The Crowned Refusal (mount) and Vesper (the man, he/him) share entity.vesper.ambient/hurt/death. Split them into mount and rider voices; the Evening Star is the man alone.
- entity.vesper.ambient's file is also item.living_torch.breath_loop. Vesper's phase-two Flammeus Concentration plays the torch loop on purpose, but the torch should not share its file with a boss idle.
- Several focal moments stack two or three vanilla calls on the same tick (Annetta head burst, shield bash, break stab; Priestess plunge and plummet). Each should become one designed file.
- PriestessEruptionEntity plays two sounds per thorn, and plunge or plummet spawn 9 to 11 thorns over a few ticks, so 18 to 22 sounds stack. Keep the replacement short, or let only the first and last of a line play.
- Most Evening Star weapon moves (blade, axe, claw, flail, staff, sickle reaps) have no swing or impact sound at all; only Flammeus, the spear spiral, crossbow bolts and the thrown sickle arcs do. That is the largest silent area in this slice.
- Mycophant death file (9.86 s) runs past her 6 s yield scene. entity.mycophant.teleport now plays at the split, and the name no longer fits.
- entity.stained_priestess.flare, entity.vesper.grab_bite/grab_release/grab_telegraph and entity.mycophant.ambient/summon/hurtother are also borrowed by Saints (Putriciel, Seraphae, Hemorath, Velorum); those uses belong to the Saint slices.
