# Sound audit: Items, living tools, armour

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- BEACON_ACTIVATE/DEACTIVATE is copy-pasted as the on/off toggle on seven items (axe, blade, spear, lance, Annetta's lance, gourd, electrode); split into item.living_weapon.wake/rest plus per-item toggles.
- Silent Archon Severance plays SCULK_SHRIEKER_SHRIEK and WARDEN_SONIC_BOOM back to back; replace both with one layered event.
- LivingSickleItem.java:94 picks between two vanilla sounds on one line; it is covered by two rows (one per mode).
- item.living_torch.breath_loop reuses Vesper's stereo ambient file, which is also used by Vesper and Putriciel; give the torch its own mono loop.
- item.synaptic_step.use reuses entity/myelin_borer/hurt and is played from blocks and manipulations, not from SynapticStepItem.
- Qliphoth Pome eat/communion files are stereo 48 kHz; the eat file at least should be mono for positional playback.
- LivingStaffItem.java:247 and LivingStaffWeaponFormHelper.java:283 both use HOGLIN_CONVERTED_TO_ZOMBIFIED for unrelated moments (Morphling fire vs. staff reshape).
- BloodStainedStoneItem.releaseUsing may be unreachable; check before sourcing a file.
- Three Unstained draughts are instant-use (no drink animation), so the HONEY_DRINK call is their only feedback.
