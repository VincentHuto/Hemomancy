# Sound audit: Saints: Hemorath, Putriciel, shared kit

Part of the [2026-11-13 sound audit](README.md). Search links open Freesound filtered to CC0.

## Notes

- Hemorath = liquid blood, hematic iron, bone (drinking, chains of debt, spikes, gutters). Putriciel = mould, mud, roots and an iron censer in the rot phases, then Flammeus fire only in the cleansing interval. Keep the two chains apart: Hemorath's is a heavy debt chain, Putriciel's a lighter censer chain.
- Putriciel is silent by design (narration only), yet his hurt sound borrows entity.fungling.hurt (a creature squeak) and his rot ambient borrows entity.erythromycelium_eruptus.ambient; both should become non-vocal Putriciel events (mould tearing, sac creak). His death sound is rite.subject_sink.
- Other borrowed mod events in this slice that should get Saint-specific ids: entity.vesper.grab_bite (Gorge start), entity.naeglerophaeon.drain (Marrow Siphon), item.blood_vial.inject (Raking Claw hit), entity.mycophant.ambient (Rot Breath), entity.mycophant.summon (Sac Swell commit), entity.mycophant.hurtother (Bloom of Rot pulses), entity.stained_priestess.flare (Cauterising Lane), item.living_torch.breath_loop (Purging Sweep, a loop file fired as a one-shot).
- Putriciel's body fire plays twice: a FIRE_AMBIENT every BODY_FIRE_SOUND_EVERY ticks (PutricielEntity:470) on top of getAmbientSound returning FIRE_AMBIENT in fire stages (1453). One looping body-fire event would replace both.
- Putriciel's step and new-pool sounds each stack two vanilla sounds per event (1473+1474, 952+953); one authored file each would be cleaner.
- mausoleum.hemorath.heartbeat and rite.ambient.heartbeat (used by beginThirstDrag) and rite.segment_seal / rite.wave_clear all alias the same Warden heartbeat file.
- EndlessHourManip (Velorum) and UnclosingEyeManip (Seraphae) sit in this slice's package; their briefs follow those Saints' glass and white-light palettes, not Hemorath's or Putriciel's.
- Shared Saint code (SaintTrialEntity, SaintRise, SaintCombatController) plays no sound at all; the rise, staggers and the enraged wake are silent for all four Saints.
