## The finale in his Chamber (2026-11-18)

The owner's ruling of 2026-11-18 moved the whole finale into the Ringmaster's Chamber of Will. It is staged on a copy of
the pavilion and only looks like the real big top coming apart. The spec is
`superpowers/specs/2026-11-18-ringmaster-finale-design.md` and the plan is `superpowers/plans/2026-11-18-ringmaster-finale.md`.
The code is in `common/encounter/ordeal/finale/`. The old real-world finale (RAFTERS, CAROUSEL and DESCENT on the real
site, the troupe-or-anchors objective, the anchors and severing on the carousel, Sleight, Knife Board, the Cue and
Strings Cut, the yield and collapse beats) is deleted. Its code and tests are in
`removed_assets/2026-11-18-ringmaster-finale-v1/`. Calls marked **(builder)** are the builder's, awaiting the owner's look.

**Beginning it.** The finale is still begun from his dialogue at the real pavilion (`circus_begin_finale`). The gate is
unchanged: Degree 4, one school report (an Unstained Liberator: the Understudy's report), and the attention act. There
is no route choice before it any more, so there is no Succession or Liberation option, no warning and no repair. A route
reads `NEUTRAL` until the finale ends, then `SUCCESSION_COMPLETE` or `LIBERATION_COMPLETE`. An old save's pre-fight
choice reads as `NEUTRAL`. A site that cannot be claimed says why first, as the old finale did
(`hemomancy.circus.finale.busy` or `.settled`, `RingmasterFinaleDirectorRules.siteRefusalKey`); then
`RingmasterFinaleDirector.refusal` answers a player it cannot take: already in an ordeal, part of them elsewhere, no
Chamber of Will in the world. A settled ring's Ringmaster offers no finale.

**The crossing.** Under his opening scene (`circus_ringmaster`, 60 ticks, `FinaleRules.INTRO_TICKS`), the player is moved
to the matching spot and facing on a copy of `crimson_troupe_pavilion.nbt`. The copy sits on the ordeal grid in his
Chamber (`FinaleStage`; template entities are ignored, so nothing piles up). The client holds the last real frame across
the move (`FinaleCrossingClientState`, `FinaleCrossingPacket`) so no cut shows. While the owner is inside, the real
Ringmaster, troupe and carousel are frozen and cannot be harmed (`RingmasterFinaleDirector.holds`; the finale troupe
through `holdsArenaOf`, 2026-11-18 fix pass). Another player who speaks to him is told `hemomancy.circus.finale.elsewhere`.
An unload of the real site releases nothing. The finale troupe standing on the real ring is raised on the copy as frozen
stand-ins at their mapped places (`FinaleStandIns`), so nobody is missing across the crossing; they are hauled off on
their strings (`PuppetStrings.unspool`) as his opening ends **(builder)**. Every copy on a stage (his, the carousel's, a
stand-in) that no running finale owns removes itself when it ticks.

**The merge.** His real Chamber render is projected onto the copied tent's own block faces, so the tent is made of his
Chamber: nearly solid at the opening, more and more see-through as the fight goes on (owner, 2026-11-18 fourth pass:
not an imitation; the Chamber's own vessels, capillaries, neural structures, nebula and membrane pulse, as seen in the
Chamber). `FinaleVeilRenderer`, drawn by `FinaleMergeRenderer` at AFTER_TRANSLUCENT_BLOCKS, finds every exposed face of
the pavilion's blocks inside the stage's bounds (each box of a block's shape, on each side not flush against a solid
neighbour: canvas, walls, galleries, seating, poles, rigging and floor) once per chunk section and again on a block
change, leaving out the ring (no face whose middle lies within `FinaleRules.RING_RADIUS` 9 of the carousel and below
`RING_HEIGHT` 9) and blocks that give light, which stay his stage lights **(builder)**. `ChamberDomePortal.project`
then, each frame the veil is up:
1. renders the Chamber sky through its own `ChamberThemeEffects.renderSky`, camera-centred as in the Chamber, into an
   offscreen target the size of the screen with its own depth (its alpha filled to opaque, as the Chamber's own dark);
2. draws the face mesh into the stencil only: depth-tested LEQUAL against the scene with a polygon offset, no colour,
   no depth write, so only pixels where a tent face is the nearest thing seen are marked (open air, the sky past canvas
   openings and anything in front of a face stay unmarked);
3. lays the offscreen sky over the marked pixels at `RingmasterFinaleClientData.veilStrength`, the scene's depth
   untouched.

The theme projected is `ChamberSkyThemeRegistry.RINGMASTER_VEIL` **(builder)**: the shared Chamber layers at the Will
default's full counts (capillary web 1, blue veins 2, blood vessels 2, neural structures 3, nebula, membrane pulse,
untinted) under his crimson-black skybox and his nebula colours. His own `THEME_RINGMASTER` was tried first: its shared
layers are few (2, 0, 2, 1) and his canvas, strings and shafts cover them, so on the tent it read as stripes on stripes
rather than as the familiar Chamber. The strength follows `FinaleRules.veilStrength`: none while the copy passes for the
real tent; rising over the opening (`VEIL_OPENING_TICKS` 100) to `VEIL_OPEN` 0.95 at full health; thinning with his
health to `VEIL_HALF` 0.55 as he comes down and `VEIL_LAST` 0.25 at the end, which the kneel holds. After the projection,
`FinaleTheatre` draws stage lights, sweeping spotlights and a phantom audience on the seats, and the true selves of the
professors this player has passed, as client-only stand-ins at their stations replaying their gestures every 90
ticks, all outside the ring's column. A stand-in hides while its real echo is on the ring. **(builder)**: the projected
theme, the strength values, the theatre.

**Phase 1, the rafters (100% to 50% health).** He blinks between authored perches (constants in `FinaleStage` and

**Phase 2, he comes down (below 50%).** A 50-tick reveal leap from the rafters drops him onto the ring in his true self

He then walks the ring:

The bat swarms (`FinaleSwarm`) wake in phase 2. Four roosts of three swoop every 160 ticks, each bite taking 25 mL
through `BloodAccounting` (a player without active blood takes a 3-damage bite instead), and carry it to him. Each
blood delivered charges him 20, up to 100, and a charged move costs 50. A bat killed before it reaches him denies the
charge. **(builder)**: the charged moves, the swarm numbers, the charged look (glow motes and dripping cells).

**The kneel and the ending.** The blow that would kill him makes him kneel instead (`FinaleRules.kneelCatches`). After
a 30-tick settle he says `hemomancy.circus.finale.line.kneel`, which names both ways out. The first time, a prompt shows
for 140 ticks in the top part of the screen, below the boss bar (`FinaleChoicePrompt`, seen once per player).
- **Kill him** with the owner's own blow after the settle: Liberation. Any other blow is absorbed.
- **Draw his blood off** with held Blood Absorption (`RingmasterAbsorption`, 100 progress): Succession. This needs
  Succession's earned requirements: 1000 acclimation, the three Succession acts (challenge bits 2 to 4), active blood,
  and not Unstained (D-31). Lacking them, his blood does not rise and he says `hemomancy.circus.finale.line.refused` at
  most every 80 ticks. The kill is still open.

Only a blow the owner deals with their own hand or cast kills him (`FinaleRules.ownBlow`: caused and dealt by the
owner); their arrow in flight, hound or bound body never does, and whatever was fighting him stands down as he kneels.
The ending is latched and applied on the next server tick (`RingmasterFinaleDirector.finish`), never inside the blow.
The player is returned and the tent snaps back to real. `CircusFinaleSettlement.apply` then lays the ending on the real
site's saved state and tells the player the ending (`hemomancy.circus.finale.complete.*`). The real cast reads that
outcome from the site whenever it next ticks (`CircusRingmasterEntity.settleFromSite`, the finale troupe through
`CircusPavilionSavedData.isRuinedRingAt`), since the site is usually unloaded by the time the finale ends; a cast already
loaded takes it at once:
- **Kill:** the ruin overlay, Thread Ripper (or its owed memory), the troupe leaves, the real captives are freed, he
  dies, and the faculty stay.
- **Absorb:** the Ringmaster Pattern, the tenancy banner with the player's name, and the real Ringmaster retired on his
  perch. He stays the trade speaker and the conductor teacher with his retired lines
  (`hemomancy.circus.ringmaster.retired.*`).

**Failure.** As with the ordeals, a killing blow on the player is caught and they are thrown out of his Chamber with
nothing dropped. The site is released and the finale can be begun again. A logout sends the player home the same way.
A player who leaves his Chamber by any other way once across (a teleport, a command, a visit's exit) for 40 ticks
(`RingmasterFinaleDirectorRules.AWAY_LIMIT`) has left the finale: it breaks off the same way. A login after a crash
mid-finale reads the saved finale marker (site level, origin, rotation and stage): the player goes home, the site is
released for them, and the stage's copies and blocks are cleared.

**His lines** are chat in the troupe's "says" format, not boss-scene subtitles **(builder)**: `finale.line.opening`,
`.seam`, `.reveal`, `.cascade`, `.pull`, `.kneel`, `.refused` and `.killed`, written to his sheet in `docs/NPC_VOICES.md`.

**Review.** `runCircusReviewClient -PcircusReviewOnly=finale,finale_kill,finale_absorb` films the fight, the kill and the
absorbing in one run. The required GameTests in `crimson_troupe_validation` (`CircusRingmasterGameTests`) cover the finale gate
(attention act and pact), the frozen real site's refusal line, and both endings settled through
`CircusFinaleSettlement.apply`. The GameTest world has no Chamber, so the fight itself is proven only on film.

Sound (D-36): `entity.circus_ringmaster.phase_change`, `.finale_collapse`, `.hurt`, `entity.impaled_procession.thread_sever`,
`.anchor_break` and `entity.circus_performer.windup` play the six cue candidates from `tools/sound_audit/candidates/`
(every candidate of an event is a variant until the owner picks). `entity.circus_ringmaster.music` is registered with
no track (an intentionally empty event) until the owner supplies one. The carousel's events use the entity's id,
`impaled_procession`, because `circus_carousel` is a retired name.

## Chamber ordeals: the Strongman pilot (2026-11-17)

A professor's puppet-body ordeal runs on a stage in their Chamber of Will, not in the open (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-chamber-ordeals-pilot-design.md`). The pilot is the Strongman and the Gorebound Hulk; the other six follow once the owner has judged it.

- **The key.** The Strongman hands over a Blood Vial of his own blood with his lesson (`OrdealKey`, a `bloody_vial` whose custom data is exactly `entity_type` `hemomancy:circus_strongman` and `troupe_key`). The Hulk ordeal's brazier takes it as a strict component ingredient, so no other vial fits. After a failed ordeal he gives another when asked, once per in-game day, and never while one is carried or owed; a syringe cannot draw a professor's blood.
- **The hand-off.** When the trial rite would manifest its puppet, `OrdealDirector.begin` draws the caster onto the Strongman's stage (`OrdealStage`, template `ordeal/strongman` from `tools/worldgen/circus/ordeal_stages.py`, at `OrdealRules.stageOrigin`, x 8192 on its own grid apart from the rooms) and manifests the Hulk there as its true self. The rite on the floor waits (`RiteKind.PUPPETEER_TRIAL.admit` answers WAIT, so the caster's absence is not abandonment) and settles from the ordeal's `Outcome`: a win lets it run to its culmination, anything else breaks it. The floor's chunk is held by a ticket while the caster is away. There is no open-world fallback: where no Chamber can hold the ordeal, `PuppeteerTrialRiteController.canBegin` refuses with a line.
- **Real stakes.** The caster fights with their real inventory. A killing blow is caught first among the death-saves (`OrdealDirector.tryCatch` in `DamageStages.deathSaves`): the caster is thrown out with nothing dropped and the rite fails. A logout, a death by other means, the five-minute limit or a broken Focus also end it as a failure; a login after a stop mid-ordeal brings the player home.
- **The professor's Chamber, not the caster's.** The stage claims its player in the Chamber tick ahead of the room logic (`OrdealDirector.tickArenaPlayer`, the Vesper arena's pattern), so none of the caster's own Chamber progression runs for it.
- **The Strongman intervenes.** His true self is a colossus wading round the stage at radius 20.5, wide of the apron, out of reach and invulnerable (`OrdealFlight`, `CircusFacultyEntity.setTrueSelf`). His true self is a waist-up bust rig whose waist is its root at the entity's feet, drawn 3 times over (about 10.7 blocks of torso and head over the apron, texels about a fifth of a block) with the entity sunk so his waist sits just under the apron's top; `TrueSelfRenderer` fades the waist into two soft walls of the Chamber's dark and culls by the drawn size, so only his torso, arms and head rise over the yard from any angle. At rest he leans in over the ring with his hands held out over it, breathing, his hands closing now and then. His own arms stay outside the ring and make each motion where he stands, while spectral copies of his arm, 1.6 times larger and drawn as translucent red light with his veins burning (`CircusTrueStrongmanModel.renderGhosts`, a layer of `CircusTrueStrongmanRenderer`), manifest over the ring from above his side and strike the gesture's synced point (`CircusFacultyEntity.gestureAt`). Each move is also a gesture timed from its wind-up (`CircusFacultyEntity.gesture`, entity events 80 to 83, sent by the driver on the move's first tick): the weight a right fist raised high and driven down, the heave both fists hauled overhead and brought down, the bar his left arm swept across the ring over the bar's 50 ticks, and holding the line both hands reaching in and hauling back for the hold. His body stays square to the ring's centre (`CircusFacultyEntity.tick` holds it against the turn toward his line of travel) and only his head follows the fighter, up to 60 degrees off his body and 30 degrees down. The entity itself stays a man's size. `OrdealInterventionsDriver` opens his moves by the Hulk's health (`OrdealRules.ramp`: one at a time above two-thirds, two below, three and quicker below a third), each telegraphed through a 20-tick wind-up on the ring's plate as a ground hazard he owns (one `BossGroundHazardEntity` kind per move, `OrdealInterventionsDriver.KIND_*`), drawn bold by the client's `OrdealTelegraphPass` in thick school-material ribbons (crimson fill, bone rim, the live edge in flame) instead of the Saints' subtle default painter: a dropped weight (radius 2), a ground heave (a ring wave from the rim, jumped), a bar sweep (a bar across the ring at knee height, jumped) and holding the line (the floor drags the caster toward the Hulk for three seconds). Damage 6, 4, 5, none. The moves stand on the top of the ring's plate (the stage row plus one); until 2026-11-17 they were a block low, inside the plate, so their footprints were hidden and any hop cleared the heave and the bar. Every blow is weighted: his gestures gather slowly to a trembling top, drop in two and a half accelerating ticks onto `CircusFacultyEntity.GESTURE_IMPACT` (the wind-up's last tick, when the driver lands it), hold on the floor for eight ticks and recover over eighteen; the landing plays a deep thud and a burst of blood and bone off the plate (the heave all round its rim), a groan of strain plays as he gathers, and `OrdealImpactClientEvents` jolts the camera on the weight and the heave and grinds it while the bar crosses and the line hauls. The floor telegraphs pulse faster and brighter toward the blow (`OrdealTelegraphPass.quickening`). The Hulk fights too (`OrdealHulkMoves`, between its ordinary blows, chosen by range): a hammer (both fists on a disc 2.5 in front of it, 14-tick wind-up, damage 7), a charge (pawing the floor, then a lowered-head rush down a lane at 0.8 a tick for 14 ticks, stopping at the curb, damage 6 and a heavy knockback), a grab (a 3-block reach; held overhead for 14 ticks, then thrown for 5) and a stomp (one leg raised for 18 ticks, then a jumpable ring wave out to 6 blocks, damage 4). ### The other six ordeals (built 2026-11-17, awaiting the owner's look)

#### The Acrobat and the Veinwing Vulture

Built 2026-11-17; every look, number and line below is the builder's call, awaiting the owner's look.

- **Presence.** The Acrobat keeps watch from the rigging (`AcrobatRigging`, `AcrobatChoreography`): their true self hangs by the knees from a trapeze bar 13 blocks over the plate and 12 out from the centre, over the net apron and outside the ring, on two lines of their own blood running up into the dark. The trapeze drifts once round the ring every 80 seconds and swings 1.2 blocks either way along its path every 3.5 seconds; at the start it drops out of the dark over three seconds, and every 25 seconds they let go, tumble across the ring 14 blocks up in two seconds and are caught by the far trapeze. Their head follows the fighter. Drawn two and a half times life size (`AcrobatRigging.SCALE`), about five blocks hanging, head about 8 blocks over the plate: out of reach and invulnerable.
- **What reaches in.** Their rigging, made of their blood: lines and bars of red light (`CircusTrueAcrobatModel.renderGhosts`, translucent crimson with a burning core) and, for the drop, a spectral double of the Acrobat themselves. Their own body stays on the trapeze and makes each motion there, a gesture timed from the move's wind-up (events 80 to 83: 0 the swing, 1 the drop, 2 the snare, 3 the net).
- **Interventions** (`AcrobatOrdealHits`, kinds 4010 to 4013), opened by the Vulture's health as for every professor:

## What it is

The Circus is a specialist Harbinger sect built on puppetry, attention, spectacle and collective mental manifestation. It is a practice and a social identity inside the Hematic Order, not a class, a second degree ladder, a reputation bar or an enemy faction by default (`docs/archive/tracks/circus/01-faction-and-progression.md`, `docs/LORE_REFERENCE.md` 6.3b). Saints keep singular unstable powers; the Circus refines systems other Harbingers already know (C-08).

It is the main Puppeteering school (`docs/HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` 17.1). Seven professors each teach one ordinary artificial body before its existing Cardinal Ordeal; instruction reveals the ordeal recipe, the ordeal unlocks the body, a practical proves mastery (`wiki/Crimson-Troupe.md`):
