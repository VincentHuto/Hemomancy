## Ringmaster finale presentation (2026-11-02, on the boss kit since 2026-11-15)

The Ringmaster (`common/entity/npc/circus/CircusRingmasterEntity`) syncs the finale phase to the client and poses
for it (`CircusRingmasterModel`: crouched on the beam in RAFTERS, presenting on the hub in CAROUSEL, walking and
striking in DESCENT, kneeling or slumping in the closing beat). It holds a `BossBarBinding` with the
`circus_ringmaster` frame (striped big-top canvas, hanging knives), plays the `circus_ringmaster` opening scene when
RAFTERS begins, and a phase card at CAROUSEL and DESCENT. It was first filmed on 2026-11-07 (audit section E).

Since 2026-11-15 (B31, chunk 15 T13 to T15) the finale runs on the generic half of the boss kit (D-18, D-33). His class
stays in `common/entity/npc/circus` and has his row in the one boss definition (`hemomancy:bosses`, read by
`HemoEntityPredicates.isBoss`).
- Opening: a saved `BossIntroWindow` of 60 ticks (`INTRO_TICKS`) at the start of RAFTERS. Knives, the Vampire-Bat
  aggregate, the troupe's `beginFinale` and the bar all wait until it ends (audit B-6). A reload mid-opening carries on.
  RAFTERS still lasts 160 ticks in all (`CircusFinaleRules.RAFTER_TICKS`).
- Moves: each is a `BossMoveTimeline` in `CircusRingmasterMoves`, synced as a move id and tick and replayed on the
  client through `BossReplayClock`; the old entity-event clocks are gone. Each strikes a `BossFootprint`, which the
  hit test uses and `CircusEffects.footprint` draws from the same `hits` test (a bone edge, a crimson body filling
  with the charge). Picking in DESCENT reads `BossBands.STANDARD`. A player he cannot path to (a pillar, a gallery)
  gets knives: the ranged answer.
- Arena: one `CircusArena` record for the ring (radius 7, leash 24 blocks from the carousel, grace 200 ticks), shared
  by the acts and the finale. An act fails at once past the leash (as before); a finale owner past it is told once
  (`hemomancy.circus.finale.leash`) and loses the site after the grace. The troupe is gathered by membership (finale
  cast homed on the ring's ground), not by a 20-block box.
- One phase writer: the carousel only reports itself destroyed; the Ringmaster moves the finale to DESCENT, so
  Liberation's DESCENT card plays too (B-7). Severing a captive and striking an anchor work only in CAROUSEL.
- Bar: in CAROUSEL it names the objective, `hemomancy.circus.finale.bar.troupe` (performers still standing, of the
  cast) or `hemomancy.circus.finale.bar.anchors` (anchors left, of three), and its fill follows it; in DESCENT it
  reads his health.

The moves (NQ-15-4 A). Wind-ups sit on the kit's floors: 12 ticks for a melee or thrown strike, 20 for an area move.

In RAFTERS he throws on a 35-tick beat and every third beat is the Spotlight, the Knife Board, then the Cue. In CAROUSEL
he calls the Cue on alternate 50-tick beats while the troupe stands. Acts 2 to 4 stay timed acts and the mini-boss
ladder, the snake creature and the staff form stay struck (NQ-15-3 A).

The closing beat (CURTAIN, chunk 15 T14, D-36). The blow that would kill opens it, in either route. Succession: he
kneels on the ring, the topper comes off his staff, he says one line (`hemomancy.circus.ringmaster.yield`), and the
Pattern is granted when the 60-tick beat ends, not on the hit tick (`CircusYieldScene`). He stands 30 ticks, then
vaults back to his perch, where he stays the trade speaker and the conductor teacher (NQ-15-11). Liberation: the
threads let go of him one by one over 40 ticks (the `circus_collapse_card`), then the ruin. The beat is a saved phase:
a death, a relog or a chunk unload during it does not release the site, and it settles once its owner is back.

Sound (D-36): `entity.circus_ringmaster.phase_change`, `.finale_collapse`, `.hurt`, `entity.impaled_procession.thread_sever`,
`.anchor_break` and `entity.circus_performer.windup` play the six cue candidates from `tools/sound_audit/candidates/`
(every candidate of an event is a variant until the owner picks). `entity.circus_ringmaster.music` is registered and
played for the finale like Vesper's, with no track (an intentionally empty event) until the owner supplies one. The
carousel's events use the entity's id, `impaled_procession`, because `circus_carousel` is a retired name.

## Troupe telegraphs and lost-Ringmaster recovery (2026-11-07)

Every troupe attack is a `BossMoveTimeline` on the Saint kit's floors (`CircusPerformerRules.MELEE_MOVE` 12 ticks,
`AREA_MOVE` 20; audit B-2): the performer's model coils through the wind-up from an entity event
(`CircusPerformerEntity.windupState`, read by `CircusWindupPose`), the server draws the footprint every wind-up tick
(`CircusEffects.ringFootprint`, `coneFootprint`, `lineFootprint`: a bone edge over a crimson body that fills with the
charge, FX-25), and the blow lands on the commit tick. The
Acrobat marks its vault spot behind the target, the Fire Eater locks and draws its 5-block, 55-degree cone, the Stilt
Walker rings its 3.5-block slam, the Dagger Thrower draws its throw line (three on a fan, which leaves as three throws
three ticks apart at -18, 0 and +18 degrees), and faculty raise an arm over a ground wedge; the Strongman hauls both
fists back over his head. Damage, ranges and attack rates are unchanged (each cooldown is shortened by the move's length).
The Ringmaster's moves are the table above (`CircusRingmasterMoves`); `MeleeAttackGoal` is gone. Circus combat files no longer name a vanilla particle except the Stilt Walker's block debris (B-9);
the Bellows' stage breath in `CircusDemonstrations` is the troupe's ember spark too (FX-27).

Footage fixes (2026-11-14, FX-25 to FX-28): troupe knives are drawn at twice a blood needle's size with a bone wake
(`BloodNeedleGeometry.knife`) and no longer stay in the target as vanilla arrows. The Acrobat's vault snaps on the
client under a covering burst (entity event 72 makes the next position update land at once), and its lunge stops
short of the target. The carousel's pole and frame are hematic iron and its trim and finial bone (`carousel.png`; the
glow layer was already crimson). `light_pavilion.py` keeps the carousel's sweep and sight line clear up to the roof
and moved the two lanterns at (28,5,32) and (36,5,32) out to (24,5,32) and (40,5,32), still on chains; `--check` reads 0
dark cells. The opening scene's skip prompt goes when the view is handed back (`CircusRingmasterScene.skippable`),
sever lines go to the action bar, the perception vignette is a soft near-black theatre edge that widens as the house goes dark, and following
bodies on foot take fanned slots behind their owner, the Hulk a row further back (`BoundSummonBehavior.followOwnerOnFoot`,
for every puppet, not only in the pavilion).

Stuck states (B-4): on Succession `die()` refuses every death but `/kill`, as a Saint does; on Liberation it refuses a
death from the owner's side. Either way an owner's last blow in DESCENT opens the closing beat. Any real loss of the Ringmaster mid-finale (death, discard, chunk unload) releases the site once
with the `hemomancy.circus.finale.ringmaster_lost` line; acts are left to their controller. CAROUSEL counts only the
finale performers still standing, so a troupe emptied by a Liberation run reads as downed and the owner is told
(`hemomancy.circus.finale.empty_troupe`).

## What it is

The Circus is a specialist Harbinger sect built on puppetry, attention, spectacle and collective mental manifestation. It is a practice and a social identity inside the Hematic Order, not a class, a second degree ladder, a reputation bar or an enemy faction by default (`docs/archive/tracks/circus/01-faction-and-progression.md`, `docs/LORE_REFERENCE.md` 6.3b). Saints keep singular unstable powers; the Circus refines systems other Harbingers already know (C-08).

It is the main Puppeteering school (`docs/HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md` 17.1). Seven professors each teach one ordinary artificial body before its existing Cardinal Ordeal; instruction reveals the ordeal recipe, the ordeal unlocks the body, a practical proves mastery (`wiki/Crimson-Troupe.md`):

| Professor | Shape | Degree |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Acrobat | Veinwing Vulture | 3 |
| Dagger Thrower | Marrow Juggler (`marrow_spitter` ids kept) | 3 |
| Stilt Walker | Scarlet Mummer | 4 |
| Strongman | Gorebound Hulk | 4 |
| Beast Tamer | Sanguine Hound | 4 |
| Fire Breather | Cinder Bellows | 4 |
| Retired Understudy | Mnemonist Puppet | 5 |

A Threadkeeper inspects the Crossbar for An Empty Ring and records reports; the Ringmaster keeps the outcome-specific conductor lesson. Side assignments: An Empty Ring, seven practicals, The Wrong Audience (two accounts), The Missing Understudy (four records plus testimony), guest acts paying four Enthralling Filaments with exact saved overflow, collected from a Threadkeeper (`03-puppetry-and-circus-mechanics.md`, "Crimson Troupe school expansion"). A guest act pays once per in-game day across the whole school, not per professor: one `guest.day` key, read from `getDayTime() / 24000`, so sleeping or `/time set` moves it (`CircusSchoolQuests`; the ledger's `ledger.guest.available` line says so since 2026-11-14, D-45). The Threadkeeper's own option, `ask_empty_ring`, opens the An Empty Ring briefing (PT-60). A practical or guest act is refused in Peaceful (`message.hemomancy.circus.school.peaceful`) and ends with the retry line when Peaceful is set or when fewer rehearsal threats live than the exercise needs: two for the Scarlet Mummer and the Cinder Bellows, one for the rest (`CircusPracticalController.targetsNeeded`; PT-59, PT-63).

Acts (D-37, NQ-15-8 A; since 2026-11-15): each taught body has one act the owner fires from the Act entry on the Crossbar radial's inner ring, sent by one rate-limited payload (`PacketPuppetAct`). The act belongs to the Crossbar's selected shape and is performed by its nearest body on that Crossbar. Thread is spent only when the act is performed; an act with nothing to do says so and costs nothing. Acts are not strands and take no strand form. Its practical (three hits on one threat inside 15 ticks) is unchanged.

The perception presentation is the phantom show since 2026-11-16 (`CircusPerceptionOverlay` holds the state and the scan; `client/render/world/circus/` draws it; rules in `PhantomShowRules`). The owner's arc: a newcomer catches unsettling fragments, an attuned visitor sits in the audience of the whole performance. Phantoms are the troupe's own jointed rigs (Understudy, Threadkeeper, Acrobat, Knife Thrower, Fire Eater) posed with no entity behind them and drawn translucent in bone with a crimson rim. Disturbed: up to three seated fragments that hide within 20 degrees of the crosshair and change seat every 240 ticks, a still figure glimpsed on a trapeze or in the ring for 30 ticks in every 400, most string-light bulbs dead, the house barely dimmed, and on the troupe's real NPCs a 6-tick afterimage stutter at most every 120 ticks (the constant shake is gone). Acclimating: a third of the seats filled, heads turning to follow the player, one pale act at a time alternating trapeze and ring, the bulbs warm and uneven, one spotlight sweeping the ring, the house half dark. Attuned: every scanned seat and gallery deck filled and leaning toward the ring, applause on each act's end, an artist swinging on each trapeze in view (up to three) with a ring act cycling acrobat, knife thrower and fire eater, the bulbs steady with a chase on the beat, three spotlights on the ring act and the artists, and the house dark. The marks are read every two seconds within 24 blocks: seating tiers, gallery decks and rails, trapezes, the ring curb (its mean is the ring) and the highest collar of each king and quarter pole, which the string lights swag between; the gallery rails carry their own garlands. The house darkens with a veil in front of the eye drawn before the show's lights (at most 55%), and the fog draws in toward crimson-black, never nearer than 32 blocks; both scale with the vanilla Darkness Effect setting. A phantom is never opaque, fades when the player comes within about two blocks, and fades while it would cover a living creature. A Liberated pavilion's ruin shows only the hollow echo, the Disturbed look with no performers, whatever the stage (`ruined` on `PacketSyncCircusPerception`, from `CircusPavilionSavedData.isRuinWithin`); a Succeeded pavilion's tenancy runs the full show. The stage motes are the mod's own particles: a crimson sporitic spore when Disturbed, a bone will-absorption glow drifting to the player when Acclimating, warm embers drifting down from the bulbs when Attuned. All of it is per viewer and presentation only; the server owns the score and never decides damage, loot or route state from it (`02-location-worldgen-and-perception.md`).

Routes: at 500 the Ringmaster becomes coherent. The attention act (bit 0) can be held from a neutral route; once it is done he offers a neutral exit plus warning-and-confirm Succession or Liberation, and the choice is bit 1. One pre-completion repair is allowed, then removed. Route state is per player; one site-global owner runs an act or finale at a time and the first completed outcome settles that pavilion. Succession runs the remaining acts in order: bit 2 holds the ring against the dolls, bit 3 reads the Dagger Thrower's knife act, bit 4 holds the ring through the carousel pattern (`hemomancy.circus.challenge.begin.<bit>`). Its finale needs 1000 acclimation and all five bits (`CircusRouteRules.ALL_CHALLENGES`); it downs the troupe nonlethally and grants the Ringmaster Pattern (D4 low-damage Conductor: one body slot, +1 shaped-body cap, so it costs no slot of the cap; relays mode/focus once within 32 blocks, never chains). Since 2026-11-15 its office is a group act, Ring: fired from the Crossbar radial, every body of the owner within 32 blocks of the Pattern performs its own act at once (30 thread, 60 s cooldown; the bodies' own costs and cooldowns are not spent). The Pattern is called at the Spindle and is no longer in the Crossbar's transposition ring (`PuppeteerSummonRules.inTranspositionRing`; D-38, NQ-15-9 A). Liberation runs no further acts and may start its finale at once. It makes the troupe lethal; the three failed initiates bound on the Impaled Procession's horses are cut by a plain main-hand right-click from the site owner (`BloodDrunkPuppeteerEntity.mobInteract` calls `CircusCarouselEntity.severCaptive`), which kills the captive and exposes its anchor. A player who already knows Thread Ripper can cut one by casting it at the carousel, the same `severCaptive` call. Thread Ripper never disrupts or unravels Vesper's encounter bodies (`VesperEncounterPuppetEvents.isEncounterPuppet`; its own line `message.hemomancy.thread_ripper.vesper_strings`, D-35). Breaking all three anchors ruins the carousel (six cobwebs placed in air) and opens the lethal descent. Thread Ripper (30 ticks, 24 blocks, 300 mL of blood since 2026-11-15, 12 s cooldown) is Liberation's prize, not its tool: it is a Summa memory, so most Liberators cannot learn it yet and receive the memory item instead; right-click cutting stays (D-35). Both endings grant the cosmetic fire-resistant Ringmaster's Topper (`04-creatures-encounters-and-rewards.md`) through the `PendingDelivery` claim `circus_finale_rewards` (`CircusPlayerProgress.FINALE_REWARDS`), which also owes the Thread Ripper memory when Liberation cannot teach it; the owed count lives in `CircusProgress`. The pact is gated since 2026-11-15 (D-30, D-31, NQ-15-5, NQ-15-6 B; `CircusRouteRules.pactGate`): a Harbinger needs Degree 4 and any one school report on record (An Empty Ring, The Wrong Audience or The Missing Understudy). A player whose Unstained state blocks Harbinger progress (`UnstainedAccessRules.blocksHarbingerProgress`) may take Liberation only, needs The Missing Understudy report and no Degree, cannot repair into Succession and cannot begin a Succession finale. The gate is checked at the pact and again at the finale, so a forged event is refused. The pact options show only when the gate is met; otherwise the Ringmaster's greeting names what is missing (`hemomancy.circus.ringmaster.gate.<needs_degree | needs_report | needs_degree_and_report | needs_understudy | unstained>`). First talks keep their 50 points. The Ringmaster uses the real troupe, knife pressure and a Vampire-Bat aggregate, never Vesper's catalogue. A blow from a body the owner holds (a non-trial bound puppet, a Blood Cur, their projectiles, the Mnemonist Puppet's replay) counts as the owner's on the Ringmaster, the performers and the carousel (`CircusPavilionStateRules.actingFor`). During an act or finale the troupe's strikes, the Fire Breather's cone, the Stilt Walker's slam, faculty swings, troupe knives and the staff blow go through `ManipulationCombatHelper.canHarmFor` and land only on the owner and the owner's bodies (`CircusPerformerEntity.canStrike`, `CircusRingmasterEntity.canStrike`); a bystander who strikes the troupe is told once per act on the action bar, however many troupe members they strike (`hemomancy.circus.finale.solo`, `CircusPavilionStateRules.refuseBystander`). The finale stays solo (D-34). A refused claim says why: `finale.busy` while another player holds the ring, `finale.settled` once the site is decided, `finale.no_route` to a visitor with no route; `finale.not_ready` is Succession's alone (PT-61). A destroyed Ringmaster leaves his site vacant, a saved flag the Waybill reads as finished (PT-62, the Waybill half of D-36).

Fauna: Peacock Jumping Spiders (attention act), Vampire Bats (manifested aggregate), Luminal Cicada (non-hostile flash on a 5 s cooldown, Specimen Jar capture) are animals, not puppets. Scarlet Serpents and Prism Cuttles are natural Hemomancy-reactive animals; two jarred Cuttles in the pavilion are the Chromatist reference (`04-...`).

Trade: the Ringmaster and the eight performer speakers (`circus_acrobat`, `circus_fire_eater`, `circus_knife_thrower`, `circus_stilt_walker`, `circus_strongman`, `circus_beast_tamer`, `circus_threadkeeper`, `circus_understudy`) are the `CIRCUS` side in `NpcTradeSides`. Only the Ringmaster takes curiosities (`npc_trade/circus_ringmaster/curiosity.json`, `#hemomancy:trade/curiosity`, graded by `rarity`); each performer has one act-themed items card (`npc_trade/circus_acrobat/rigging_line.json` and siblings). None takes blood or money. The Ringmaster also takes a banked spectacle (`NpcTradeWant.Act`, recorded through `NpcTradeActs.witnessed` by `CircusPerformanceController` when a living non-spectator player finishes an act). Challenge bits are per player and never reset, so a player banks at most four spectacles ever: four on Succession, one (the attention act) on Liberation. Trading needs no Degree and no active blood; no trade counts as a lesson (`docs/archive/tracks/npc_trade/05-npc-catalogues.md`, `06-rapport-rotation-and-demand.md`).
