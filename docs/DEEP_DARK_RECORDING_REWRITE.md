This transcript and the in-game spoken captions match the shipped OGG recordings in `assets/hemomancy/sounds/antecedent/`. Keep the recorded wording and existing caption cue timing together. Any future dialogue rewrite must update the voice recordings and captions in the same change.

## Severed Record

Unknown Recorder: Lower district. Record nineteen.

Unknown Recorder: The growth to the west has stopped.

Distant Voice: Stopped?

Unknown Recorder: It has not died. It stopped.

Unknown Recorder: The catalysts still take the dead. The listeners still answer movement. Nothing there has changed.

Unknown Recorder: But they do not answer one another the way they did.

Unknown Recorder: Do not move.

Unknown Recorder: The cut held.

Distant Voice: Then why are they still alive?

Unknown Recorder: I don't know.

Unknown Recorder: We cut the voice.

Unknown Recorder: The flesh remained.

## Lower District Survey

Unknown Recorder: Lower district survey. The growth takes the dead. The listeners answer movement, but do not go beyond the black floor.

Distant Voice: Do not carry the growth upward. Keep the dead elsewhere. Mark the last clean stair.

## The Quieting

Unknown Recorder: Do not ring bells below the third stair. No iron wheels. Wrap the road. Walk softly.

Distant Voice: If the floor answers twice, leave. If it answers three times, do not return for those below.
