# Fungal Gardens Purpose & Mausoleum Structure

Oct 27, 2026 · @Vi

The two questions share an answer if the places are linked: the Gardens hold what was taken, and the Mausoleums hold what was kept. Each place should be incomplete without the other, following the "everything points somewhere else" rule and the preservation-and-witness identity.

## 1. Why be in the Fungal Gardens

**Core premise: the incursion doesn't destroy, it digests and keeps.** Everything it has consumed still exists inside it in some form. That makes the Gardens the largest archive in the mod, held by something that doesn't want to give it back.

### Depth layers

| Layer | What's there | Why go |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fringe | Fruiting growth, blooms, rendering pools | Materials and alchemical reagents; routine runs |
| Archive | Fungal facsimiles of everything consumed: mobs, plants, structures, people | Recovery: lost memories, Saint fragments, extinct variants |
| Core | The site of the Severance, "the part they left alive" | Antecedent endgame, Mycophant ending, the truth of the cut |

### Per-path reasons

- **Apotheosis: a home.** Plant a heart-node that grows into a fungal sanctum, a Fane equivalent that exists only in the Gardens. It serves as respawn point and workshop, and is the only place they can molt: re-shape morphology or realign tendencies.
- **Silentium: recollection.** The hivemind tracks and protects the Saints' powers for its own study, so the memory needed to recreate a Saint's gift is held in the Archive. Silentium players must possess a host to recollect it.
- **Everyone on the Harbinger path: the Core.** The narrative pull. The Antecedent Inquiry already points there, so the dimension is where that thread pays off.

The Gardens are designed for Harbinger-path players only. Unstained and unaligned players are not a target audience. A possible far-future Unstained invasion rite is parked until Unstained gameplay is more developed.

## 2. Mausoleum structure

### The Saints

All four were infected hemomancers of great power, feared and revered. Hemorath is the exception: his successors and students, fearing they would be lost to bloodlust as he was, gradually formed the structured Harbinger order. The other three were never directly associated with the Harbingers, whether because they predate the order or lived far from it; that is left to interpretation. Each unlocked a singular gift, whether on purpose or not. By this late stage the player has mastered most mundane powers, and their task is to rediscover the Saints' gifts for themselves.

| Saint | Tendencies | Gift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hemorath | Animus, Ferric | Eternal bloodlust |
| Seraphae | Lux, Ductilis | Cursed, uncontrollable healing |
| Putriciel | Mortem, Flammeus | Consecration corruption |
| Velorum | Congeatio, Umbral | Perfection through stillness |

### Per-Saint mausoleums

| Saint | Core mechanic | Signature hazard | Enraged (forced) fight | Gift |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hemorath | Blood basins built to ration his absorption | Player blood drains toward him, faster with depth; full basins slow the drain | He begins refilling, swelling toward his bloated form as the fight goes on | Passive siphon: blood answers your presence |
| Seraphae | Chains and containment anchors | Any healing causes overgrowth (swelling, involuntary flinches, damage over time); broken anchors let her radiance reach deeper | She reaches to heal you; every touch makes it worse | Healing pulse that also mutates whatever it touches |
| Putriciel | Cycling rot phases and burn phases | Rhythm puzzle: fungal spread alternates with flame purges sweeping the halls; apotheosis hyphae lengthen the rot phase | No lucid burn windows; the man beneath never surfaces | A purge that burns infection, then lets it regrow stronger |
| Velorum | The frozen congregation | Stillness kills: lingering slows, then freezes; his Vigil Station inverts into one where you cannot stop. Devotee poses mark safe paths and answers | Devotees thaw and fight as a congregation | Arrested circulation: freeze a target, mind still aware |
|  |  |  |  |  |

The ring door draws its blood toward Hemorath, making the shared door mechanic his signature.

**Walk the Saint's life backwards.** Players enter at the Saint's death and move toward who they were. The chamber is where it all began, not only where the body lies. Rooms are called Stations.

**Layout (jigsaw generation):** antechamber, three Station slots, nave, ring door, chamber.

All four mausoleums share this layout and the same room types. Each differs in its challenges, traps and aesthetics, drawn from its Saint.

### Station types

Each Station is drawn from a deed in the Saint's life.

| Station | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Witness | A memory replays as an echo re-enactment (Echo Phials, Curor Lens). The player must observe correctly; something in the scene answers the next room. |
| Litany | An Abocipher inscription must be decoded to find the right order of offerings. |
| Penance | The Saint's martyrdom as a combat or hazard gauntlet. |
| Vigil | Stillness under threat. Silentium players have an edge; everyone can do it. |

### The ring door

The door's three rings are three locks, one per Station, and they track progress. Each completed Station fills one ring. Once all three are filled, the Silentium projects into the door and it opens.

**D7 / D8 split.** Any D7 Archon can explore a mausoleum and complete its Stations. Projecting into the door to wake the Saint is reserved for the D8 Silentium; forcing it open is reserved for the D8 Apotheos.

### Skipping Stations (apotheosis)

- An apotheosis player who reaches the door can brute-force it at any time, skipping every Station.
- The enraged Saint carries the power of every Station the player didn't complete. Skip the Penance and it fights with its martyrdom; skip the Vigil and it never pauses.
- Enrage is the sum of what the player refused to see, not a flat difficulty bump.
- In reverse, each Station a Silentium completes reveals a tell or removes a phase from the fight.

### Linking the Gardens and the Mausoleums

Recreating a Saint's gift takes two things, crafted together at the Somatic Loom. Each path finds one easy and the other hard.

| Ingredient | Where | Silentium | Apotheosis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hallowed residue | Saint's mausoleum, after the fight | Easier: Stations completed, Saint fought as designed | Harder: forced entry, enraged Saint, hallowed ground burns |
| Recollection (the memory, or the reagent for it) | Gardens' Archive | Harder: possession rite, focus counter, Mind Broken risk | Easier: free to come and go |

Thematically, the hivemind keeps the Saints' powers for its own study: it guards them from Silentium players and simply gives them to apotheosis players. In multiplayer, the two paths have something to trade.

### Reward

Residue plus recollection, woven at the Somatic Loom, lets the player rediscover that Saint's gift as a new power. A forced Saint yields only fragments.
