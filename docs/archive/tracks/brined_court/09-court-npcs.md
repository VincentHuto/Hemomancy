## How the models are built (SETTLED 2026-10-29, P1)

**Not a vanilla humanoid.** Court members follow the Hemomancy NPC body plan, the jointed rig the Annetta and Stained Priestess generators build (`tools/model_export/annetta_knowles.py`, `stained_priestess.py`), so they can kneel, swim, reach, brace and gesture:

- Hierarchy: `root → hips` (hip joints, y 12) `→ body` (waist, y 10) `→ head, right_arm, left_arm`; `hips → right_leg, left_leg`. A lean or twist pivots at the waist and carries the shoulders and head, so the neck, shoulders and hips never part.
- Jointed limbs: `right_arm → right_forearm` with the elbow four pixels below the shoulder pivot; `right_leg → right_shin` with the knee halfway down the leg. Same on the left.
- Each lower segment has an inset filler reaching back into its joint, so a bent elbow or knee closes over instead of opening a gap.
- Standard humanoid box sizes, with clothing as thin panels rather than inflated shells, at 16 px per block.

Build it once as a shared rig helper, `tools/model_export/court_humanoid.py`, that every Court role's generator calls and then dresses. Each role generator writes its Java model, `.bbmodel`, preview PNG and an `_anim.java` with its idle, walk, swim and talk poses, so animation lives in the rig and not in hand-tuned vanilla arm swings.

**Strain growths on the rig.** Track 3's attachments are drawn by `MorphlingMutationLayer` from a strain and a stage. Court NPCs use the same attachment models, fed from the NPC's house and role instead of an equipped item. The player model has no forearm or shin, so add an anchor adapter: on the player, `ARMS` and `LEGS` attach to the arm and leg; on a jointed NPC, forearm growths (Holdfast roots, Marrowplume clubs, Purse finlets) attach to the forearm and calf growths to the shin, so they bend with the joint. Track 3 should tag each attachment part as upper-limb or lower-limb so the adapter knows where it goes.

## Entity design (PROPOSED)

- `common/entity/npc/court/CourtMemberEntity` (abstract, extends `PathfinderMob`, implements `ProgressionDialogueNpc` and `ProtectedNpc` like the Voyager), with `CourtHouse` and `CourtRole` fields. Subclasses only where behaviour differs (anchored, sleeper, swimmer, Kindled elder).
- **Water:** members breathe underwater and never drown; swimmers use water navigation, shore roles amphibious navigation. Anchored roles don't path at all (a fixed anchor point, turn-to-face only).
- **Combat:** `ProtectedNpc` for now (SETTLED 2026-10-29, P2). Court members can't be hurt by players and don't fight them. They defend against hostiles near home where the role allows (Tidewarden, Edge-swimmer), targeting through `ManipulationCombatHelper.canHarmFor`.
- **Spawning:** with the structure, by a spawner like `ActiveHarbingerVoyagerNpcSpawner` reading the template's data markers. Persistent; tied to their structure (a home position and a leash). A member killed by something else (a mob, the void) is replaced on a slow timer only while no player is nearby, capped at the structure's count.
- **Shared Wake school (SETTLED 2026-10-29, P3):** a group of 4–6 Edge-swimmers with a leader, moving through Pelagic Ocean along a wide loop. They spawn from nursery grounds and also naturally, rarely, capped at one school per 1024×1024 region and never within 256 blocks of a Seat.
- **No other natural spawns.** Court members never appear outside their structures and the school.
