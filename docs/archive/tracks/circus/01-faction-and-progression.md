# Circus Faction and Progression

## Identity

**SETTLED:** The Circus is an eccentric specialist sect within the Hematic Order. It is a practice, community, and social identity, not a new class, a replacement degree ladder, or an enemy faction by default.

Its members specialize in the instant when attention, expectation, posture, color, sound, and imposed movement cause another person to surrender control of the next decision. They use ordinary Harbinger principles at an advanced collective scale. That makes them unsettling and morally disputed without making them heretics.

The Blood Drunk Puppeteer remains the central warning: a puppeteer who became the puppet. Circus members may disagree about whether destroying a faltering Blood Drunk is mercy or whether Commandeering one can be called rescue. This is an internal ethical fracture, not proof that the whole sect is hostile.

## Progression position

**SETTLED:** The Mnemonist introduces the Circus at Degree 4, after the player has practical exposure to puppetry.

The introduction should:

1. Check whether the player has already discovered the location.
2. If undiscovered, frame the Circus as dangerous specialists who can teach what the Mnemonist cannot.
3. If already discovered, acknowledge that fact and direct the player back rather than pretending it is new.
4. Avoid granting a parallel Circus rank, reputation bar, or hidden mental-versus-physical alignment score.

`HarbingerMnemonistDialogueTrees` now introduces the pavilion at Degree 4. Discovery is server-authoritative and persisted on the player; early discovery changes the Mnemonist branch instead of duplicating it, and a reissuable Scarlet Waybill provides the return route.

## Relationship to other Harbinger groups

| Group | Specialization | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main Order | Broad instruction and degree progression | Introduces standard systems; does not become obsolete. |
| Circus | Puppetry, attention, decoys, light, sound, mental manifestation | Advanced versions of known systems; no separate degree ladder. |
| Sea specialists | Morphlings, bodily symbiosis, adapted sea life | Separate later expansion. |
| Mnemonic-whale Voyagers | Memory, field research, whale remains and travel | Distinct subgroup; not the entire sea faction. |
| Saints | Singular, unstable powers discovered by one historical practitioner | Circus techniques must not consume this design space. |

## Internal roles

These remain working social roles, not registrations or progression classes:

- **Ringmaster:** coordinates attention and multiple tethers. The title describes a job, not authority over unrelated Harbingers.
- **Chromatist:** maintains shifting cloth, puppet skins, masks, and false visual tells.
- **Charmer:** studies serpent display, target fixation, threat posture, and expectation.
- **String-Surgeon:** maintains Crossbars, Spindles, artificial joints, and bodies strained by long-term tethering.
- **Animal tamer, juggler, acrobat, flame eater:** recognizable performance jobs expressed through existing powers and staff forms.
- **Blood Drunk:** a diagnosis or slur for reversed control, never a glamorous promotion.

## Relationship routes

**SETTLED:** The Circus is not hostile by default, and cooperative and antagonistic outcomes should serve different player motivations.

**IMPLEMENTED:** Neither proximity nor ordinary combat silently chooses a route. At 500 acclimation the Ringmaster explains Succession and Liberation through separate warning nodes; the player must confirm one. Route state and prerequisites are per player, while one site-global owner may run an act or finale at a time and the first completed outcome permanently settles that pavilion. A player may switch their chosen route once before completion; the repair option disappears after use. Completed routes cannot be repaired.

## Reward principles

- **IMPLEMENTED:** Succession grants the Ringmaster Pattern, a Degree-4 low-damage Conductor summon.
- **IMPLEMENTED:** Liberation grants Thread Ripper, an anti-tether manipulation usable outside the pavilion.
- Both routes grant the cosmetic, fire-resistant Ringmaster's Topper.
- The hostile reward must not demand adoption of the playstyle the player just rejected.
- Neither route may grant a Saint's unique power or imitate Vesper's broad player-system mastery.
- Rewards are granted only by authoritative finale completion and use their existing duplicate-safe unlock paths.

## Acceptance criteria for a progression design

- A Degree 4 player can receive an authored Mnemonist lead.
- A player who discovers the Circus early receives acknowledgment rather than duplicate discovery.
- The location remains visitable without choosing immediate hostility.
- No new class, degree track, or generic reputation framework is introduced.
- Cooperative and hostile outcomes are both intelligible before their irreversible point.
- Multiplayer ownership and persistence are specified before route state is coded.
