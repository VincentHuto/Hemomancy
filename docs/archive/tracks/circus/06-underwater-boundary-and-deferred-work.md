# Underwater Boundary and Deferred Work

## Separation rule

**SETTLED:** Circus and underwater content are separate specialist developments.

- Circus: mental manifestation, puppetry, attention, decoys, displacement, light, sound, and collective perception.
- Underwater: bodily adaptation, biological symbiosis, Morphlings, and adapted sea life.
- Mnemonic whales: memory-oriented exception handled by a distinct Voyager/whaling subgroup, not the identity of the entire sea faction.

Prism Cuttle imagery may bridge the themes through chromatophores and visual deception. That bridge does not merge their progression, structures, factions, or release schedules.

## Release order

1. **Initial release/pass:** modest Circus integration that strengthens existing puppetry and adds a discoverable faction identity.
2. **First major post-release update / 1.1:** Saints.
3. **Later expansion:** broader underwater faction, biology, structures, and progression.

Do not pull the full sea expansion into Circus work merely because a candidate organism could support illusion or spectacle.

## Deferred underwater pool

**DEFERRED:**

- sea-cucumber forms for different body regions;
- siphonophores and man o' wars;
- giant moss balls and gas-filled organisms used for oxygen or buoyancy;
- bone, coral, and whale-fall material culture;
- deep-sea clothing, armor, tools, settlements, and progression;
- detailed cannibalism or bodily-transformation horror;
- a full sea-specialist NPC hierarchy or degree-adjacent track.

These are planning inputs, not commitments. Alyssa's existing sea-cucumber forms remain art references for the later biological pass.

## Existing systems to preserve

- The current Erythrocoral ocean ecosystem already has strong coverage; new sea life must fill a specific deep-sea or symbiotic role.
- Prism Cuttle remains a naturally spawning aquatic creature unless a later design explicitly changes its ecology.
- Morphlings remain the primary bodily-symbiosis system. Do not create a sea-only attachment framework.
- Harbinger Voyagers and mnemonic-whale content retain their own field-research and memory identity.
- Minecraft's loose early-Renaissance material level remains the equipment boundary; avoid modern diving technology.

## Bridge contracts

The initial Circus pass may create only narrow future seams:

- dialogue acknowledging Chromatist study of Prism Cuttle behavior;
- cloth, mask, armor, or puppet visuals inspired by chromatophores;
- field notes that distinguish visual mimicry from biological symbiosis;
- a non-progression reference to Voyagers or sea specialists.

It must not add underwater route state, sea reputation, new ocean structures, deep-sea traversal, or unfinished organism mechanics.

## Gates for the later underwater design

Before implementation, settle:

- faction culture and its relationship to the main Order and Voyagers;
- location type and structure/worldgen distribution;
- which organisms are ecology, equipment, mounts, Morphlings, or encounters;
- how underwater breathing, buoyancy, pressure, and travel interact with vanilla and current Hemomancy systems;
- progression timing after Saints;
- whether cannibalism appears at all and what player-facing purpose it serves.

Until those gates close, this document is a boundary that prevents accidental scope expansion, not an underwater implementation specification.
