# Hemomancy — Mnemonic Taxonomy, Noetic/Thelemic Memory, and Epistemic Pathing Brief

> **Status:** Design consolidation / implementation planning brief  
> **Purpose:** Preserve the complete design discussion around replacing the colloquial “muscle memory” terminology, formalizing Hematic Memory taxonomy, strengthening the Mnemonist/Alchemist relationship, and creating a staged path for implementation without overloading the player with philosophy.  
> **Canon status:** **Not yet a canonical replacement document.** Treat this as a proposed doctrine and implementation map to be reconciled into `LORE_REFERENCE.md`, `HEMOMANCY_REFERENCE.md`, dialogue, items, UI, and mechanics in deliberate stages.  
> **Primary design rule:** **Complex foundation, simple facade.** The metaphysics may be deep; the ordinary player-facing vocabulary should remain compact.

---

# 1. Executive Summary

The existing “muscle memory” terminology is too modern and colloquial for Hemomancy’s established register. It communicates the intended mechanic, but it sounds comparatively cheeky beside terms such as **Somatic Loom**, **Mnemonist**, **Cardinal Rite**, **Qliphoth**, **Voluntas**, **Hematic Covenant**, and **Chamber of Will**.

The proposed replacement is a nested mnemonic taxonomy:

| Layer | Preferred term | Function |
|---|---|---|
| Umbrella | **Mnemonics** / **Hematic Memories** | Any blood-carried remembered instruction, faculty, route, or encoded pattern |
| Intellectual expression | **Noetic Memories** | Memories apprehended and expressed through intellect, understanding, study, interpretation, or deliberate formulation |
| Volitional / embodied expression | **Thelemic Memories** | Memories expressed through will becoming bodily action, replacing the current “muscle memory” register |
| Deep doctrine | **Mneme / Nous / Thelēma** | Memory preserves; intellect comprehends; will enacts |

The core formulation is:

> **Mneme keeps. Nous knows. Thelēma acts.**

Or, in fuller Hematic language:

> **Memory preserves the pattern. Intellect gives the pattern form. Will gives the pattern consequence.**

This taxonomy should **not** become three new progression meters or three mandatory UI systems. The player should normally need to understand only:

- **Hematic Memory / Mnemonic**
- **Noetic Memory**
- **Thelemic Memory**

The deeper Mneme–Nous–Thelēma framework belongs primarily in Mnemonist dialogue, Liber passages, advanced lore, the Chamber of Will, and later philosophical interpretation.

---

# 2. Why This Strengthens Hemomancy

This is useful only if it reinforces existing identity rather than adding vocabulary for its own sake.

It succeeds because it consolidates themes Hemomancy already uses:

- blood as inherited or cultivated **memory**;
- manipulations as **remembered instructions** rather than conventional spells;
- the **Somatic Loom** as deliberate mnemonic shaping;
- the **Mnemonist** as a practitioner of blood-memory rather than a generic wizard;
- scars as remembered **routes**;
- the **Chamber of Will** as remembered **space**;
- the late-game conflict between communal fungal cognition and sovereign selfhood;
- the Harbinger tendency system as a human attempt to classify recurring blood-memory behavior.

The concept becomes excessive only if every philosophical layer is surfaced equally to the player.

## Guardrail

Do **not** make the player memorize:

`Mneme -> Nous -> Thelema -> Noetic -> Thelemic -> eight tendencies -> ranks -> scars -> enzymes -> manipulation levels -> etc.`

Instead:

### Common vocabulary
- Hematic Memory / Mnemonic
- Noetic Memory
- Thelemic Memory

### Specialist vocabulary
- Mneme
- Nous
- Thelēma
- Memoria
- Intelligentia
- Voluntas
- epistemic disputes between Alchemists and Mnemonists

The specialist vocabulary should deepen understanding for attentive players without becoming required to operate the systems.

# 3. Philosophical and Linguistic Foundation

The terminology should nod toward real intellectual traditions without pretending Hemomancy reproduces one historical philosophical system exactly.

## 3.1 The Latin triad: *memoria, intelligentia, voluntas*

The closest historical analogue to the proposed three-faculty model is the Augustinian psychological triad:

- **Memoria** — memory
- **Intelligentia / Intellegentia** — understanding or intellect
- **Voluntas** — will

Augustine develops this triad prominently in *De Trinitate*, especially Book X, as interrelated operations of one mind.

Hemomancy should **borrow the architecture, not Christian Trinitarian doctrine wholesale**.

The useful conceptual echo is:

> one subject can retain, understand, and will without becoming three separate subjects.

That maps elegantly to Hematic Memory:

> one mnemonic substrate can be preserved, intellectually apprehended, and volitionally enacted without requiring three separate magical substances.

## 3.2 Greek terminology

The Greek terms are being used as an intentionally adjacent philosophical register:

- **Mnēmē / Mneme** — memory, remembrance
- **Nous** — mind, intellect, understanding; hence **noetic**
- **Thelēma** — will, wish, desire, intention; hence **Thelemic**

These terms should not be presented in-game as though an ancient Greek school historically taught the exact Hemomancy triad. They are Harbinger scholarly vocabulary and authorial allusion.

## 3.3 Thelema and Crowley

The Crowley association is acceptable and can become deliberate dramatic irony.

Hemomancy uses **Thelemic** primarily from the older lexical sense of *thelēma*: will, desire, intention moving toward action.

The current design inversion is:

### Thelemic Memory
Will descends into embodiment.

> *I will the act, and the blood-body has already begun.*

### Silent Archon
Will becomes increasingly abstract, sovereign, self-contained, and self-legislating.

> *My will requires no external sanction.*

Thus players familiar with modern occult Thelema may initially expect “Thelemic” to describe the Silent Archon more directly, while Hemomancy deliberately uses the word closer to its literal root for embodied volition.

That is not a contradiction that needs removal. It is a useful philosophical echo.

Potential Silent Archon doctrinal phrase:

> **Voluntas Sola** — *Will Alone.*

Use sparingly. It works best as a late doctrine, skill, chamber inscription, capstone, or scholarly label rather than another major player-facing subsystem.

# 4. The Hematic Mnemonic Model

## 4.1 Mneme — the substrate

**Mneme is not a third spell school.**

It is the condition that makes either Noetic or Thelemic expression possible.

A Hematic Memory is an encoded instruction or pattern carried through the practitioner’s blood-memory. At the deepest biological level, these patterns are fungal instructions made transmissible through the host.

To the Harbinger, they appear to be inherited or cultivated recollections.

To the player, the useful rule is:

> **The blood remembers before the practitioner understands.**

Mneme answers:

> **What persists?**

## 4.2 Nous — intellectual apprehension

Nous is the faculty by which a practitioner:

- recognizes a latent mnemonic pattern;
- interprets it;
- gives it conceptual form;
- relates it to known phenomena;
- calls it deliberately into expression.

A **Noetic Memory** is therefore not merely a “mental spell.”

It is a Hematic Memory whose usable expression depends upon **apprehension**.

The practitioner has learned enough to recognize what the blood is trying to do.

Noetic expression answers:

> **What does this pattern mean, and how may I deliberately call it forth?**

## 4.3 Thelēma — embodied volition

Thelēma is the faculty by which intention becomes action.

A **Thelemic Memory** is not simply muscular procedural memory. It may encompass:

- movement;
- posture;
- reflex;
- weapon handling;
- vascular rerouting;
- respiration;
- balance;
- physical timing;
- automatic bodily responses;
- other acts where the distinction between willing and doing becomes thin.

The body has been trained, altered, or mnemonicly conditioned until the practitioner does not need to intellectually formulate every step.

Thelemic expression answers:

> **What happens when the blood-body knows the act implied by my intention?**

Useful doctrinal contrast:

> **Noetic:** I conceive the act, therefore my blood performs it.  
> **Thelemic:** I will the act, and my blood has already begun.

# 5. Orthogonal Axes — Do Not Turn This Into Another Element System

This distinction must remain separate from the eight blood tendencies.

A memory can be described across several independent axes:

| Axis | Example values | What it describes |
|---|---|---|
| Mnemonic class | Noetic / Thelemic | **How** the memory becomes expression |
| Tendency | Animus, Ferric, Lux, etc. | **What recurring blood relationship** it expresses |
| Rank | Humilis, Mediocritas, Summa, Magister, Perfectus | Complexity / degree gate |
| Vein section | Head, arm, body, etc. | Anatomical strain site |
| Acquisition | Crude shard, Loom, experiment, study, Saint, graft, etc. | How the practitioner discovered or stabilized it |

Do not encode statements such as:

- “Ferric is Thelemic.”
- “Lux is Noetic.”
- “Thelemic memories are one tendency.”
- “Noetic is a ninth tendency.”

Those would collapse two useful systems into one.

A Ferric memory may be Noetic or Thelemic.  
An Animus memory may be Noetic or Thelemic.

The mnemonic class describes **mode of expression**, not metaphysical element.

---

# 6. Blood Tendencies as Human Taxonomy, Not Cosmic Elements

The eight tendencies should remain **surface-level correlations**.

Harbingers observe that certain Hematic Memories repeatedly correlate with certain natural processes:

| Tendency | Observed correlation family |
|---|---|
| **Animus / Vivacious** | growth, vitality, repair, living proliferation |
| **Flammeus / Fervent** | heat, combustion, fever, oxidation |
| **Ductilis / Neurotic** | nerve conduction, excitation, impulse, rapid response |
| **Lux / Incandescent** | illumination, exposure, revelation, visibility |
| **Mortem / Ruinous** | decay, cessation, decomposition, destructive metabolic endings |
| **Congeatio / Frigid** | cooling, stasis, preservation, arrested motion |
| **Ferric** | iron, mineral structure, rigidity, persistence, load-bearing form |
| **Tenebris / Umbral** | concealment, absence, obscurity, hidden routes |

The critical lore statement is:

> **These are useful maps, not proof that reality is secretly made from eight Hemomantic elements.**

Ferric is not literally “Matter.”  
Lux is not literally “Light as a cosmic substance.”  
Mortem is not literally metaphysical Death.

The practitioner is applying a human taxonomy to recurring behavior in something deeper and alien.

This preserves Hemomancy’s biological and epistemic identity instead of turning it into a conventional elemental magic system.

# 7. Noetic Memory Discovery

Noetic Memories are especially suited to **learning through understanding**.

This does **not** mean every Noetic Memory must come from the Mnemonist.

The same latent memory may be approached through:

- metaphysical meditation;
- philosophical analogy;
- textual scholarship;
- biological dissection;
- enzyme separation;
- experimental alchemy;
- field observation;
- creature ecology;
- Saint examination;
- material behavior;
- historical reconstruction;
- personal experience.

The essential requirement is not the *discipline* used.

The essential requirement is that the practitioner acquires enough conceptual purchase for **Nous to recognize the mnemonic pattern**.

## Important distinction: discovery versus stabilization

A discovery route need not immediately grant a usable memory.

A useful three-stage model is:

1. **Pattern encountered**  
   The player witnesses a reliable relationship.

2. **Pattern understood**  
   The player learns enough to identify a possible Hematic Memory.

3. **Pattern stabilized / remembered**  
   Existing acquisition mechanics—Somatic Loom, crude shards, rites, grafts, special events—make the mnemonic usable.

This prevents the lore from forcing a complete mechanical rewrite.

The Somatic Loom can remain the main deliberate stabilization mechanism while discovery becomes richer.

# 8. The Mnemonist and Alchemist — Same Blood, Different Epistemology

The most important NPC consequence is that the Mnemonist and Alchemist should overlap **without becoming redundant**.

They study the same underlying Hematic phenomenon through different epistemologies.

They should have **respectful methodological tension**, not distrust, hostility, or disapproval.

Neither school is secretly the correct one.

---

## 8.1 The Mnemonist — interpretive tradition

The Mnemonist studies blood-memory through:

- manuscripts;
- marginalia;
- diagrams;
- meditation;
- symbolic correspondences;
- comparative accounts;
- historical recurrences;
- introspection;
- Chamber experiences;
- inherited terminology;
- the structure of remembered thought.

The Mnemonist asks:

> **“What principle does the blood appear to remember?”**

Example:

The Mnemonist sees Lux memories repeatedly associated with exposure, illumination, revelation, and the uncovering of concealed things.

He studies the **pattern of meaning**.

His strengths are:

- finding structural relationships that experiments have not yet isolated;
- comparing centuries of practitioner reports;
- interpreting strange results;
- understanding memory as experience and form;
- guiding the player into the Chamber of Will;
- identifying when a phenomenon belongs to a broader mnemonic family.

His weakness is not foolish mysticism.

His weakness is that **symbolic systems can overfit evidence**.

---

## 8.2 The Alchemist — empirical tradition

The Alchemist studies blood-memory through:

- blood samples;
- enzymes;
- tissue;
- conductivity;
- temperature;
- mineral content;
- fungal cultures;
- decomposition;
- reaction conditions;
- controlled trials;
- comparative biology;
- repeatability.

The Alchemist asks:

> **“Under what conditions does the blood consistently behave this way?”**

Example:

He need not meditate upon the metaphysical meaning of Ductilis to discover that a particular enzyme expression correlates with:

- neural excitation;
- electrical discharge;
- muscular response;
- rapid signaling.

He studies the **pattern of reproducibility**.

His strengths are:

- rejecting elegant theories that fail under repeatable conditions;
- identifying biochemical or material triggers;
- finding relationships the traditional literature never noticed;
- producing reproducible recipes and apparatus.

His weakness is not soulless materialism.

His weakness is that **a reproducible relationship does not necessarily explain why the blood recognizes that relationship**.

# 9. Their Intellectual Tension

The relationship should feel like two scholars who have spent years proving each other both irritating and useful.

### Mnemonist criticism of the Alchemist

> “An experiment tells you that the blood answered. It does not tell you what question it heard.”

### Alchemist criticism of the Mnemonist

> “A symbol tells you what you expected to find. A reaction has the courtesy to disagree.”

### Vicar synthesis

> “The Mnemonist reads the blood as scripture. The Alchemist reads it as evidence. The blood has never shown particular concern for the distinction.”

This should be the tone:

- amused irritation;
- precise disagreement;
- professional respect;
- occasional borrowing of one another’s language;
- no suggestion that one is a fraud.

## Gameplay expression

One may discover something and refer the player to the other.

Examples:

- The **Alchemist** isolates a repeatable reaction but cannot explain why two unlike catalysts provoke the same mnemonic response. He sends the player to the Mnemonist.
- The **Mnemonist** notices two ancient memories share the same symbolic structure and asks the Alchemist to test whether they share an enzyme or physiological response.
- A memory may have an **empirical unlock route** and an **interpretive unlock route** leading to the same eventual mnemonic.
- Their dialogue may change after the player completes the other NPC’s assignment, acknowledging the result.

This is a better loop than “science versus magic.”

It is:

> **Observation -> Interpretation -> Experiment -> Reinterpretation**

# 10. Relationship to the Existing Mnemonist Progression

The current progression already gives the Mnemonist the right foundation:

- blood-memory teaching;
- crude memories;
- active manipulation practice;
- Mnemonic Reliquary;
- Degree 3 **The Woven Vessel**;
- deliberate Somatic Loom weaving;
- later Chamber of Will integration.

This taxonomy should **expand** that role rather than overwrite it.

The existing Chamber doctrine also supplies a natural escalation:

1. **A manipulation is a remembered instruction.**
2. **A scar is a remembered route.**
3. **A Chamber is remembered space.**

The new triad supplies an interpretive layer beneath that:

1. **Mneme preserves.**
2. **Nous apprehends.**
3. **Thelēma enacts.**

The Mnemonist can therefore evolve from “memory tutor” into a genuine **internal cartographer** without becoming the exclusive owner of every memory system.

---

# 11. Relationship to the Existing Alchemist Progression

The current Alchemist progression already emphasizes:

- Vial Centrifuge;
- Ghastly Alembic;
- enzyme separation;
- Red Taxonomy;
- Eightfold Centrifuge;
- specimen study;
- Living Bestiary;
- biological and material processing.

That makes the Alchemist a natural co-discoverer of Noetic principles.

The Alchemist does not need to become a second Mnemonist.

Instead:

- the Alchemist discovers **correlations through matter**;
- the Mnemonist discovers **correlations through meaning**;
- the Somatic Loom provides the place where understood relations may become deliberate Hematic Memory.

This also gives the existing D2 -> D3 handoff more intellectual weight.

The Alchemist is not “finished with the player and sending them to the spell NPC.”

He is effectively saying:

> *I have shown you what blood does. Now speak to someone who studies what it remembers.*

# 12. Silent Archon Consequence

The Silent Archon provides a deliberate inversion of ordinary Thelemic doctrine.

## Ordinary Thelemic memory

Will becomes embodied.

The practitioner becomes more efficient because intention and bodily action collapse toward one another.

> **Will perfects itself by becoming action.**

## Silent Archon

Will becomes sovereign through separation.

The Chamber’s monoliths, barren haze, sealed architecture, and refusal of fungal communion externalize a different doctrine:

> **Will perfects itself by requiring nothing outside itself.**

This is where **Voluntas Sola** may appear.

The useful irony is that the path visually and philosophically closest to a modern occult reading of sovereign Will is **not** the system named Thelemic Memories.

Thelemic Memory retains the older, more literal emphasis on wanting / willing / intending and making that intention bodily consequence.

Silent Archon turns will into law, architecture, boundary, and refusal.

Do not explain this irony directly in ordinary UI text. Let it be available to players who recognize it.

---

# 13. Fungal Truth Consequence

The taxonomy should eventually be undermined—but not rendered meaningless—by the fungal revelation.

The player may spend most of the game learning:

- tendencies;
- mnemonic classes;
- symbolic correlations;
- experimental correlations;
- ranks;
- bodily routes.

Then late-game lore reveals:

> the Harbingers were classifying fungal instructions using human intellectual categories.

This does **not** mean their categories were useless.

Human taxonomies can predict real behavior even when their metaphysical explanation is incomplete.

The Alchemist’s experiments still work.

The Mnemonist’s correspondences still predict mnemonic behavior.

The horror is that both traditions have become extremely sophisticated scholars of an inherited alien system without recognizing the full provenance of their subject.

This should reinforce, not invalidate, the Order’s intellectual tradition.

## 14.1 Preferred

| Current / conceptual term | Proposed player-facing term |
|---|---|
| Blood Manipulation | **Noetic Memory** where appropriate |
| Muscle Memory | **Thelemic Memory** |
| Generic learned blood power | **Hematic Memory** / **Mnemonic** |
| Memory item | **Hematic Memory** |
| Broad body of blood-memory practice | **Mnemonics** / **Hematic Mnemonics** |
| Deep philosophical “memory” faculty | **Mneme / Memoria** |
| Deep intellectual faculty | **Nous / Intelligentia** |
| Deep volitional faculty | **Thelēma / Voluntas** |

## 14.2 Avoid

- “Mental Memories” — redundant and bland.
- “Physical Memories” — serviceable but weak.
- “Muscle Memories” as formal UI vocabulary — too colloquial.
- “Noetic tendency” — Noetic is not a tendency.
- “Thelemic tendency” — same problem.
- “Thelema school” — risks making the term sound like a ninth elemental branch.
- calling Mneme a third power category unless a future mechanic specifically needs it.

# 15. Naming and Migration Strategy

Do **not** begin with destructive registry renames.

The mod already has a large persistence surface. A conceptual terminology pass should not needlessly create save migration work.

## Phase A — player-facing vocabulary only

Change:

- localization strings;
- tooltips;
- Liber language;
- dialogue;
- progress-screen labels;
- item inquiry prose;
- tutorial wording.

Keep internal registry IDs and Java class names where changing them provides no player benefit.

Examples:

```text
blood manipulation -> Noetic Memory
muscle memory -> Thelemic Memory
known manipulations -> Known Noetic Memories (UI only)
Manipulations tab -> Memories / Noetic Memories (after UI review)
```

Do not blindly replace every use of “manipulation.” In-world Harbingers may still use **manipulation** as a verb or older practical term.

## Phase B — add classification metadata

If mechanics need to distinguish the types, add a dedicated classification axis rather than inferring from names.

Possible form:

```java
enum HematicMemoryExpression {
    NOETIC,
    THELEMIC
}
```

or

```java
enum MnemonicFaculty {
    NOETIC,
    THELEMIC
}
```

Avoid adding `MNEME` as a normal expression unless a real mechanic exists for it.

## Phase C — mechanical differentiation

Only after terminology is stable should the systems diverge mechanically where useful.

Possible general distinction:

### Noetic
- deliberate selection;
- conscious activation;
- explicit blood cost / cooldown;
- manipulation radial;
- tendency and Loom-heavy acquisition.

### Thelemic
- embodied technique;
- movement/reflex/action trigger;
- physical practice;
- possible use-based mastery;
- less “cast a spell,” more “perform an act the altered body remembers.”

Do not force every Thelemic Memory into passive effects. “Embodied” and “passive” are not synonyms.

## Phase D — internal refactors only if valuable

Potential eventual refactors:

- `KnownManipulations` -> a broader known-mnemonics capability;
- manipulation registry abstraction;
- shared memory metadata;
- UI reorganization.

These are high-risk and should not be performed merely to make Java naming aesthetically perfect.

# 16. Proposed Gameplay Pathing Consequences

The taxonomy should create **more discovery routes**, not more currencies.

## 16.1 No new “Noetic XP” meter

Avoid.

Existing systems already provide:

- degree gates;
- tendency;
- recipes;
- Loom requirements;
- assignments;
- known manipulations;
- manipulation use-level;
- advancements.

Use those.

## 16.2 Discovery flags / milestones

Where a memory should be learned through scholarship or experiment, use lightweight state:

- advancement criterion;
- player persistent knowledge flag;
- recipe unlock;
- Liber knowledge;
- assignment milestone;
- item acquisition;
- existing mnemonic capability.

Examples:

```text
observed_ductilis_conduction
mnemonist_interpreted_ductilis_correspondence
alchemist_verified_ductilis_response
noetic_memory_<id>_recognized
```

These names are illustrative, not prescribed.

## 16.3 Multiple routes, one mnemonic

A powerful design pattern:

### Mnemonist route
Read / meditate / reconstruct / compare.

### Alchemist route
Test / separate / assay / observe.

### Common resolution
The same Hematic Memory becomes available for weaving or stabilization.

This reinforces epistemic plurality without duplicating content.

# 17. Dialogue Change Plan

The data-driven inquiry system makes the terminology pass suitable for staged dialogue work.

## 17.1 Mnemonist voice changes

Introduce:

- Mneme / Nous / Thelēma slowly;
- “Noetic” before “Thelemic” if Noetic memories are encountered first;
- study of tendency correspondences as interpretive maps;
- explicit acknowledgement that Alchemical evidence can reveal mnemonic truth.

Avoid:

- implying Alchemists merely handle ingredients while Mnemonists possess “real understanding”;
- presenting correspondences as absolute cosmic law;
- dumping the entire triad in the player’s first conversation.

## 17.2 Alchemist voice changes

Introduce:

- skepticism toward overly neat symbolic classification;
- respect for mnemonic predictions that survive experiments;
- tendency as repeatable physiological/material behavior;
- occasional referral to Mnemonist when reaction and explanation diverge.

Avoid:

- caricatured “I only believe what I can measure” materialism;
- pretending symbolic discovery is meaningless;
- turning the Alchemist into a modern laboratory scientist transplanted unchanged into the setting.

## 17.3 Cross-NPC acknowledgement

Add lines conditional on relevant assignments or discoveries.

Examples:

### Alchemist after Mnemonist result

> “He predicted that response from a diagram copied three centuries ago. Irritatingly, the vial agreed with him.”

### Mnemonist after Alchemist result

> “The Alchemist has found the reaction twice under controlled conditions. I am obliged, therefore, to take his ugly little table seriously.”

### Mnemonist on method

> “We disagree chiefly over which error is more dangerous: seeing meaning where there is only recurrence, or seeing recurrence and refusing to ask what it means.”

### Alchemist on method

> “If his correspondences predict the flask, I write them down. If they do not, I write that down too.”

The tone should suggest colleagues, not factions.

# 23. Design Risks

## Risk A — terminology overload

**Failure state:** The player must know the entire philosophical triad merely to cast powers.

**Guardrail:** Noetic/Thelemic/Hematic are sufficient for ordinary play.

---

## Risk B — generic elemental metaphysics

**Failure state:** tendencies become eight objectively fundamental cosmic substances.

**Guardrail:** tendencies are reliable Harbinger classifications of recurring mnemonic behavior.

---

## Risk C — Mnemonist becomes “correct” and Alchemist becomes shallow

**Failure state:** metaphysics is treated as truth while experimentation is merely crafting support.

**Guardrail:** both discover real patterns; both can be wrong about interpretation.

---

## Risk D — science-versus-religion cliché

**Failure state:** Alchemist rejects anything mystical and Mnemonist rejects experiment.

**Guardrail:** respectful methodological friction. Both are members of the same intellectual culture.

---

## Risk E — lore rewrite forces save-breaking code rename

**Failure state:** registry IDs and capability schemas are renamed to match new prose.

**Guardrail:** player-facing terminology first; internal refactors only when mechanically useful.

---

## Risk F — Thelemic means “passive”

**Failure state:** every embodied memory becomes a stat buff.

**Guardrail:** Thelemic describes the relationship between intention and bodily execution, not activation mode.

---

## Risk G — late fungal truth makes previous learning meaningless

**Failure state:** “the fungus did it” invalidates hundreds of years of Harbinger scholarship.

**Guardrail:** their models remain predictively useful. The revelation changes provenance, not every observation.

# 24. Canonical-Sounding Doctrinal Lines Worth Preserving

These emerged from the discussion and are useful candidates for future prose.

### Core triad

> **Mneme keeps. Nous knows. Thelēma acts.**

> **Memory is not power. Memory is preservation. Nous gives the recollection shape; Thelēma gives it consequence.**

### Noetic / Thelemic distinction

> **I conceive the act, therefore my blood performs it.**

> **I will the act, and my blood has already begun.**

### Mnemonist / Alchemist

> **An experiment tells you that the blood answered. It does not tell you what question it heard.**

> **A symbol tells you what you expected to find. A reaction has the courtesy to disagree.**

> **The Mnemonist reads the blood as scripture. The Alchemist reads it as evidence. The blood has never shown particular concern for the distinction.**

### Epistemic humility

> **A tendency is a name for a recurrence, not proof that the world was built to match our diagrams.**

### Silent Archon

> **Will perfects itself by becoming action.**

versus:

> **Will perfects itself by requiring nothing outside itself.**

> **Voluntas Sola.**

# 25. Existing Canon Anchors This Must Respect

The eventual integration should preserve these existing facts:

- Traditional blood powers are already framed as remembered instructions carried in blood.
- The Somatic Loom is the deliberate memory-weaving system.
- The Degree 3 Woven Vessel assignment already sends the player from Alchemical progression into formal Mnemonist memory work.
- The Alchemist currently owns machine processing, enzyme discovery, taxonomy, and biological experimentation.
- The Mnemonist currently owns blood-memory instruction, crude memories, loadout practice, and Loom interpretation.

- The eight tendencies are already canonical and should remain an independent axis.

# 26. Historical / Philosophical Reference Notes for Writers

These are **inspiration notes**, not statements that Hemomancy canonically descends from the historical schools.

### Augustine
`memoria — intelligentia — voluntas`

Useful for:
- the three-faculty structure;
- one mind expressed through interdependent memory, understanding, and will;
- Latin scholastic register.

### Classical / Late Antique *nous*
Useful for:
- intellect;
- apprehension;
- intelligible form;
- the distinction between merely sensing something and understanding what it is.

### Greek *thelēma*
Useful for:
- will;
- desire;
- wish;
- intention;
- volition becoming consequence.

### Modern Thelema
Useful as:
- a subtle echo;
- a deliberate later inversion through Silent Archon;
- **not** the direct doctrinal source of Thelemic Memories.

### Important historical-writing guardrail
Do not write:

> “The ancient triadic doctrine of Mneme, Nous and Thelema taught…”

unless Hemomancy itself invents such a Harbinger doctrine.

There is no need to falsely merge distinct historical vocabularies into a single ancient school.

Instead:

> Harbinger scholars developed a three-faculty mnemonic doctrine and expressed it using inherited Greek and Latin philosophical vocabulary.

That preserves the allusion without pseudo-history.

# 27. Final Design Position

The intended result is not a larger magic taxonomy.

It is a clearer explanation of a system Hemomancy already possesses.

**Hematic Memories** are the remembered substrate.

**Noetic Memories** are those the practitioner learns to apprehend and deliberately formulate.

**Thelemic Memories** are those in which willing and bodily action have been trained or altered toward unity.

**Blood tendencies** describe recurring correlations within either class.

**Alchemists** approach those correlations through reproducible matter.

**Mnemonists** approach them through interpretation, memory, analogy, and introspection.

Neither discipline owns the truth.

Both have spent centuries studying the same alien inheritance from opposite sides.

Late progression then changes the question from:

> *What does the blood remember?*

to:

> *Who taught it to remember that?*

And the Silent Archon ultimately asks something harsher still:

> *If the source of my memory is not mine, can my will nevertheless become entirely my own?*
