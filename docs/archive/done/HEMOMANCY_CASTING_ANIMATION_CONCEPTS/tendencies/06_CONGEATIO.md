# Congeatio Casting Animation and Particle Brief

Implemented coverage includes all five ranks, with separate first-person tracks and school-specific cancellation. These storyboards remain material and silhouette references; quick casts execute gameplay immediately and begin their visual release afterward. Rank does not imply charging or channeling. The runtime uses six rigid player parts and bounded anatomy meshes. See [implemented behavior and preview](../../../../wiki/Casting-Animations.md).

![Congeatio rank progression](../images/06_congeatio_rank_progression.png)

## Identity

Congeatio arrests blood in motion and preserves it as frozen anatomy. The effect is frozen blood with blue frost accents, not ordinary ice magic.

- **Dominant material:** Wine-red frozen blood with trapped droplets and vessels.
- **Secondary accent:** Pale-blue frost along edges and cracks.
- **Motion character:** Compress, slow, lock.
- **Silhouette rule:** Every moving body part stops on the same decisive frame.

## Base animation language

- Arms move slowly inward or across the torso.
- Legs widen into a planted stance.
- Head lowers or fixes upon the target.
- Release is communicated by simultaneous total-body immobility.
- Recovery begins with small fractures and a delayed return to motion.

## Rank progression

| Rank | Animation | Blood manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Humilis | One arm slows and locks | One blood ribbon freezes around or beyond the arm |
| Mediocritas | Both arms compress toward the torso | Paired streams harden into translucent red crystal |
| Summa | Full-body planted lock | Jagged frozen-blood shells and suspended droplets |
| Magister | Charged immobility; pose becomes progressively entombed | Vascular red-ice cage grows around the caster |
| Perfectus | Caster remains completely motionless | Enormous frozen anatomical heart and circulatory structure preserve the caster within |

## Particle expectations

- Liquid droplets visibly slow before crystallizing.
- Ice remains red at its core; blue exists chiefly on surfaces and fractures.
- Crystals should contain trapped beads, bubbles, or capillary lines.
- Shattering releases red shards and a small amount of thawed blood.
- Structures grow by freezing an existing blood stream rather than appearing from empty air.

## Cast-purpose variants

- **Surface freeze:** Blood traces the intended boundary, then freezes outward along it.
- **Area chill:** Suspended droplets halt around targets before crystallizing.
- **Wall or bastion:** A red blood sheet rises first and then hardens into frosted architecture.
- **Defensive delay:** A frozen vascular shell records incoming force before breaking or repaying it.

## Avoid

- Clear or purely blue ice
- Snowstorms without blood
- Generic icicle spawning
- Smooth motion through the release frame
- Crystals appearing without a preceding blood phase
