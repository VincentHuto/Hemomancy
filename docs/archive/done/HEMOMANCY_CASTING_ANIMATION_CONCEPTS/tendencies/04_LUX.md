# Lux Casting Animation and Particle Brief

Implemented coverage includes all five ranks, with separate first-person tracks and school-specific cancellation. These storyboards remain material and silhouette references; quick casts execute gameplay immediately and begin their visual release afterward. Rank does not imply charging or channeling. The runtime uses six rigid player parts and bounded anatomy meshes. See [implemented behavior and preview](../../../../wiki/Casting-Animations.md).

![Lux rank progression](../images/04_lux_rank_progression.png)

## Identity

Lux is blood made revelatory: illuminated vessels, exposed anatomy, opened eyes, and the removal of concealment. It should be luminous hemomancy rather than generic holy magic.

- **Dominant material:** Burgundy vessels, capillary membranes, and suspended blood droplets.
- **Secondary accent:** White to pale-gold internal illumination.
- **Motion character:** Symmetrical, upright, declarative.
- **Silhouette rule:** The caster opens the body to view rather than shielding it.

## Base animation language

- Both arms rise or part symmetrically.
- Torso straightens to a centered vertical axis.
- Head tilts upward or fixes directly upon the revealed subject.
- Legs form a balanced stance at Summa.
- Recovery closes the pose slowly, as though curtains are being drawn.

## Rank progression

| Rank | Animation | Blood manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Humilis | One arm raises while the head follows | Small blood ribbon illuminates from within |
| Mediocritas | Both arms part symmetrically | Twin capillary curtains and glowing droplets |
| Summa | Full-body formal stance | Eyes open in red vascular membranes; first anatomical transparency |
| Magister | Held orant pose | Illuminated blood veils expose skeleton and circulation |
| Perfectus | Perfectly still iconic pose | Vast capillary halo and many vessel-formed eyes reveal the caster and surroundings |

## Particle expectations

- White light remains enclosed within red droplets, vessels, or membranes.
- Eye forms are grown from capillary outlines and filled with luminous fluid.
- Rays should resolve into thin vascular lines when viewed closely.
- Revealed targets receive brief blood-red outlines with white internal pulses.
- Recovery dims from white to red before the blood falls or retracts.

## Cast-purpose variants

- **Light placement:** A suspended blood bead becomes luminous without losing its red shell.
- **Reveal:** A red capillary eye opens toward the target before applying Glowing or stripping concealment.
- **Healing:** Illuminated blood stitches a visible vascular path toward the wounded target.
- **Area beacon:** A capillary membrane rises around the point and pulses light through its vessels.

## Avoid

- Clean white spell geometry
- Angel wings
- Solar or divine iconography detached from anatomy
- Generic holy beams
- Eyes floating without vascular construction
