# Ferric Casting Animation and Particle Brief

Implemented coverage includes all five ranks, with separate first-person tracks and school-specific cancellation. These storyboards remain material and silhouette references; quick casts execute gameplay immediately and begin their visual release afterward. Rank does not imply charging or channeling. The runtime uses six rigid player parts and bounded anatomy meshes. See [implemented behavior and preview](../../../../wiki/Casting-Animations.md).

![Ferric rank progression](../images/07_ferric_rank_progression.png)

## Identity

Ferric concentrates the iron already carried in blood. Liquid blood coagulates, aligns, hardens, and becomes hematic metal. It is not generic earth or magnetism.

- **Dominant material:** Dark-red blood, rust-red clots, and metallic blood beads.
- **Secondary accent:** Gunmetal-black hardened surfaces and sparse gray filings.
- **Motion character:** Plant, brace, hammer, lock.
- **Silhouette rule:** The caster appears heavy before any metal is visible.

## Base animation language

- Legs plant apart and torso lowers.
- One arm forms a rigid brace.
- The opposite arm drives downward or across it like a hammer.
- Every movement ends with a hard mechanical stop.
- Recovery is slow, as if the body must shed accumulated mass.

## Rank progression

| Rank | Animation | Blood manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Humilis | One heavy arm strike | Blood globule compresses into a dense metallic clot |
| Mediocritas | Two-arm brace-and-hammer action | Blood streams align into rust-red beads and bars |
| Summa | Full planted stance | Hardened vascular ridges plate portions of the body |
| Magister | Charged pose strains beneath increasing weight | Ordered rings of dripping blood beads and iron clots orbit the caster |
| Perfectus | Caster becomes still like a monument | Vast circulatory foundry of hardened vessels and metallic clots aligns around the body |

## Particle expectations

- Liquid blood should visibly precede metal formation.
- Beads become less fluid and more regularly aligned as concentration increases.
- Hardened particles retain red seams, wet edges, or dripping cores.
- Motion accelerates along magnetic lines but ends in heavy impacts.
- Repair and mining effects can send iron-rich blood into cracks before hardening.

## Cast-purpose variants

- **Repair:** Blood fills fractures and solidifies into dark metallic seams.
- **Mining:** Iron-rich droplets map the target before a hammer-like pulse breaks connected material.
- **Self-buff:** Blood circulates around the body, then locks into temporary vascular plating.
- **Travel or network use:** Blood enters an Earthen Vein node and hardens into a momentary connection line.

## Avoid

- Floating stone
- Clean metal rings without blood
- Generic telekinesis
- Instant armor appearing from nowhere
- Lightweight or graceful movement
