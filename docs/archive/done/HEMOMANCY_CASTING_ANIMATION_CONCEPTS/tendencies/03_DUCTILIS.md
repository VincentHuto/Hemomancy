# Ductilis Casting Animation and Particle Brief

Implemented coverage includes all five ranks, with separate first-person tracks and school-specific cancellation. These storyboards remain material and silhouette references; quick casts execute gameplay immediately and begin their visual release afterward. Rank does not imply charging or channeling. The runtime uses six rigid player parts and bounded anatomy meshes. See [implemented behavior and preview](../../../../wiki/Casting-Animations.md).

![Ductilis rank progression](../images/03_ductilis_rank_progression.png)

## Identity

Ductilis expresses blood as a carrier for neural impulses. Yellow conduction travels through red, blood-filled nerve structures; it is not free lightning or storm magic.

- **Dominant material:** Thin branching red vessels and blood threads.
- **Secondary accent:** Brief neurotic-yellow impulses.
- **Motion character:** Staccato, discontinuous, reflexive.
- **Silhouette rule:** Head, torso, and arms snap into opposed angles with visible pauses between impulses.

## Base animation language

- Arms jump between two or three rigid poses rather than moving smoothly.
- Head and torso counter-rotate.
- The final discharge occurs after the body visually completes a circuit.
- Legs join at Summa through an asymmetric, electrically arrested stance.
- Charged casting becomes increasingly involuntary without adding forbidden joints.

## Rank progression

| Rank | Animation | Blood manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Humilis | One arm and head snap once | Single blood filament with one yellow impulse |
| Mediocritas | Both arms alternate through opposed poses | Branching blood nerves connect both sides of the torso |
| Summa | Full body forms a closed circuit before discharge | Vascular-neural overlay with sparse conduction flashes |
| Magister | Repeating twitch loop with short held pauses | Dense nerve net accumulates around the caster; impulses accelerate with charge |
| Perfectus | Central caster becomes rigid between instantaneous pose changes | Monumental blood-filled nervous system conducts pulses through the surrounding space |

## Particle expectations

- Yellow light should travel inside a red filament rather than arc freely through air.
- Impulses move in discrete packets from node to node.
- Blood beads can function as temporary synapses.
- Afterimages may show earlier rigid poses, but must remain translucent blood silhouettes.
- Release produces one sharp flash followed by residual red filaments.

## Cast-purpose variants

- **Area pulse:** A vascular circuit closes around nearby targets, then fires impulses simultaneously.
- **Detection:** Blood filaments emit a pulse and return with highlighted life-sign information.
- **Continuous ward:** A stable red neural mesh periodically flashes yellow when struck.
- **Projectile:** A blood thread establishes the route before a conduction packet races along it.

## Avoid

- Lightning bolts without blood carriers
- Storm clouds
- Continuous yellow aura
- Smooth dance-like motion
- Physical extra arms instead of translucent pose echoes
