# Flammeus Casting Animation and Particle Brief

Implemented coverage includes all five ranks, with separate first-person tracks and school-specific cancellation. These storyboards remain material and silhouette references; quick casts execute gameplay immediately and begin their visual release afterward. Rank does not imply charging or channeling. The runtime uses six rigid player parts and bounded anatomy meshes. See [implemented behavior and preview](../../../../wiki/Casting-Animations.md).

![Flammeus rank progression](../images/02_flammeus_rank_progression.png)

## Identity

Flammeus is blood driven into violent metabolism until it ignites. The player manipulates combustible blood, not free-standing fire.

- **Dominant material:** Dark-red liquid blood under pressure.
- **Secondary accent:** Pinkish-red flame with black tips.
- **Motion character:** Recoil, compress, expel.
- **Silhouette rule:** The body coils away from the release and then drives into it.

## Base animation language

- One or both arms recoil behind or beside the torso.
- The torso twists or pitches into the release.
- The head recoils from the resulting heat and pressure.
- Area casts drive both arms apart as though rupturing a pressurized vessel.
- Recovery is abrupt and slightly unstable.

## Rank progression

| Rank | Animation | Blood manifestation |
|---|---|---|
| Humilis | One-arm recoil and sharp thrust | Small burning blood globule or short jet |
| Mediocritas | Two-arm pressure release with torso drive | Paired blood jets igniting after leaving the body |
| Summa | Wide stance and full-body explosive release | Burning vascular cords and boiling droplets |
| Magister | Braced charge with visible pressure building in repeated loops | Pressurized blood sphere or arterial furnace grows around the caster |
| Perfectus | Caster holds a severe, nearly still pose | Vast circulatory furnace whose blood-filled vessels burn only along their surfaces |

## Particle expectations

- Every flame must cling to, trail from, or erupt out of visible blood.
- Blood remains readable beneath combustion.
- Flame tips use the established black-tipped motif.
- Droplets may smoke, boil, swell, and rupture before ignition.
- Soot is secondary and should not obscure the blood stream.

## Cast-purpose variants

- **Projectile:** A pressurized blood globule ignites during flight.
- **Area pulse:** Blood beads erupt outward first; their outer surfaces catch fire a fraction later.
- **Self-cleanse:** Burning blood races outward from the torso and carries corruption off as blackened clots.
- **Utility heat:** A tight blood circuit heats the affected object without producing a broad flame aura.

## Avoid

- Ordinary fireballs
- Pure flame columns
- Orange replacing blood-red
- Generic flaming hands
- Fire appearing before the blood carrier exists
