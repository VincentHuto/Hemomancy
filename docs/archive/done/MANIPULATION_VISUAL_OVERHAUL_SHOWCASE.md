# The Eight Tendencies, In Game

The manipulation overhaul gives each blood tendency its own material language instead of relying on the same particles for every cast. These are real Minecraft client captures from the disposable visual-review worlds used while testing the overhaul. They are representative examples rather than a complete catalogue of every manipulation.

| Tendency | Example | Visual identity |
|---|---|---|
| Animus | Blood Binding | Living crimson streams, elastic tethers and moving blood |
| Mortem | Bloom of Rot | Dried oxblood, crusted ground stains and pale infection seams |
| Ductilis | Synaptic Storm | Fine nerve-like filaments, bright impulses and conductive links |
| Ferric | Iron Choir | Rigid dark-metal lamellae split by red stress seams |
| Flammeus | Vitric Combustion | Hot sanguine glass, red-orange fragments and smoke |
| Congeatio | Absolute Stillness | Frozen cruor, fractured frost and pale exact boundaries |
| Lux | White Verdict | Unlit white cores, warm fringes and narrow flowing ribbons |
| Tenebris | Eclipse Well | Shallow liquid darkness, charcoal currents and eroded smoke |

## Animus — blood in motion

Animus effects look alive. Blood Binding sends joined crimson streams from the caster to the target, coils around the body and retracts when the hold ends. The same living-blood material appears across projectiles, clouds, command effects and the Avatar family, but their silhouettes and movement remain distinct.

![Blood Binding joining the caster to its target](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/showcase/animus-blood-binding.png)

## Mortem — blood left to decay

Mortem trades Animus's clean flow for dark residue, broken wisps and infected surfaces. Bloom of Rot briefly stains the ground around its victims and lays branching decay across affected bodies, making the danger readable without turning it into permanent terrain.

![Bloom of Rot spreading across the ground and affected targets](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/showcase/mortem-bloom-of-rot.png)

[Watch the Mortem daylight cast reel](../build/animus-mortem-review/mortem-day.mp4)

## Ductilis — nervous conduction

Ductilis uses fast, fine lines rather than heavy spell bodies. Synaptic Storm branches bright impulses between nearby targets, while Conductive Mark, Living Circuit and Thread Ripper reuse the same nervous material in seals, moving beads and taut bundles.

![Synaptic Storm branching through a group of targets](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/showcase/ductilis-synaptic-storm.png)

[Watch the Ductilis nervous-electricity reel](../build/ferric-ductilis-review/nerves-after.mp4)

## Ferric — blood made rigid

Ferric magic forms weighty, defensive shapes. Iron Choir surrounds the caster with overlapping blood-iron plates; damage and paid pulses change the number of plates, so the visual remains tied to the interception budget instead of acting as decoration alone.

![Iron Choir's blood-iron plates around the caster](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/showcase/ferric-iron-choir.png)

[Watch the Ferric defenses and utility reel](../build/ferric-ductilis-review/defenses-after.mp4) · [Compare the old and new iron formations](../build/ferric-ductilis-review/iron-comparison.mp4)

## Flammeus — heat, flame and sanguine glass

Flammeus uses animated red-orange flame, rising smoke and boiling blood. Vitric Combustion gathers thin, uneven plates of hot sanguine glass before ejecting them, giving the charge and release a recognizable silhouette of their own.

![Vitric Combustion releasing sanguine glass plates](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/showcase/flammeus-vitric-combustion.png)

[Watch the matched Flammeus and Congeatio before/after reel](../build/thermal-review/flammeus-congeatio-before-after.mp4)

## Congeatio — frozen cruor and fracture

Congeatio places opaque wine-red cruor under pale frost and translucent cracked edges. Absolute Stillness holds that material nearly motionless inside its exact boundary, while other cold powers fracture, shed powder or track frost across affected bodies.

![Absolute Stillness holding frozen cruor inside its boundary](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/showcase/congeatio-absolute-stillness.png)

[Watch the matched Flammeus and Congeatio before/after reel](../build/thermal-review/flammeus-congeatio-before-after.mp4)

## Lux — concentrated living light

Lux is built from bright white cores, warm translucent fringes and fine streaming ribbons. White Verdict now reads from first person as a full released beam rather than a flat line, and its two-second afterglow remains at the firing position as it fades.

![White Verdict after release in first person](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/lux-umbra/verdict-fired-first-person.png)

[Watch White Verdict's charge and ribbon comparison](../build/lux-umbra-review/ribbons-and-verdict-before-after.webm) · [Watch its final afterglow](../build/lux-umbra-review/white-verdict-afterglow.webm)

## Tenebris — liquid darkness

Tenebris forms shallow near-black bodies with charcoal flow and eroded smoke. Eclipse Well spreads as a low pool rather than a solid object; the separate square outline preserves the power's exact gameplay radius even while the liquid interior moves.

![Eclipse Well with Minimal particles enabled](manipulation_work_plan/evidence/lux-umbra/well-minimal.png)

## About the captures

The review fixtures cast registered manipulations or emit their production visual packets inside disposable `VisceralReview` saves. The reels preserve formation, active motion and retirement, which a still image cannot show. Some scenes intentionally include armor stands, stone and glass occluders, Minimal-particle settings, first-person views or the red low-health vignette because those were part of the visibility and depth tests.

The linked recordings live under `build/` and are local test artifacts, so they are available in this checkout but are not release media. The source captures and reproduction notes are documented in [the visceral material review](manipulation_work_plan/07-visceral-visuals.md), [Lux and Tenebris flow](manipulation_work_plan/08-lux-umbra-flow.md), [Flammeus and Congeatio materials](manipulation_work_plan/09-flammeus-congeatio-visuals.md), and [Animus and Mortem presentation](manipulation_work_plan/10-animus-mortem-visuals.md).
