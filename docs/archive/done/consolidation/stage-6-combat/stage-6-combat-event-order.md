# Stage 6: combat and attack-event order

This is a source audit of the 2026-09-25 gameplay checkout. It follows a hit from attack intent to damage commitment and death, with representative player powers. It does not claim that a world/client run has confirmed the outcomes below. [Stage 1](../stage-1-capabilities/README.md) inventories the wider capability event surface; this sheet identifies the combat contracts a cleanup must preserve.

## One hit has several meanings

| Boundary | Active owners and source behavior | Contract for consolidation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attack intent | [SchoolCombatEvents.attack](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/damage/SchoolCombatEvents.java) captures the swing's school identity and clears Veiled. [MuscleMemoryEvents.rememberPrimaryAttack](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/capability/player/harbinger/musclememory/MuscleMemoryEvents.java) records player UUID, target ID, and tick for Sanguine Fists. [ManipulationReactiveEvents.onAttack](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/manipulation/ManipulationReactiveEvents.java) stops selected Penumbral Drift; [SheolicBastionBloodlustArmorAbilityHandler](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/armor/ability/SheolicBastionBloodlustArmorAbilityHandler.java) can set the target burning. [ChamberVisitEvents](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/event/ChamberVisitEvents.java) can cancel a dream attack. | `AttackEntityEvent` is an attempt, not a landed hit. Keep intent effects, direct-hit evidence, and canceled-attack behavior distinct. These subscribers use default priority, so their relative order is not an explicit contract in the source. |
| Damage-source attribution | [MixinSchoolDamage](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/mixin/core/MixinSchoolDamage.java) calls [SchoolDamage.wrap](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/damage/SchoolDamage.java) at `LivingEntity.hurt` entry. [SchoolHitContext](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/damage/SchoolHitContext.java) carries ability, school, owner/direct UUIDs, root attack, direct/periodic/reaction kind, role, charge, and application data. | Preserve the native damage type plus authored school metadata. | Incoming, before reductions | [SchoolCombatEvents.prepare](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/damage/SchoolCombatEvents.java) runs at `HIGH`, rejects protected school targets/repeated reactions, applies affinity and state bonuses, then stores a prepared hit by `DamageSource` and target UUID. [ManipulationReactiveEvents.onIncomingDamage](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/manipulation/ManipulationReactiveEvents.java) uses `LOW` for Sanguine Ward's pool. [BodyIdiomEvents](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/manipulation/BodyIdiomEvents.java) uses `LOWEST` for incoming damage conversion. [ChamberVisitEvents](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/event/ChamberVisitEvents.java) can cancel protected-player damage. | A prepared school hit is pending. Incoming amount, ward absorption, cancellation, and final damage are different values and outcomes. Do not apply school state or landed-hit rewards at preparation. |
| `LivingDamageEvent.Pre` | `HIGH` [MuscleMemoryEvents.applySecondPulse](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/capability/player/harbinger/musclememory/MuscleMemoryEvents.java) reduces qualifying damage. At default priority, [BloodVolumeEvents](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/capability/player/harbinger/bloodvolume/BloodVolumeEvents.java) applies Iron Will, drains blood, records damage, and can spawn a formation; [VascularSystemEvents](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/capability/player/harbinger/vascular/VascularSystemEvents.java) derives strain from its observed `getNewDamage()`. Blood and vascular costs are calculated at their handler's observation point; other default-priority handlers and later `LOWEST` changes can alter the eventual amount. A shared “final damage” value would change these costs unless the desired timing is decided explicitly. |
| `LivingDamageEvent.Post` | `HIGH` [SchoolCombatEvents.commit](../../../src/main/java/com/vincenthuto/hemomancy/common/damage/SchoolCombatEvents.java) removes the prepared hit and requires positive health damage **plus absorption reduction**. Same-priority reward and cancellation ordering is not declared here; do not centralize kill credit until canceled-death behavior is observed and specified. |

## Representative paths to preserve

| Path | Source trace | Review question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct player melee | Attack intent captures a school swing and primary target. The `hurt` mixin wraps the source if the swing or held tendency weapon supplies a context. Incoming prepares the school hit. Pre handlers may change damage and player capability costs. School Post commits state/reaction; Sanguine Fists requires the primary target record plus a direct source. | Does a canceled attack leave any intent effect (Veiled loss, burning, channel stop), and is that intended? Check a missed/blocked hit separately from a landed hit. |
| Projectile/cast | `SchoolDamage.captureProjectile` stores context on launch, with the actual projectile as direct entity; `wrap` or `projectileSource` retains it on impact. SchoolHitContext distinguishes owner and direct entity. | Check owner credit, PvP/allied rejection, and school identity after the caster switches weapons or the projectile crosses a save/reload boundary. Do not infer “direct melee” from owner alone. |
| Absorbed or reduced hit | School Post accepts `newDamage + ABSORPTION reduction > 0`; Bloodlust lifesteal and Reprisal use health damage. Incoming Ward and several Pre powers can reduce or zero damage after earlier hooks have inspected it. | Decide whether a blood drain, vascular strain, formation roll, or defensive trigger should occur when later protection removes all health damage. This is a behavior decision before moving those hooks. |
| Reaction/periodic hit | School context marks children `REACTION` or `PERIODIC`. Incoming rejects a reaction whose root already paid on that target; Post marks payoffs and avoids reapplying a direct state. School tick and effect-expiry handlers originate further child hits. | Preserve root-attack identity and the one-payoff guard through nested `hurt` calls. A generic damage helper that strips the context can cause a repeated payoff or missing school state. |
| Player rescue and kill reward | Pre damage can be reduced to zero or leave one health through armor and Phoenix Debt. Separately, Curved Horn and Chamber Visit can cancel `LivingDeathEvent`; several reward handlers listen at default priority. | Run lethal-hit cases for each rescue with a player attacker and inspect reward counters/blood/purity, saved player state, and whether low-priority cleanup runs. Source alone does not establish the final canceled-event dispatch behavior. |

## Where consolidation is small enough to review

1. Write a compact combat-event contract for each subscriber: phase and priority, actor (owner/direct/victim), hit kind, amount read, cancellation rule, state mutation, and visual/sync action. Use this table to settle the absorbed-hit and canceled-death policies with actual gameplay evidence before changing event registration.
2. If repeated predicates remain identical after that decision, share named helpers for **health damage**, **health plus absorption**, and **primary direct melee**. Keep each caller's exact condition visible. `SchoolHitContext` and `SchoolDamage` already own school provenance; avoid a second attack-context system.
3. Keep `SchoolCombatEvents.prepare` and `commit` paired. Its `PENDING` entry is removed in Post, while a later canceled incoming hit may have no Post; inspect cancellation and repeated `DamageSource` reuse before changing the pending lifetime. The weak-keyed map does not by itself specify gameplay cleanup timing.
4. Treat death reward dispatch as a separate migration from damage modifiers. Preserve bloodless-victim checks, hostile-mob skill milestones, Purity tiers, scar callbacks, and rescue semantics. Do not replace these with one undifferentiated “on kill” callback.

Focused gameplay acceptance for a future code change: one direct melee hit, a projectile after weapon swap, an absorption-only hit, a Sanguine Ward or zeroed Pre hit, a reaction child hit, and each rescued lethal hit with another player as attacker. Observe capability costs, rewards, school state, visual cues, and client sync. No such gameplay run or code extraction was performed in this Stage 6 audit.

## Stage 6 implementation and observed order (2026-09-25)

The dedicated `runCombatOrderGameTestServer` run exercised six server GameTests. The source and run establish this order for the touched paths:

| Phase | Priority and cancellation | Amount or identity kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AttackEntityEvent` | School swing capture and Muscle Memory primary-target capture remain separate default-priority subscribers. Their order relative to other default-priority cancellation is unspecified. | Intent alone did not spend Living Circuit in the direct-melee test. Muscle Memory still requires its recorded primary target, direct player source, and the original tick window in Pre. |
| `LivingEntity.hurt` entry | `MixinSchoolDamage` wraps the native source before incoming processing. | Projectile launch context, owner, direct projectile, root attack, and native damage type survive a weapon swap. |
| `LivingIncomingDamageEvent` | `SchoolCombatEvents.prepare` is HIGH; Ward is LOW; Body Idiom is LOWEST. A new LOWEST listener with `receiveCanceled=true` discards only a canceled target's pending entry. NeoForge returns from `hurt` when incoming is canceled, so Post does not run. | Preparation uses incoming amount and captures pre-hit states. The cancel/reuse test found no pending entry after LOW cancellation and committed a fresh hit from the same `DamageSource`. |
| `LivingDamageEvent.Pre` | Second Pulse is HIGH; Blood Volume and Vascular are default; Phoenix Debt, deferred damage, and Sanguine Fists are LOWEST. | Blood Volume and Vascular continue to use their own mutable `getNewDamage()` at their call sites. Zeroing Pre damage prevented school state in the focused run. |
| `LivingDamageEvent.Post` | School commit is HIGH; armor, morphling, Coronation, Circuit, Retort, and Reprisal retain their existing priorities. | Shared predicates distinguish positive health damage from positive health plus absorption reduction. Each payoff still reads its previous amount and school kind. Absorption-only damage applied school state; a reaction child recorded its root payoff without applying direct state. |
| `LivingDeathEvent` | Player kill rewards and Curved Horn remain default priority; school cleanup remains LOWEST. Same-priority order is not established. | A controlled LOW-priority rescue after `die` restored the victim and still granted 25 blood to the attacker (500 to 525). This does not settle Curved Horn or Chamber Visit ordering. No death handler was moved. |

The focused run used a dedicated template namespace and covered server mechanics, not live client visuals or multiplayer. A current-tick invulnerability early return after incoming preparation can also leave a weak-keyed pending entry without Post; reuse at HIGH preparation overwrites that target's entry. The LOWEST cancellation cleanup only observes cancellations made before it runs, so cancellation by another LOWEST subscriber registered later remains a possible ordering edge. Neither case showed a stale payoff in these tests; further lifecycle changes require a concrete failing path.
