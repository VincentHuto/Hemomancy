## Implementation and limits

- M26: charge forms cover every active charged power. White Verdict alone projects its exact clipped beam preview; the other charge forms are hand/body buildup, not a claim of a valid target. Release effects use successful action positions. Teleport cues show accepted endpoints after release; they do not add a pre-cast landing ghost.
- M27: Well/Beacon have square footprints matching their AABB fields; cloud, pillar, ice and channel cues follow real world state and expiry. Some self-centered outlines approximate the envelope of differently sized mob/projectile hit boxes; they are not pixel-exact collision overlays.
- M28: remaining crown swords, Ward pool, Choir interceptions, owner connections, status effects and debts now have world cues. Existing heart HUD remains exact. Short readiness refreshes may linger up to 25 ticks after an eligibility change; these are cues, not authorization to trigger.
- M29: projectile bodies and charge silhouettes distinguish family roles. Blood Shot previously had an empty entity renderer; its new body is now independent of particle settings. Growth, repair and forge show successful results.
- M30: existing Avatar and Living Staff authored models/transitions retained. Added receiver-directed absorption/projection success streams through shared action paths. Full first-person/remote form acceptance remains open.
- M31: added missing Shot, Needle and Avatar family sound mappings; Mortar has a heavier release pitch. Continuous startup remains separate; fixed the sustained passive payment path so Avatar upkeep no longer repeats the summon sound. Avatar toggle emits activation once. No new sound assets; listening for mix/double playback remains an acceptance task.

## Effect lifecycle and cost

`ManipulationVisuals` sends server-authored shape, coordinates/entity ID, size, count and lifetime. `ManipulationVisualRenderer` builds faceted swords, glass, wings, bells, stitches, plates and motion ribbons. A client retains at most 192 cues, culls past 128 blocks, bounds packet values/lifetimes, and clears state on world replacement/disconnect. Entity-bound cues follow interpolated entities and skip dead/missing owners. Timed status glyphs check the corresponding effect where available. Charge previews are throttled to four ticks and expire after ten ticks without refresh; they do not apply damage, spend blood or grant a cast.

Visible swords and fired swords share one mesh and orbit math. Fired sword identity and Shot family identity synchronize through entity data and persist in saves. Cloud/pillar lifetime removal is server-owned. The world overlay renders after level composition with an explicit camera matrix; this avoids cloud bands cutting through transparent meshes. Ordinary geometry remains depth-tested; only caster-private ore markers intentionally bypass depth.
