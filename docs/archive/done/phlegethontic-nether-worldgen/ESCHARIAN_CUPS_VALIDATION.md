# Escharian cup geometry validation

Validated on 2026-09-14 in the existing checkout. The change replaces merged solid lobes and cluster-wide perimeter colouring with individually rimmed, flared cups. Their dark interiors step downward, their undersides narrow, and their bases connect without roofing over neighbouring mouths. Terrain-aware retries fit complete cups beside uneven banks; final preflight rejects obstructed mouths or partial cups. Existing block IDs, textures, collision, rarity, habitat recognition, and configured budgets remain in place. Existing chunks are not rebuilt.

## Surface contours and variation follow-up

Cups now sample the natural backing separately for each surface column, within four blocks inward or outward of the anchor plane. Their floors and lips inherit that relief. Floor roots extend through available space toward the stone, stopping at existing decorations; the shell and mouth still have to fit intact. This allows short overhangs around hanging scab without replacing it. Colony footprints spread along both surface axes, including vertically on walls. The six-block local depth cap no longer squeezes wall colonies into a six-block-high strip. Primary and secondary radii, skew, rim outlines, offsets, and depths vary across the colony. The selected habitat gets up to 32 candidate locations before the remaining wall and ceiling candidates are interleaved.
