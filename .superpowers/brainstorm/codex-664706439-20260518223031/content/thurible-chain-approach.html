Sporitic Thurible: Chain Rendering Direction

Pick the feel we should design around first. The thurible silhouette stays liturgical in all three: vented bowl, domed lid, ring, and hanging chain.

A. Procedural Client Physics

Recommended: client simulates a spring pendulum from the player hand, draws chain links along the curve, and renders the thurible body at the bob.

B. Held Visual Entity

Most physical and inspectable, but heavier: a client-visible entity tracks the hand and may need extra sync rules to stay correct for other players.

C. Authored Animated Model

Safest visually, least experimental: model bones swing with movement/swing progress, but the chain is animation-driven rather than physics-driven.

My bias

Start with A. It hits the strange “real object dangling from my hand” goal while staying client-side and avoiding a new gameplay entity unless the prototype proves it needs one.
