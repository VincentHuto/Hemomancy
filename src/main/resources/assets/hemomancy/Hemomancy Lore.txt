Hemomancy   




Hemomancy is a blood magic mod based around the quality of blood manipulation vs just a large quantity of blood, it covers topics of gore, magic, pretty exaggerated biology and much much more.
Getting Started
The guide book below is the source of truth for everything in the mod using my homebrewed guidebook system all my mods use, while not complete because well…im lazy, it is a good start
  
the power to control blood is actually the result of a special fungal infection, said fungus came from outer space as part of a chunk of a larger ball of hive mind fungus(which itself is actually the manifestation of an outer god type thing) that deliberately broke off and landed on earth/Minecraft and is slowly taking hold

One of the main groups who don't shun the existence of it and instead indulge are called "The Harbingers" and they cultivate and innoculate themselves and their surroundings to spread it 
The green goo jar is called a morphling jar and contains special strains of the fungus that the user can use for special effects temporarily, and the flesh heart acts as a source of nutrition for the fungus on the book

another group that acts as the resistance are "The Unstained" a group of antiblood zealots whose role is to purge the influence of the fungus from the world wherever it spreads, focusing on using liquid weapons that spew Hemolytic enzymes and antifungals as well as blessed copper weapons to combat the spread

Blood manipulations
    Blood manipulations(manips for short) are sort of the bread and butter of the mod, lore wise they are memories that everyone has access to but just needs a way to remember them,
        these are in the form of "Hematic memories" which when used allows you access to the base level of each manip

Radial Selection menu  






        Manipulations have different levels of power which are increased based on use as well as a ritual type system you can perform on yourself to force a higher strength(WIP)
Visceral Recaller
        The way these memories are accessed is through a station called the “Visceral Recaller”.
The Recaller acts as a form of artificial brain, since probing your own while your still using it isn't the best idea..it uses a combination of your own blood, a blank memory and a certain combinations of items called enzymes which act as ways of forcing the memory back to a state where it can be recalled and therefore used.


        The current way that it displays it is using this radial display that floats above the block and changes the sharpness of each section based on how many of the corresponding enzymes have been used and the corresponding “Catalyst” item above the blank memory

The Infection
        A fungal spread scattered around the world known colloquially as Erythromycelium, while not necessarily malicious, it can be dangerous if not managed or worked with when encountered
  



Morphlings
One of the beneficial uses for the infection comes in the forms of small symbiotic parasites which can take on a variety of forms are called morphlings, which using a base polyp and some enzymes can be nursed into usable forms
  



These can be used with the living staff to allow it to perform a variety of attacks and actions, like summoning an army of leeches to fight for you and gain some blood, a chitin shield, or a paralyzing serpent that can hold enemies in place.
